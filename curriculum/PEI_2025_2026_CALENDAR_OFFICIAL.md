# PEI 2025-2026 Official School Calendar

## 📅 Key Statistics
- **Total School Days**: 195
- **Total Instructional Days**: 181
- **First Day of School**: Thursday, September 4, 2025
- **Last Day of Classes (K-9)**: Thursday, June 25, 2026
- **Last Day for Staff**: Tuesday, June 30, 2026

## 📊 Monthly Breakdown

### SEPTEMBER 2025 (20 School Days, 18 Instructional)
- **Sept 1**: Labour Day (no classes)
- **Sept 2**: Administrative Day (no classes)
- **Sept 3**: Teacher Orientation Day (no classes)
- **Sept 4**: ✅ **FIRST DAY OF SCHOOL FOR STUDENTS**
- **Sept 30**: National Day for Truth and Reconciliation (no classes)

### OCTOBER 2025 (22 School Days, 21 Instructional)
- **Oct 10**: Professional Learning Day - Provincial PD (no classes)
- **Oct 13**: Thanksgiving Day (no classes)

### NOVEMBER 2025 (19 School Days, 15 Instructional)
- **Nov 6-7**: PEITF Convention / CUPE Annual Convention (no classes)
- **Nov 10**: Report Card Day - Teacher Administrative Day (no classes)
- **Nov 11**: Remembrance Day (no classes)
- **Nov 21**: Parent-Teacher Interviews K-12 (no classes)

### DECEMBER 2025 (16 School Days, 15 Instructional)
- **Dec 19**: ✅ **LAST INSTRUCTIONAL DAY 2025**
- **Dec 22**: Professional Learning Day - Joint Staff PD (no classes)
- **Dec 23-Jan 4**: Winter Break (implied)

### JANUARY 2026 (20 School Days, 20 Instructional)
- **Jan 1**: New Year's Day (no classes)
- **Jan 5**: ✅ **FIRST INSTRUCTIONAL DAY 2026**

### FEBRUARY 2026 (19 School Days, 18 Instructional)
- **Feb 2**: Administrative Day - High School ONLY (no classes HS)
- **Feb 13**: Professional Learning Day - School Goals (no classes)
- **Feb 16**: Islander Day (no classes)

### MARCH 2026 (17 School Days, 17 Instructional)
- **Mar 6**: Parent-Teacher Interviews K-9 / PD Day 10-12 (no classes)
- **Mar 16-20**: ✅ **MARCH BREAK** (no classes)

### APRIL 2026 (20 School Days, 19 Instructional)
- **Apr 3**: Good Friday (no classes)
- **Apr 6**: Easter Monday (no classes)
- **Apr 10**: Parent-Teacher Interviews 10-12 / PD Day K-9 (no classes)

### MAY 2026 (20 School Days, 19 Instructional)
- **May 1**: Area Association/CUPE 3260 Annual Convention (no classes)
- **May 18**: Victoria Day (no classes)

### JUNE 2026 (22 School Days, 19 Instructional)
- **Jun 10**: First Day High School Exams
- **Jun 25**: ✅ **LAST DAY OF CLASSES K-9**
- **Jun 26**: Professional Learning Day - School Goals (no classes)
- **Jun 29**: Administrative Day
- **Jun 30**: Administrative Day, Last Day for Staff

- Start date: September 4, 2025 (not Sept 3, 2024)

- Ensure 181 instructional days are utilized
