## French Immersion vs English Stream

### French Immersion Grade 1 Structure

**Taught in French:**
- Français (Immersion) (French Language Arts)
- Mathématiques (Mathematics)
- Sciences de la nature (Science)
- Études sociales/Sciences humaines (Social Studies)
- Arts (Visual Arts, Music, Dance, Drama)
- Formation personnelle et sociale (Health/Wellness)

**Taught in English:**
- Physical Education (often in English even in French Immersion)
- English Language Arts (typically starts Grade 3-4, not Grade 1)

**NOT Applicable to French Immersion:**
- Health Education (W-1.1 to W-1.14) - This is English stream only
- French Immersion uses "Formation personnelle et sociale" instead

## Root Cause Analysis

### Why Are We Missing Content?

1. **Document Availability**: The actual Math and Science curriculum documents for Grade 1 French Immersion are not in the resources folder

2. **Document Misunderstanding**: PR 2766 was assumed to be comprehensive but is only French Language Arts

3. **Extraction Limitations**: Some documents (Unités transdisciplinaires) weren't processed due to size

4. **Language Confusion**: Mixed English stream and French Immersion content without clear separation
