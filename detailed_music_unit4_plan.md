# Music Unit 4: Instruments and Sound Creation - Detailed Implementation Plan

## Unit Overview
**Grade**: 1 French Immersion  
**Duration**: January 7 - February 14, 2026 (6 weeks)  
**Total Hours**: 12 hours (24 × 30-minute lessons)  
**Frequency**: 4 lessons per week  
**Building On**: Beat foundation (Unit 1), rhythm patterns (Unit 2), and dynamics/tempo (Unit 3)  
**Culmination**: Instrument Discovery Showcase  

## Enhanced Learning Goals
Students will:
- Explore and categorize instrument families (percussion, string, wind, keyboard, electronic)
- Create sounds using voice, body, found objects, and simple instruments
- Develop timbre recognition and sound description vocabulary
- Build ensemble cooperation and musical leadership skills
- Appreciate cultural instruments from diverse traditions
- Express creativity through instrument construction and sound experimentation

## Week 1: Percussion Family Exploration (January 7-10)

### Lesson 1: "Our Percussion Orchestra" (January 7)
**French Connection**: "Notre orchestre de percussion"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Opening Circle (5 min)**: Welcome back with body percussion warm-up
- **Percussion Discovery (18 min)**:
  - Explore classroom percussion: shakers, drums, bells, triangles, wood blocks
  - French vocabulary: "percussion", "tambour" (drum), "clochette" (bell)
  - Sound characteristics: sharp vs. smooth, metallic vs. wooden
  - Demonstration of striking, shaking, scraping techniques
- **Classification Game (5 min)**: Sort instruments by how they make sound
- **Closing (2 min)**: Gentle percussion lullaby

**Materials**: Full classroom percussion collection, classification chart
**Assessment**: Observe instrument handling and sound description abilities

### Lesson 2: "Making Percussion Magic" (January 8)
**French Connection**: "Créer la magie de percussion"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Technique Review (6 min)**: Proper ways to play each percussion instrument
- **Percussion Stations (18 min)**:
  - Station 1: Drums and hand percussion
  - Station 2: Shakers and bells
  - Station 3: Wood blocks and rhythm sticks
  - Station 4: Triangle and metallic sounds
  - French instructions: "doucement" (gently), "fort" (strong)
- **Mini Ensemble (6 min)**: Each station performs for others

**Materials**: Percussion stations setup, technique reminder cards
**Assessment**: Track proper technique and cooperative station work

### Lesson 3: "Found Object Orchestra" (January 9)
**French Connection**: "Orchestre d'objets trouvés"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Sound Exploration (10 min)**:
  - Investigate classroom objects: pencils, books, desks, water bottles
  - Test tapping, scraping, shaking sounds
  - French discovery words: "découvrir" (discover), "explorer" (explore)
- **Creative Construction (15 min)**:
  - Create rhythm instruments from safe classroom materials
  - Bottle shakers with beans/rice
  - Cardboard drums with mallets
  - Paper plate tambourines
- **Presentation (5 min)**: Share favorite found sound

**Materials**: Safe classroom objects, construction supplies, beans/rice
**Assessment**: Evaluate creativity and sound exploration

### Lesson 4: "Percussion Around the World" (January 10)
**French Connection**: "Percussion autour du monde"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Cultural Introduction (8 min)**:
  - Percussion instruments from different cultures
  - Hand drum (Native traditions), cajón (South American), djembe (African)
  - Respectful cultural appreciation
- **Cultural Percussion Experience (17 min)**:
  - Simple hand drum patterns
  - Cajón-style box drumming on chairs
  - French cultural words: "tradition", "culture", "respecter"
- **Gratitude Circle (5 min)**: Thank cultures for sharing musical knowledge

**Materials**: Cultural percussion instruments or pictures, hand drum
**Assessment**: Demonstrate respectful cultural engagement

## Week 2: String Family Discovery (January 13-17)

### Lesson 5: "Strings that Sing" (January 13)
**French Connection**: "Cordes qui chantent"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **String Introduction (10 min)**:
  - Examine guitar, violin, or ukulele (if available)
  - How strings vibrate to make sound
  - French vocabulary: "corde" (string), "vibrer" (vibrate), "pincer" (pluck)
- **Homemade String Exploration (15 min)**:
  - Rubber band guitars on tissue boxes
  - Different tensions create different pitches
  - Plucking vs. strumming techniques
- **String Orchestra (5 min)**: Play homemade instruments together

**Materials**: Tissue boxes, rubber bands, real string instrument for demonstration
**Assessment**: Observe understanding of vibration and pitch concepts

### Lesson 6: "High and Low Adventures" (January 14)
**French Connection**: "Aventures hautes et basses"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Pitch Exploration (12 min)**:
  - Tight rubber bands = high sounds
  - Loose rubber bands = low sounds
  - Voice demonstration: high singing vs. low singing
  - French pitch words: "aigu" (high), "grave" (low)
- **Pitch Matching Games (13 min)**:
  - Teacher plays high/low on instrument, students match with voice
  - Move high (arms up) and low (crouch down)
  - Simple melodies on homemade string instruments
- **Pitch Stories (5 min)**: Tell stories with high and low sounds

**Materials**: Various string instruments, pitch cards, movement space
**Assessment**: Track pitch recognition and vocal matching abilities

### Lesson 7: "String Ensemble Fun" (January 15)
**French Connection**: "Plaisir d'ensemble à cordes"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Ensemble Skills (8 min)**:
  - Practice starting and stopping together
  - Following conductor signals
  - French ensemble words: "ensemble", "chef d'orchestre" (conductor)
- **String Arrangements (17 min)**:
  - Divide into high and low string groups
  - Simple alternating patterns
  - Add percussion to string ensemble
  - Practice "Beautiful Strings" class composition
- **Performance Share (5 min)**: Present to another class

**Materials**: All string instruments, conductor signals, performance space
**Assessment**: Evaluate ensemble cooperation and musical timing

### Lesson 8: "Cultural String Traditions" (January 16)
**French Connection**: "Traditions de cordes culturelles"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **String Heritage (10 min)**:
  - Acadian fiddle traditions
  - Celtic harp connections to PEI
  - Indigenous string instruments (with cultural respect)
  - French cultural instruments: "violon" (violin), "harpe" (harp)
- **Cultural Appreciation (15 min)**:
  - Listen to traditional PEI fiddle music
  - Simple dance movements to string music
  - Respectful discussion of musical heritage
- **Community Connection (5 min)**: How string music brings people together

**Materials**: Cultural string music recordings, cultural resource materials
**Assessment**: Note respectful cultural appreciation and understanding

## Week 3: Wind Family Exploration (January 20-24)

### Lesson 9: "Breath Makes Music" (January 20)
**French Connection**: "Le souffle fait de la musique"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Breath Exploration (8 min)**:
  - Feel breath on hand, control breath flow
  - Make different sounds with breath: "Shhh", "Whooo", "Ppp"
  - French breath words: "souffle" (breath), "respirer" (breathe)
- **Simple Wind Instruments (17 min)**:
  - Blow across bottle tops for pitch sounds
  - Paper kazoos with wax paper
  - Straw instruments by cutting slits
  - Experiment with breath control
- **Wind Ensemble (5 min)**: Combine all breath instruments

**Materials**: Empty bottles, straws, wax paper, tape, kazoo supplies
**Assessment**: Observe breath control and wind instrument technique

### Lesson 10: "Wind Orchestra Discovery" (January 21)
**French Connection**: "Découverte de l'orchestre à vent"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Wind Family Introduction (10 min)**:
  - Pictures/sounds of flute, trumpet, clarinet, saxophone
  - How air creates vibrations in different ways
  - French wind words: "flûte" (flute), "trompette" (trumpet), "air" (air)
- **Wind Simulation Activities (15 min)**:
  - Pretend flute fingering with humming
  - Brass buzzing lips for trumpet sounds
  - "Conducting" wind sections with breath patterns
- **Wind Movement (5 min)**: Move like wind instruments - smooth vs. bold

**Materials**: Wind instrument pictures/recordings, movement scarves
**Assessment**: Track understanding of wind instrument families

### Lesson 11: "Nature's Wind Instruments" (January 22)
**French Connection**: "Instruments à vent de la nature"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Natural Wind Sounds (12 min)**:
  - Wind through trees, over water, through grass
  - Whistle through fingers, blow through hands
  - French nature words: "vent" (wind), "nature", "siffler" (whistle)
- **Nature Wind Creation (13 min)**:
  - Leaf whistles (safe, large leaves)
  - Grass blade humming
  - Shell sounds and beach connections
  - PEI nature sound exploration
- **Nature Symphony (5 min)**: Combine natural wind sounds

**Materials**: Safe natural materials, recordings of PEI nature sounds
**Assessment**: Evaluate creative sound exploration and nature connection

### Lesson 12: "Mi'kmaq Wind Traditions" (January 23)
**French Connection**: "Traditions du vent mi'kmaq"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Respectful Introduction (8 min)**:
  - Acknowledge traditional territory
  - Indigenous wind instruments with cultural respect
  - Importance of breath in Indigenous traditions
- **Cultural Wind Exploration (17 min)**:
  - Simple, appropriate Indigenous-inspired breathing exercises
  - Connection between breath, life, and music
  - Respectful listening to traditional wind music
  - French respect words: "respecter", "honorer" (honor)
- **Gratitude Practice (5 min)**: Thank Indigenous knowledge keepers

**Materials**: Cultural resource person if available, appropriate recordings
**Assessment**: Demonstrate respectful cultural learning approach

### Lesson 16: "Voice as Ultimate Instrument" (January 30)

## Week 5: Sound Creation and Construction (February 3-7)

### Lesson 17: "Instrument Construction Workshop" (February 3)
**French Connection**: "Atelier de construction d'instruments"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Construction Planning (8 min)**:
  - Choose which instrument family to build
  - Gather safe materials for construction
  - French construction words: "construire" (build), "créer" (create), "fabriquer" (make)
- **Building Time (17 min)**:
  - Percussion: decorated shakers, cardboard drums
  - String: rubber band guitars, simple harps
  - Wind: bottle flutes, straw instruments
  - Work in small groups with teacher guidance
- **Testing Phase (5 min)**: Try out newly constructed instruments

**Materials**: Construction supplies, decorating materials, safe building tools
**Assessment**: Observe planning, construction skills, and safety awareness

### Lesson 18: "Sound Science Laboratory" (February 4)
**French Connection**: "Laboratoire de science sonore"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Sound Experiments (20 min)**:
  - How length affects pitch (different straw lengths)
  - How thickness affects sound (thick vs. thin rubber bands)
  - How material affects timbre (metal vs. wood vs. plastic)
  - French science words: "expérience" (experiment), "découverte" (discovery)
- **Scientific Recording (8 min)**:
  - Record discoveries about sound
  - Draw pictures of sound experiments
  - Simple charts: high/low, loud/soft, smooth/rough
- **Science Sharing (2 min)**: Present one discovery to class

**Materials**: Experiment materials, recording sheets, measurement tools
**Assessment**: Track scientific thinking and discovery documentation

### Lesson 19: "Community Instrument Museum" (February 5)
**French Connection**: "Musée d'instruments communautaires"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Museum Setup (10 min)**:
  - Display all constructed instruments
  - Create information cards for each instrument
  - French museum words: "musée" (museum), "exposition" (exhibition), "présenter" (present)
- **Museum Tours (15 min)**:
  - Students give tours of instrument displays
  - Explain how each instrument works
  - Demonstrate playing techniques
  - Practice museum etiquette: quiet voices, careful handling
- **Museum Visitors (5 min)**: Invite another class to visit our museum

**Materials**: Display materials, information cards, museum setup
**Assessment**: Evaluate presentation skills and instrument knowledge

### Lesson 20: "Repair and Care Workshop" (February 6)
**French Connection**: "Atelier de réparation et de soins"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Instrument Care (12 min)**:
  - How to properly care for different instruments
  - Cleaning, storage, gentle handling
  - French care words: "prendre soin" (take care), "nettoyer" (clean), "ranger" (store)
- **Repair Skills (13 min)**:
  - Fix loose strings, tighten drum heads
  - Replace broken materials safely
  - Simple maintenance for homemade instruments
- **Care Demonstration (5 min)**: Show proper care to younger students

**Materials**: Cleaning supplies, repair materials, instrument storage
**Assessment**: Track responsibility and care-taking skills

## Differentiation Strategies

### For Advanced Learners
- **Leadership Roles**: Assist others with instrument construction, lead ensemble sections
- **Complex Compositions**: Create multi-layered pieces incorporating multiple families
- **Cultural Research**: Explore additional cultural instruments and traditions
- **Teaching Opportunities**: Help teach younger students about instrument families

### For Emerging Learners
- **Visual Supports**: Picture cards for all instrument families and techniques
- **Simplified Construction**: Easier instrument building projects with extra support
- **Buddy System**: Partner with confident peer for construction and exploration
- **Multiple Attempts**: Extra time for sound exploration and skill development

### For Diverse Learners
- **Multi-Sensory Approach**: Visual, auditory, tactile exploration of all concepts
- **Choice Options**: Select preferred instrument families and construction projects
- **Flexible Participation**: Various ways to contribute to ensemble and showcase
- **Home Connections**: Include family instruments and musical traditions

## Cultural Sensitivity and Inclusion

- **Territory Acknowledgment**: Respectful recognition of traditional lands
- **Appropriate Sharing**: Only use Indigenous musical elements that are appropriate for sharing

- **Global Awareness**: Explore instrument families from around the world
- **Respectful Education**: Learn about traditions with appreciation and accuracy

- **Classroom Lending**: Loan instruments to families for home practice

- **Inclusive Opportunities**: Ensure all students can participate regardless of resources
