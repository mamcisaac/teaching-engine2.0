# Unit 4: Addition and Subtraction Strategies
## Stratégies d'addition et de soustraction - Mathématiques d'hiver

**Grade:** 1 French Immersion  
**Subject:** Mathématiques  
**Duration:** December 2, 2025 - January 24, 2026 (28 hours over 7 weeks)  
**Teacher:** Emily McIsaac

---

## Unit Overview

### Big Ideas / Grandes idées
- **English:** Addition and subtraction are inverse operations that help us solve problems. Numbers can be composed and decomposed in multiple ways. Strategies make mental math efficient and flexible.
- **Français:** L'addition et la soustraction sont des opérations inverses qui nous aident à résoudre des problèmes. Les nombres peuvent être composés et décomposés de plusieurs façons. Les stratégies rendent les mathématiques mentales efficaces et flexibles.

### Essential Questions / Questions essentielles
1. Comment peux-tu résoudre ce problème d'addition de différentes façons?
2. Qu'est-ce qui arrive quand nous ajoutons zéro à un nombre?
3. Comment la soustraction et l'addition sont-elles liées?
4. Quelle stratégie est la plus efficace pour ce problème?
5. Comment peux-tu expliquer ta pensée mathématique?

### Curriculum Expectations
- **1.N4:** Démontrer une compréhension de l'addition de nombres dont la somme ne dépasse pas 20
- **1.N5:** Démontrer une compréhension de la soustraction de nombres de 20 ou moins
- **1.N6:** Résoudre des problèmes d'addition et de soustraction comportant des nombres de 20 ou moins
- **1.FE1:** Reconnaître l'égalité et l'inégalité concrètement et symboliquement

## Week 1: Counting On Strategies (December 2-6, 2025)

### Learning Goals
Students will develop counting on strategies for addition within 10 using winter and holiday contexts.

### Daily Lessons

#### Day 1: Introduction à l'addition (Introduction to Addition)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Holiday manipulatives, ornament cutouts, ten frames, winter counting bears
**Vocabulary Focus:** addition, additionner, plus, égale

**Activities:**
1. **December Discovery Circle (15 min)**
   - "Décembre est arrivé! Nous allons apprendre l'addition!"
   - Show holiday objects: ornaments, candy canes, snowflakes
   - "Nous pouvons compter et additionner avec des objets d'hiver"

2. **Concrete Addition Exploration (25 min)**
   - Use holiday manipulatives for simple addition
   - Start with stories: "Il y a 3 ornements sur l'arbre, et maman ajoute 2 de plus"
   - Students model with physical objects
   - Practice "counting on": start at 3, count "4, 5"

3. **Ten Frame Introduction (15 min)**
   - Use winter-themed ten frames with snowflakes
   - Show 3 + 2 using ten frame
   - Students practice placing objects and counting
   - "Le cadre de dix nous aide à voir les nombres"

4. **Exit Ticket Winter Addition (5 min)**
   - Quick draw: 2 candy canes + 1 candy cane
   - Students show their work with pictures and numbers

**Assessment:** Observation checklist for counting on strategy and French vocabulary use

#### Day 2: Compter à partir du plus grand (Counting On from the Larger Number)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Number lines, holiday stickers, counting on cards
**Vocabulary Focus:** compter, plus grand nombre, efficace

**Activities:**
1. **Strategy Introduction (15 min)**
   - Show two addition problems: 2 + 7 and 7 + 2
   - Demonstrate both ways: counting from 2 vs counting from 7
   - "Il est plus facile de commencer avec le plus grand nombre!"

2. **Holiday Counting On Practice (30 min)**
   - **Station 1:** Present counting (4 presents + 6 presents)
   - **Station 2:** Cookie addition (3 cookies + 8 cookies)
   - **Station 3:** Snowflake mathematics (5 + 9 snowflakes)
   - Students practice starting with larger number

3. **Number Line Adventures (10 min)**
   - Use classroom number line for counting on
   - Students physically move along number line
   - "Commencez à 8, comptez 8, 9, 10 pour ajouter 2"

4. **Reflection and Strategy Discussion (5 min)**
   - "Pourquoi commence-t-on avec le plus grand nombre?"
   - Preview tomorrow's doubles exploration

**Assessment:** Strategy choice documentation and counting accuracy

#### Day 3: Les doubles magiques (Magical Doubles)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Double dominoes, mirror manipulatives, doubles recording sheets
**Vocabulary Focus:** doubles, même nombre, miroir

**Activities:**
1. **Doubles Discovery (15 min)**
   - Use mirrors with manipulatives
   - "Regardez! 4 + 4 fait 8 - c'est un double!"
   - Show doubles as "twin numbers" or "mirror numbers"
   - Practice doubles 1+1 through 5+5

2. **Holiday Doubles Stations (30 min)**
   - **Station 1:** Ornament doubles (hang matching ornaments)
   - **Station 2:** Cookie doubles (matching cookie pairs)
   - **Station 3:** Gift doubles (wrapping paper patterns)
   - **Station 4:** Snowman doubles (build matching snowmen)

3. **Doubles Chant Creation (10 min)**
   - Create class chant: "Double 3 is 6, hooray!"
   - Add movements for kinesthetic learning
   - Practice with holiday rhythm

4. **Doubles Journal (5 min)**
   - Students record discovered doubles
   - Draw pictures of double situations
   - Goal: memorize doubles to 5+5

**Assessment:** Doubles fluency check and visual representation accuracy

#### Day 4: Presque des doubles (Near Doubles)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Near doubles cards, holiday manipulatives, thinking mats
**Vocabulary Focus:** presque, un de plus, un de moins

**Activities:**
1. **Near Doubles Introduction (20 min)**
   - Start with known double: 6 + 6 = 12
   - "Et si nous avons 6 + 7? C'est presque un double!"
   - Show how 6 + 7 is "double 6 plus 1 more"
   - Practice with concrete objects

2. **Holiday Near Doubles Investigation (25 min)**
   - Present problems in holiday contexts
   - "Il y a 4 cadeaux sous l'arbre et 5 dans le placard"
   - Students solve using "double 4 plus 1" strategy
   - Practice 3+4, 5+6, 4+5, 6+7

3. **Strategy Comparison (10 min)**
   - Compare counting on vs near doubles
   - "Quelle stratégie préférez-vous pour 5+6?"
   - Discuss when each strategy is efficient

4. **Winter Problem Solving (5 min)**
   - Quick winter word problems using near doubles
   - "7 flocons de neige tombent, puis 6 de plus"

**Assessment:** Strategy selection and explanation in French

#### Day 5: Résolution de problèmes d'addition (Addition Problem Solving)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Holiday word problem cards, problem-solving mats, manipulatives choice
**Vocabulary Focus:** problème, résoudre, expliquer, vérifier

**Activities:**
1. **Problem-Solving Process (15 min)**
   - Introduce problem-solving steps in French
   - "Lire, Penser, Résoudre, Vérifier"
   - Model with holiday problem: "Marie a 6 ornements, sa sœur lui donne 4 de plus"

2. **Holiday Problem-Solving Centers (30 min)**
   - **Center 1:** Gift wrapping problems
   - **Center 2:** Cookie baking mathematics
   - **Center 3:** Decoration counting challenges
   - **Center 4:** Winter outdoor activity math

3. **Solution Sharing Circle (10 min)**
   - Students present solutions using different strategies
   - Appreciate multiple solution paths
   - "Il y a plusieurs façons de résoudre un problème!"

4. **Weekly Strategy Reflection (5 min)**
   - Students choose favorite addition strategy
   - Set goals for subtraction introduction next week

**Weekly Assessment:** Addition strategy fluency and problem-solving portfolios

## Week 2: Introduction to Subtraction (December 9-13, 2025)

### Learning Goals
Students will understand subtraction as "take away" and develop counting back strategies.

### Daily Lessons

#### Day 6: Enlever des objets (Taking Away Objects)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Holiday manipulatives, take-away stories, subtraction mats
**Vocabulary Focus:** soustraction, enlever, moins, reste

**Activities:**
1. **Subtraction Story Introduction (15 min)**
   - Tell engaging holiday subtraction story
   - "Il y avait 8 biscuits sur l'assiette. Papa en mange 3."
   - Use real objects to demonstrate "taking away"
   - Introduce subtraction vocabulary in context

2. **Concrete Take-Away Practice (25 min)**
   - Students act out subtraction scenarios
   - Use holiday manipulatives: ornaments, candy canes, gift boxes
   - "Commencez avec 7, enlevez 2, combien reste-t-il?"
   - Practice problems 10 - 4, 8 - 3, 9 - 5

3. **Holiday Subtraction Theater (15 min)**
   - Students create and perform subtraction stories
   - "Il y avait 6 rennes, 2 sont partis voler"
   - Act out with props and costumes
   - Audience calculates the answer

4. **Subtraction Journal Start (5 min)**
   - Begin subtraction portfolio
   - Draw first "take away" story
   - Write subtraction sentence

**Assessment:** Concrete subtraction demonstration and vocabulary use

#### Day 7: Compter à rebours (Counting Back)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Number lines, counting back cards, winter number tracks
**Vocabulary Focus:** compter à rebours, reculer, moins

**Activities:**
1. **Counting Back Introduction (15 min)**
   - Use classroom number line for demonstration
   - "Pour soustraire, nous comptons à rebours!"
   - Show 9 - 3: start at 9, count back "8, 7, 6"
   - Practice with simple examples

2. **Winter Counting Back Adventures (30 min)**
   - **Station 1:** Snowflake number line (outdoor theme)
   - **Station 2:** Icicle counting tracks
   - **Station 3:** Winter animal migration counting
   - **Station 4:** Temperature drop mathematics

3. **Number Line Games (10 min)**
   - "Subtraction Hopscotch" using floor number line
   - Students hop backwards for subtraction
   - Call out problems: "Start at 8, subtract 5"

4. **Strategy Practice (5 min)**
   - Quick practice with counting back for small numbers
   - Focus on subtracting 1, 2, or 3

**Assessment:** Counting back accuracy and number line use

#### Day 8: Différence et comparaison (Difference and Comparison)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Comparison mats, holiday objects for comparing, difference cards
**Vocabulary Focus:** différence, comparer, plus que, moins que

**Activities:**
1. **Difference Concept Introduction (20 min)**
   - Show two groups: 7 ornaments and 4 ornaments
   - "Quelle est la différence entre ces groupes?"
   - Teach subtraction as finding difference
   - "7 - 4 = 3, la différence est 3"

2. **Holiday Comparison Stations (25 min)**
   - **Station 1:** Compare gift quantities
   - **Station 2:** Cookie type comparisons
   - **Station 3:** Decoration color differences
   - **Station 4:** Winter clothing comparisons

3. **Difference Investigation (10 min)**
   - Use ten frames to show differences visually
   - Line up objects to see difference clearly
   - "Combien de plus? Combien de moins?"

4. **Comparison Language Practice (5 min)**
   - Practice comparative language in French
   - "Il y a 3 de plus" / "Il y a 2 de moins"

**Assessment:** Difference calculation and comparative language use

#### Day 9: Familles de nombres (Number Families)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Number family houses, fact family triangles, holiday number sets
**Vocabulary Focus:** famille de nombres, addition et soustraction, inverse

**Activities:**
1. **Number Family Introduction (15 min)**
   - Show number family triangle: 3, 4, 7
   - "Ces nombres forment une famille!"
   - Demonstrate: 3+4=7, 4+3=7, 7-3=4, 7-4=3
   - "L'addition et la soustraction sont des amis!"

2. **Holiday Number Family Creation (30 min)**
   - Students build number families using holiday themes
   - Christmas tree families: 5, 3, 8 (5 red + 3 gold = 8 ornaments)
   - Cookie families: 6, 2, 8 (6 chocolate + 2 sugar = 8 cookies)
   - Create fact family triangles for each

3. **Number Family Games (10 min)**
   - "Complete the Family" game
   - Give two numbers, find the third
   - Practice both addition and subtraction facts

4. **Family Houses Display (5 min)**
   - Create number family houses for classroom
   - Students choose families to display
   - Preview using families for mental math

**Assessment:** Number family completion and relationship understanding

#### Day 10: Stratégies de soustraction (Subtraction Strategies)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Strategy choice boards, holiday problem sets, thinking protocols
**Vocabulary Focus:** stratégie, choisir, efficace, rapide

**Activities:**
1. **Strategy Menu Introduction (15 min)**
   - Present subtraction strategy "menu"
   - Count back, take away, use addition, use doubles
   - "Différents problèmes utilisent différentes stratégies"

2. **Strategy Practice Rotation (30 min)**
   - Students rotate through strategy stations
   - Each station focuses on one subtraction strategy
   - Holiday problems matched to appropriate strategies
   - Students record which strategy they used

3. **Strategy Comparison Discussion (10 min)**
   - Share which strategies worked best for different problems
   - "Pour 10-1, je compte à rebours. Pour 8-4, j'utilise des doubles."
   - Build strategy flexibility

4. **Week 2 Portfolio Review (5 min)**
   - Complete subtraction strategy portfolio
   - Set goals for advanced strategies next week

**Weekly Assessment:** Subtraction strategy selection and fluency

## Week 3: Making 10 Strategies (December 16-20, 2025)

### Learning Goals
Students will develop "make 10" strategies for efficient addition within 20.

### Daily Lessons

#### Day 11: Décomposer pour faire 10 (Decomposing to Make 10)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Ten frames, two-color counters, holiday "make 10" cards
**Vocabulary Focus:** décomposer, faire dix, partie, compléter

**Activities:**
1. **Make 10 Introduction (15 min)**
   - Show ten frame with 7 dots
   - "Combien avons-nous besoin pour faire 10?"
   - Demonstrate 7 + 3 = 10 visually
   - "Dix est un nombre spécial et facile!"

2. **Holiday Make 10 Exploration (30 min)**
   - **Station 1:** Ornament combinations to make 10
   - **Station 2:** Cookie arrangements for 10
   - **Station 3:** Gift bow combinations
   - **Station 4:** Snowflake patterns to 10

3. **Ten Frame Investigation (10 min)**
   - Students fill ten frames in different ways
   - Record all combinations that make 10
   - "Il y a beaucoup de façons de faire 10!"

4. **Make 10 Song Creation (5 min)**
   - Create class song for combinations of 10
   - Add holiday rhythm and movements
   - Practice for automatic recall

**Assessment:** Make 10 combinations accuracy and ten frame use

#### Day 12: Addition en décomposant (Addition by Decomposing)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Decomposition mats, holiday number bonds, thinking charts
**Vocabulary Focus:** décomposer, recomposer, parties, stratégie de dix

**Activities:**
1. **Decomposition Strategy Teaching (20 min)**
   - Show problem: 8 + 5
   - "Je peux décomposer 5 en 2 + 3"
   - "8 + 2 = 10, puis 10 + 3 = 13"
   - Model thinking process clearly

2. **Christmas Shopping Math (25 min)**
   - Present shopping scenarios requiring decomposition
   - "Tu as 9 dollars, tu veux acheter quelque chose qui coûte 6 dollars"
   - Students practice decomposition strategy
   - Use play money and holiday items

3. **Strategy Recording (10 min)**
   - Students record decomposition steps
   - Use thinking maps to show process
   - "D'abord je..., puis je..., alors..."

4. **Peer Teaching (5 min)**
   - Students explain decomposition to partners
   - Practice mathematical discourse in French
   - "Peux-tu expliquer ta stratégie?"

**Assessment:** Decomposition strategy use and mathematical explanation

#### Day 13: Bonds de nombres à 10 (Number Bonds to 10)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Number bond mats, holiday part-whole circles, bond practice cards
**Vocabulary Focus:** liens de nombres, parties et ensemble, automatique

**Activities:**
1. **Number Bonds Introduction (15 min)**
   - Show various ways to represent number bonds
   - Use part-whole circles and number bond notation
   - "Les liens de nombres nous aident à calculer rapidement"

2. **Holiday Number Bond Centers (30 min)**
   - **Center 1:** Christmas tree ornament bonds
   - **Center 2:** Present wrapping paper bonds
   - **Center 3:** Cookie ingredient bonds
   - **Center 4:** Winter sports equipment bonds

3. **Automatic Recall Practice (10 min)**
   - Speed practice with number bonds to 10
   - Use flashcards and quick games
   - Goal: instant recognition of bonds

4. **Bond Application (5 min)**
   - Apply bonds to solve addition problems
   - "Si je connais 6+4=10, je peux résoudre 16+4"
   - Preview bridging through 10

**Assessment:** Number bond fluency and application speed

#### Day 14: Addition à deux chiffres (Two-Digit Addition Introduction)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Base-ten blocks, place value mats, holiday two-digit problems
**Vocabulary Focus:** deux chiffres, dizaines, unités, échanger

**Activities:**
1. **Two-Digit Number Review (15 min)**
   - Review tens and ones with base-ten blocks
   - "Quatorze a une dizaine et quatre unités"
   - Practice building two-digit numbers

2. **Simple Two-Digit Addition (30 min)**
   - Start with no regrouping: 12 + 5, 14 + 3
   - Use base-ten blocks for concrete understanding
   - Holiday context: "14 ornaments + 5 more ornaments"
   - Focus on adding to ones place first

3. **Place Value Addition Practice (10 min)**
   - Use place value mats for organization
   - Students practice with manipulatives
   - Record number sentences

4. **Challenge Introduction (5 min)**
   - Preview adding across ten: 18 + 4
   - "Demain, nous apprendrons à échanger!"
   - Set excitement for advanced strategies

**Assessment:** Two-digit addition with manipulatives and place value understanding

#### Day 15: Problèmes d'hiver (Winter Problem Solving)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Winter word problems, choice of manipulatives, solution recording sheets
**Vocabulary Focus:** résolution de problèmes, stratégies multiples, hiver

**Activities:**
1. **Winter Problem Context (15 min)**
   - Introduce winter mathematical scenarios
   - Ice skating, sledding, building snowmen
   - "L'hiver nous donne beaucoup de problèmes mathématiques!"

2. **Problem-Solving Showcase (35 min)**
   - Students work on multi-step winter problems
   - Choice of strategies and manipulatives
   - Focus on showing mathematical thinking
   - Winter Olympics-themed math problems

3. **Solution Gallery Walk (10 min)**
   - Display various solution methods
   - Students explain different approaches
   - Celebrate mathematical diversity

**Weekly Assessment:** Make 10 strategy application and winter problem solving

## Week 5: Problem Solving Applications (January 13-17, 2026)

Students will apply addition and subtraction strategies to solve real-world problems with mathematical communication.

### Daily Lessons

#### Day 21: Problèmes de mesure d'hiver (Winter Measurement Problems)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Measuring tools, winter clothing, temperature charts, snowfall data
**Vocabulary Focus:** mesurer, température, longueur, comparer

**Activities:**
1. **Winter Measurement Introduction (15 min)**
   - Discuss winter measurements: temperature, snowfall, daylight
   - "L'hiver nous donne beaucoup de choses à mesurer!"
   - Connect measurement to addition and subtraction

2. **Indoor vs Outdoor Temperature (25 min)**
   - Record classroom and outdoor temperatures
   - Calculate differences using subtraction
   - "Il fait 20°C dedans et 5°C dehors. Quelle est la différence?"
   - Graph temperature changes over week

3. **Clothing Measurement Math (15 min)**
   - Measure winter clothing lengths
   - Compare mitten sizes, scarf lengths
   - Use addition to find total length of winter accessories
   - "Mon foulard fait 80 cm, mes mitaines font 15 cm chacune"

4. **Measurement Problem Creation (5 min)**
   - Students create measurement problems for classmates
   - Focus on winter and classroom contexts
   - Share and solve together

**Assessment:** Measurement problem solving and mathematical communication

**Materials:** Clocks, winter activity schedules, time problem cards

**Activities:**

**Activities:**

## Assessment and Evaluation

### Success Criteria / Critères de réussite
- ✅ Je peux additionner des nombres jusqu'à 20 en utilisant différentes stratégies
- ✅ Je peux soustraire des nombres de 20 ou moins avec efficacité
- ✅ Je peux expliquer ma pensée mathématique en français
- ✅ Je peux résoudre des problèmes d'addition et de soustraction dans des contextes réels
- ✅ Je peux choisir la stratégie la plus efficace pour différents problèmes
- ✅ Je peux enseigner des stratégies mathématiques à d'autres élèves

### Assessment Strategies
**Week 1:** Counting on strategy development and holiday problem solving
**Week 2:** Subtraction concept understanding and strategy application
**Week 3:** Make 10 strategies and number bond fluency
**Week 4:** Advanced strategy use and multi-step problem solving
**Week 5:** Real-world application and mathematical communication
**Week 6:** Mastery demonstration through teaching and celebration

### Differentiation Strategies

#### Emerging Learners
- **Concrete Extended:** Focus on manipulatives, counting strategies, simple problems within 10, visual supports, guided practice with teacher, basic French mathematical vocabulary

#### Developing Learners
- **Balanced Progression:** Mix of concrete and abstract, developing strategy flexibility, problems within 20, some independent practice, expanding French mathematical discourse

#### Extending Learners
- **Advanced Applications:** Abstract strategies, complex multi-step problems, numbers beyond 20, mathematical reasoning and justification, sophisticated French mathematical explanations, peer teaching opportunities

## Culminating Task: Festival de Stratégies Mathématiques

Students organize a Mathematical Strategies Festival where they teach addition and subtraction strategies to kindergarten students, present solution methods to community members, and demonstrate mathematical thinking through interactive stations. This authentic assessment allows students to showcase mastery while contributing to the learning community.

### Festival Stations:
1. **Station Compter**: Counting on strategies with manipulatives
2. **Station Doubles**: Doubles and near doubles games
3. **Station Dix**: Make 10 strategies demonstration
4. **Station Problèmes**: Real-world problem solving showcase
5. **Station Communication**: Mathematical explanation videos
