# Getting Started with Teaching Engine 2.0

Welcome to Teaching Engine 2.0! This guide will help you get started with ETFO-aligned planning specifically designed for Grade 1 French Immersion teachers in PEI.

## Overview

Teaching Engine 2.0 helps elementary teachers create comprehensive, curriculum-aligned lesson plans using a 5-level planning hierarchy:

1. **Long-Range Plans** - Yearly overview
2. **Unit Plans** - Multi-week learning sequences  
3. **Lesson Plans** - Individual lesson details
4. **Daybook Entries** - Daily planning notes
5. **Assessment Framework** - Learning skills tracking

## Quick Start (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Complete Onboarding (2 minutes)
**The onboarding wizard starts automatically when you first open the app:**

1. **Welcome & Introduction** - Learn about ETFO planning methodology
2. **Planning Hierarchy** - Understand the 5-level planning framework
3. **Subject Selection** - **CRITICAL STEP**: Choose which subjects you teach:
   - ✅ **Core Subjects**: Français langue première, Mathématiques
   - ✅ **Optional Subjects**: Sciences, Études sociales, English Language Arts, Arts
   - ⚠️ **Specialist Subjects**: Only select Éducation physique, Éducation à la santé if you actually teach them
4. **Feature Tour** - Overview of AI assistant and key features

### Step 2: Explore Your Personalized Curriculum (2 minutes)
1. Navigate to "📋 Curriculum Expectations" from the dashboard
2. **You'll see only the subjects you selected** - this is the key feature!
3. Browse the **68 Grade 1 French Immersion curriculum expectations** for PEI
4. All content is now filtered to match your teaching assignments

### Step 3: Check Your Curriculum Coverage (1 minute)
1. Look at your **Dashboard** - you'll see coverage progress bars for each selected subject
2. Initially, all subjects will show 0% coverage - this is normal!
3. As you create lesson plans, these percentages will automatically increase
4. This helps ensure you address all 68 Grade 1 expectations throughout the year

That's it! You're ready to start planning. The app is now personalized to your specific teaching subjects.

- **Smart Suggestions**: Get contextual recommendations for activities and assessments

### Weekly Planning
- **Calendar View**: See your week at a glance

## Tips for Success

### For New Teachers (Especially New to French Immersion)
- **Complete the onboarding carefully** - subject selection is crucial for your experience
- **Start with core subjects only** (Français, Mathématiques) until comfortable
- **Use the 68 curriculum expectations** as your learning guide for Grade 1 standards
- Start with templates and modify them gradually
- Use the AI suggestions as a learning tool for French Immersion pedagogy
- Don't try to perfect everything immediately
- Ask for help in the built-in chat or help system

### For Experienced Teachers (Transitioning to Subject-Based Planning)
- **Update your subject selection** if your teaching assignment changes
- **Use curriculum coverage tracking** to ensure you're not missing any of the 68 expectations
- Create templates from your best lessons for specific subjects
- Use the AI to suggest new approaches to familiar topics within your subjects
- Share successful subject-specific strategies with colleagues

### For All Grade 1 French Immersion Teachers
- **Verify your subject selection matches your actual teaching assignment**
- **Use curriculum coverage tracking** to stay on top of the 68 expectations
- Plan in small chunks (15-20 minutes at a time)
- Save frequently - auto-save helps but manual saves are safer
- Use the mobile view for quick updates on the go
- Print key plans for backup in case of technical issues

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
- **Slow loading**: Close other tabs, check your internet connection
- **AI not responding**: Try rephrasing your request or wait a moment
- **Lost work**: Check your drafts folder or contact support
- **Print formatting**: Use the "Print View" option for best results

### Getting Help
- **In-App Help**: Click the "?" icon anywhere in the app
- **Video Tutorials**: Available in the Help menu
- **Support Chat**: Use the chat widget in the bottom right
- **Colleague Collaboration**: Share and learn from other teachers
