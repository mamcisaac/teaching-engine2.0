# Detailed Flexible Learning Unit 4 Plan
**Technology Skills & Digital Citizenship**

---

## 📋 Unit Overview

**Duration**: January 6 - February 28, 2026 (45 hours)  
**Grade Level**: Grade 1 French Immersion  
**Integration Focus**: Technology skills and digital citizenship foundations  
**Foundation**: Building on holiday traditions exploration and seasonal investigation skills  

**Mission**: Transform students into confident, responsible digital citizens who use technology as a powerful tool for learning, creating, and connecting. Through hands-on exploration and guided practice, students will develop essential technology skills while building strong foundations for ethical, safe, and purposeful digital participation that will serve them throughout their educational journey.

---

## 🎯 Unit Objectives & Outcomes

### Primary Learning Goals
1. **Basic Technology Skills**: Master fundamental iPad navigation, app use, and digital tool operation
2. **Digital Citizenship Foundations**: Understand online safety, appropriate digital behavior, and responsible technology use
3. **Creative Technology Applications**: Use digital tools for storytelling, art creation, and multimedia expression
4. **Research & Information Literacy**: Develop skills for finding, evaluating, and using digital information appropriately
5. **Digital Collaboration & Sharing**: Learn to work together using technology and share digital creations responsibly

### Curriculum Connections
**Français langue première**: Digital storytelling, multimedia vocabulary, online reading comprehension  
**Mathématiques**: Data collection apps, digital measurement tools, coding concepts, pattern creation  
**Sciences de la nature**: Digital documentation, research tools, virtual experiments, technology in nature  
**Sciences humaines**: Digital community exploration, online cultural resources, virtual museum visits  
**Arts visuels**: Digital art creation, multimedia presentations, creative technology applications  
**Formation personnelle et sociale**: Digital citizenship, online safety, responsible technology use, digital communication ethics

- **Creative Project Analysis**: Assessing technology integration in creative and academic projects

### **Summative Assessment Framework**

#### **Digital Citizenship Portfolio**
Students create comprehensive portfolios including:
1. **Technology Skills Documentation**: Evidence of mastery in basic through advanced technology operations
2. **Digital Safety Knowledge**: Demonstration of understanding and application of online safety principles
3. **Creative Technology Projects**: Examples of using technology for creative expression and academic learning
4. **Digital Communication Examples**: Samples of appropriate digital communication and collaboration
5. **Reflection & Growth Documentation**: Personal assessment of technology learning journey and future goals
6. **Community Connection Evidence**: Documentation of using technology to connect with family and community

#### **Performance-Based Assessment**
- **Technology Navigation Demonstration**: Independent completion of complex multi-step technology tasks
- **Digital Citizenship Scenario Response**: Appropriate handling of various online safety and ethics scenarios
- **Creative Project Presentation**: Using multiple technology tools to create and share learning
- **Peer Teaching & Support**: Demonstrating ability to help others learn technology skills
- **Problem-Solving Application**: Using technology skills to solve real academic and creative challenges

### **Assessment Criteria & Rubrics**

#### **Basic Technology Skills (30%)**
- **Level 1**: Uses technology with significant assistance, demonstrates basic awareness of functions
- **Level 2**: Uses basic technology functions with some guidance, shows developing independence
- **Level 3**: Operates technology independently with confidence, integrates multiple functions effectively
- **Level 4**: Masters technology functions, teaches others, innovates creative applications

#### **Digital Citizenship & Safety (30%)**
- **Level 1**: Shows basic awareness of digital safety with constant reminders and support
- **Level 2**: Demonstrates understanding of safety principles with occasional guidance
- **Level 3**: Consistently applies digital citizenship principles independently
- **Level 4**: Models exemplary digital citizenship, helps others develop safety awareness

#### **Creative & Academic Technology Integration (25%)**
- **Level 1**: Uses technology for basic tasks with significant support and guidance
- **Level 2**: Integrates technology into learning with some teacher direction and support
- **Level 3**: Independently uses technology to enhance learning and creative expression
- **Level 4**: Innovatively applies technology across subjects, inspiring others' creative use

#### **Digital Communication & Collaboration (15%)**
- **Level 1**: Participates in digital collaboration with extensive support and guidance
- **Level 2**: Engages in digital communication appropriately with some teacher direction
- **Level 3**: Collaborates effectively using digital tools, communicates respectfully online
- **Level 4**: Leads digital collaboration, models exceptional online communication skills
