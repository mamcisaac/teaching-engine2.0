## 🎯 Unit Details

### Unit 1: Mon bien-être et moi (My Well-being and Me)
**Focus**: Personal health practices and self-awareness

**Performance Task**: Portfolio de bien-être et foire familiale
- Students create personal wellness portfolios
- Family health fair presentation
- Ambassador role for well-being

**Key Features**:
- Mi'kmaq concept of Msit No'kmaq (all my relations)
- Medicine wheel for understanding wellness balance
- Community health professionals as guests
- Garden-to-table healthy snacks
- Equity focus on access to healthcare

### Unit 2: La sécurité partout (Safety Everywhere)
**Focus**: Safety awareness and personal responsibility

**Performance Task**: Programme ambassadeur de sécurité
- Safety ambassador certification
- Teaching kindergarten students
- Visual safety guide creation

**Key Features**:
- Emergency services partnerships
- Seven Generations teachings on prevention
- Inclusive evacuation planning
- Environmental safety connections
- "Nobody left behind" campaign

### Unit 3: Mes amis et moi (My Friends and Me)
**Focus**: Relationship skills and emotional intelligence

**Performance Task**: Courtepointe de l'amitié communautaire
- Collaborative friendship quilt
- Community installation ceremony
- Individual square representing friendship

**Key Features**:
- Circle process for conflict resolution
- Seven Grandfather teachings on relationships
- Anti-bullying initiatives
- Celebrating all family structures
- "Nobody plays alone" campaign

### Unit 4: Grandir ensemble (Growing Together)
**Focus**: Integration and growth celebration

**Performance Task**: Programme de mentorat et célébration
- Survival guide for future Grade 1 students
- Growth portfolio documentation
- Mentorship of kindergarten students
- Year-end gala presentation

**Key Features**:
- Circle of celebration for growth
- Knowledge transmission to future generations
- Adapted passage ceremony (with permission)
- Scholarship fund creation for next year
- Digital legacy for future classes
