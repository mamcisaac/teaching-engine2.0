# ✨ Arts Unit 4: Winter Light and Holiday Celebrations
**Grade 1 French Immersion | December 16, 2025 - February 7, 2026 | 20 Hours Total**

## 📚 Unit Overview

### Grandes idées / Big Ideas
- **FR**: La lumière et l'ombre créent la magie de l'hiver et des célébrations de nos communautés
- **EN**: Light and shadow create the magic of winter and the celebrations of our communities

### Questions essentielles / Essential Questions
- Comment la lumière change-t-elle en hiver et pendant les fêtes?
- How does light change in winter and during celebrations?
- Que pouvons-nous créer pour célébrer la lumière dans l'obscurité?
- What can we create to celebrate light in darkness?
- Comment les cultures célèbrent-elles la lumière et les fêtes d'hiver?
- How do cultures celebrate light and winter holidays?

### Expectations Covered
- **AV1**: Reconnaître la valeur de son environnement visuel (winter light observation, holiday traditions)
- **AV2**: Utiliser la création artistique afin de communiquer des idées ou des sentiments (celebration through light art)
- **AV3**: Utiliser une variété d'outils, de matériaux, de styles et de techniques (light exploration, 3D creation, mixed media)
- **AV4**: Reconnaître la valeur de l'art en tant qu'élément de notre culture (cultural winter celebrations, community traditions)

## ✨ LIGHT AND SHADOW EXPLORATION

### 1. NATURAL LIGHT STUDIES

#### **Études de lumière naturelle / Natural Light Studies**
**Duration**: 4 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Winter Light Observation**
- **Minds-On**: Observe winter light at different times of day
- **Action**: Document changes in winter light quality through sketching
- **Vocabulary**: lumière (light), ombre (shadow), brillant (bright), doux (soft), doré (golden)
- **Materials**: Observation journals, pencils, colored pencils, clipboards

**Session 2: Shadow Creation Outdoors**
- **Activity**: Create shadows using bodies, objects, and natural materials
- **Documentation**: Photograph and sketch shadow shapes and sizes
- **Exploration**: How shadows change throughout the day
- **Materials**: Digital cameras, measuring tools, shadow documentation sheets

**Session 3: Indoor Light Investigation**
- **Study**: Compare natural window light with artificial classroom lights
- **Creation**: Arrange objects to create interesting shadow patterns
- **Artistic Response**: Draw the difference between light sources
- **Materials**: Various objects, light sources, drawing materials

**Session 4: Light and Shadow Art Creation**
- **Integration**: Use light observation learning in artwork
- **Technique**: Create paintings showing winter light effects
- **Expression**: Show mood and feeling through light representation
- **Materials**: Watercolors, tempera paints, brushes, light reference sketches

### 2. ARTIFICIAL LIGHT SOURCES

#### **Sources de lumière artificielle / Artificial Light Sources**
**Duration**: 3 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Holiday Light Exploration**
- **Investigation**: Study different types of holiday lights and decorations
- **Observation**: Notice how artificial lights create warmth and celebration
- **Safety**: Learn about electrical safety and appropriate light sources for art
- **Materials**: Various safe light sources, observation tools, safety guidelines

**Session 2: Flashlight Shadow Play**
- **Activity**: Use flashlights to create dramatic shadows on walls
- **Creativity**: Make shadow puppets and shadow stories
- **Documentation**: Record interesting shadow discoveries
- **Materials**: Flashlights, white sheets/walls, shadow puppet materials

**Session 3: Candle-Safe Light Art (Electric)**
- **Safety**: Use battery-powered candles for safe light art
- **Creation**: Create art inspired by candlelight warmth
- **Technique**: Paint the glow and warmth of candle-like light
- **Materials**: Battery candles, warm-colored paints, brushes

### 3. LIGHT AND DARK CONTRAST

#### **Contraste lumière et obscurité / Light and Dark Contrast**
**Duration**: 4 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Value Scale Creation**
- **Learning**: Understand light, medium, and dark values
- **Practice**: Create gradual value scales from white to black
- **Application**: Use value scales in winter scene creation
- **Materials**: Pencils, charcoal, blending tools, practice paper

**Session 2: Dramatic Light Effects**
- **Technique**: Create artwork showing strong light/dark contrasts
- **Theme**: Spotlight effects on winter scenes
- **Expression**: Use contrast to show drama and excitement
- **Materials**: Black paper, white and light-colored paints, brushes

**Session 3: Sunrise and Sunset Winter Scenes**
- **Study**: Observe winter sunrise/sunset photographs
- **Creation**: Paint dramatic winter sky light effects
- **Color**: Use warm colors for light, cool colors for shadows
- **Materials**: Tempera paints, large brushes, gradient techniques

**Session 4: Night and Day Winter Comparisons**
- **Project**: Create paired artworks showing same scene day/night
- **Contrast**: Emphasize the difference light makes to winter landscapes
- **Technique**: Use different color palettes for each version
- **Materials**: Two sets of paper, full paint palette, comparison references

### 4. TRANSPARENT, TRANSLUCENT, OPAQUE

#### **Transparent, translucide, opaque / Transparent, Translucent, Opaque**
**Duration**: 3 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Material Light Investigation**
- **Exploration**: Test how light passes through different materials
- **Classification**: Sort materials by how they interact with light
- **Documentation**: Create charts showing material light properties
- **Materials**: Various materials, light sources, classification charts

**Session 2: Stained Glass Effect Art**
- **Technique**: Use tissue paper and contact paper for stained glass effects
- **Creation**: Design simple winter or holiday stained glass art
- **Display**: Hang in windows to catch natural light
- **Materials**: Colored tissue paper, contact paper, black construction paper

**Session 3: Layered Transparency Art**
- **Method**: Layer transparent and translucent materials
- **Effect**: Create depth and light interaction through layering
- **Theme**: Winter scenes with layered snow, ice, and sky effects
- **Materials**: Transparent papers, vellum, acetate, layering adhesives

### 5. WINTER LIGHT QUALITY STUDIES

#### **Études de qualité de lumière hivernale / Winter Light Quality Studies**
**Duration**: 3 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Soft Winter Light**
- **Observation**: Study how snow and clouds soften winter light
- **Technique**: Create soft, diffused light effects in paintings
- **Materials**: Soft brushes, blending techniques, muted colors
- **Expression**: Capture peaceful, gentle winter moments

**Session 2: Crisp Clear Winter Light**
- **Study**: Observe sharp, clear light on bright winter days
- **Creation**: Paint scenes with sharp shadows and bright highlights
- **Contrast**: Use strong value contrasts for crisp light effects
- **Materials**: Detailed brushes, high-contrast colors, sharp edge techniques

**Session 3: Golden Hour Winter Light**
- **Magic Hour**: Study warm golden light in winter settings
- **Emotion**: Connect golden light with feelings of warmth and hope
- **Technique**: Use warm colors to show golden hour effects
- **Materials**: Warm color palette, glazing techniques, soft brushes
