## Mathematics Crisis Analysis

### What We Found
1. **SP2**: Construct and interpret concrete graphs and pictograms
2. **1N5**: Compare sets up to 20 elements
3. **N1**: Compare sets up to 20 elements (duplicate)

### What's Missing (85-90%)
- **Number Strand**: 
  - Counting to 100
  - Addition/subtraction facts to 10
  - Place value (tens and ones)
  - Ordinal numbers

- **Geometry Strand**:
  - 2D shapes (circle, square, triangle, rectangle)
  - 3D objects (sphere, cube, cylinder)
  - Spatial relationships

- **Measurement Strand**:
  - Length (non-standard units)
  - Time (days, weeks, months)
  - Money concepts
  - Mass and capacity

- **Patterning Strand**:
  - Creating patterns
  - Extending patterns
  - Pattern rules

### Root Cause
PEI uses an **integrated transdisciplinary approach** where math is embedded in thematic units rather than taught as a discrete subject with formal expectations.
