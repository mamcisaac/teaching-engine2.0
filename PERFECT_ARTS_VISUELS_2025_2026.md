## 🎨 THE 6 PERFECT UNIT PLANS

### Unit 1: Découvrir l'art dans notre monde (Sept 8 - Oct 10)
**Duration**: 5 weeks, 10 hours  
**Expectations**: AV1, AV3

**Focus**: Exploring art all around us - in nature, classroom, and community
- Identifying art in our environment
- Introduction to basic art materials
- Exploring different tools and techniques
- Building art observation skills

**Culminating Task**: "Art in My World" collection showcasing found art and personal creations

### Unit 2: Les couleurs et les sentiments (Oct 14 - Nov 21)
**Duration**: 6 weeks, 12 hours  
**Expectations**: AV2, AV3

**Focus**: How colors express emotions and ideas
- Primary and secondary color mixing
- Connecting colors to feelings
- Using color to communicate
- Exploring color in nature and culture

**Culminating Task**: "Feelings Rainbow" artwork showing emotions through color choices

### Unit 3: Les célébrations d'hiver par l'art (Nov 24 - Jan 30)
**Duration**: 10 weeks, 16 hours  
**Expectations**: AV2, AV3, AV4

**Focus**: Creating art for winter celebrations and cultural traditions
- Making decorations and gifts
- Learning about cultural art traditions
- Creating art to share with others
- Understanding art as celebration

**Culminating Task**: Winter celebration art gallery with decorations and gifts

### Unit 4: Les textures et les motifs (Feb 2 - Mar 27)
**Duration**: 8 weeks, 16 hours  
**Expectations**: AV1, AV3, AV4

**Focus**: Exploring texture through printmaking and patterns
- Creating different textures
- Finding patterns in nature and culture
- Printmaking techniques
- Mixed media exploration

**Culminating Task**: Textured pattern quilt square for collaborative class quilt

### Unit 5: Les histoires dans l'art (Mar 30 - May 15)
**Duration**: 7 weeks, 14 hours  
**Expectations**: AV2, AV3

**Focus**: Using visual art to tell stories and communicate ideas
- Illustrating stories and ideas
- Creating characters through art
- Sequential art and storytelling
- Being illustrators

**Culminating Task**: Class illustrated storybook featuring student artwork

### Unit 6: Notre galerie d'art (May 19 - June 25)
**Duration**: 5 weeks, 12 hours  
**Expectations**: AV1, AV2, AV4

**Focus**: Celebrating growth as artists and sharing with community
- Reflecting on artistic journey
- Curating personal exhibitions
- Creating artist statements
- Appreciating others' art

**Culminating Task**: Student-curated art gallery exhibition for families and community
