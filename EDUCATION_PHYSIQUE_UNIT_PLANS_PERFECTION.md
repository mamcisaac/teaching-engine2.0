- **Body Awareness**: Understanding how bodies move
- **Balance Mastery**: Static and dynamic equilibrium

### Unit 2: Jeux et manipulation (Skills)
**Performance Task**: Festival des jeux d'habiletés
- **Object Control**: Throwing, catching, manipulation mastery
- **Hand-Eye Coordination**: Progressive skill development
- **Game Skills**: Foundation for sports participation
- **Equipment Familiarity**: Safe and effective use

- **Sportsmanship**: Fair play and respect
- **Leadership**: Emerging captain skills

- **Performance**: Confidence in presentation
- **Innovation**: Original movement sequences
