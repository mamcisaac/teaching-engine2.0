# 🚀 Quick Assessment Tool - Quick Start Guide

## For Grade 1 Teachers

### Getting Started (2 minutes)

#### Step 1: Add Your Students
1. Click **"Assessment & Tracking"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Set Up Student Roster"** button
3. Add each student:
   - Click **"Add Student"** 
   - Enter first and last name
   - Click the ✓ to save
4. Your roster is automatically saved!

#### Step 2: Start Your First Assessment
1. On the Assessment page, click **"Quick Assessment Grid"**
2. The grid shows all your students with 4 mastery levels:
   - 🔴 **Not Yet** - Student needs significant support
   - 🟡 **Approaching** - Student is developing the skill
   - 🟢 **Meeting** - Student meets expectations
   - 🔵 **Exceeding** - Student exceeds expectations

#### Step 3: Assess Your Students
1. For each student, click their mastery level
2. The button will highlight when selected
3. Default is "Meeting" if not assessed

#### Step 4: Create Tomorrow's Groups
1. Click **"Create Groups"** button
2. Students are automatically sorted into:
   - **Reteaching** - For students marked "Not Yet"
   - **Support** - For students marked "Approaching"
   - **Independent** - For students marked "Meeting"
   - **Extension** - For students marked "Exceeding"

#### Step 5: Save Your Work
1. Click **"Save & Close"**
2. Your assessment is saved with:
   - Date and time stamp
   - Student assessments
   - Tomorrow's groups
   - Automatic backup

## Daily Workflow (30 seconds per lesson)

### From Your Day View
1. Click **"Quick Assess"** on any lesson block
2. Grid opens with that lesson's context
3. Click mastery levels for each student
4. Click **"Create Groups"** → **"Save & Close"**
5. Done! Groups ready for tomorrow

### Quick Tips
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: Use Tab to move between students
- **Bulk Assessment**: If most students are meeting, just assess the exceptions
- **View Groups**: Click "Tomorrow's Groups" to see your differentiated groups
- **Export Data**: Use "Export" button to save a backup file

## Common Scenarios

### "I made a mistake"
- Just click a different mastery level - it updates instantly
- Or close without saving and start over

### "I need to add a new student mid-year"
1. Go to Assessment page
2. Click **"Manage Roster"**
3. Add the new student
4. They'll appear in your next assessment

### "I want to see yesterday's groups"
- Groups are saved by date
- Check "Tomorrow's Groups" section on Assessment page

### "Can I assess multiple subjects?"
- Yes! Each assessment is independent
- Do a quick assessment for Math, then another for Reading

## Data Management

### Your Data is Safe
- ✅ Saved locally on your computer
- ✅ Auto-backup after every save
- ✅ Works offline
- ✅ Export anytime for extra backup

### Export Your Data
1. Click **"Quick Actions"** menu (⚡ icon)
2. Click **"Export Data"**
3. Save the file to your computer

### Import Data (New Computer)
1. Click **"Quick Actions"** menu
2. Click **"Import Data"**
3. Select your backup file

## Troubleshooting

### "The button doesn't work"
- Refresh the page (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
- Check that students are in your roster

### "I can't see my students"
- Click "Manage Roster" to add students
- Make sure you saved after adding them

### "My data disappeared"
- Check "Quick Actions" → "Import Data"
- Your browser might have cleared storage
- Always export important data as backup
