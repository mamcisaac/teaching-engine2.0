# Detailed Flexible Learning Unit 2 Plan
**Fall Projects & Seasonal Exploration**

---

## 📋 Unit Overview

**Duration**: October 1-31, 2025 (40 hours)  
**Grade Level**: Grade 1 French Immersion  
**Integration Focus**: Autumn scientific investigation and cultural celebration  
**Foundation**: Building on September's library orientation and reading discovery skills  

**Mission**: Transform students into autumn investigators who observe, document, and celebrate seasonal changes while developing independent research skills, choice-based learning, and deep connections to PEI's rich fall traditions and natural environment.

---

## 🎯 Unit Objectives & Outcomes

### Primary Learning Goals
1. **Scientific Investigation**: Develop systematic observation and documentation skills through autumn phenomena
2. **Choice-Based Learning**: Build independent project selection and self-directed learning capabilities
3. **Cultural Celebration**: Explore and participate in fall traditions while respecting diverse cultural perspectives
4. **Technology Integration**: Use digital tools for documentation, research, and creative expression
5. **Cross-Curricular Synthesis**: Apply skills from all subjects to meaningful, real-world autumn investigations

### Curriculum Connections
**Français langue première**: Vocabulary expansion (autumn terminology), descriptive writing, oral presentations  
**Mathématiques**: Data collection and graphing, measurement, pattern recognition, counting and sorting  
**Sciences de la nature**: Scientific observation, classification systems, weather patterns, life cycles  
**Sciences humaines**: Cultural traditions, community celebrations, seasonal changes in PEI communities  
**Arts visuels**: Natural art creation, seasonal color exploration, documentation through visual arts  
**Formation personnelle et sociale**: Gratitude practices, community celebration, seasonal safety awareness

## 📅 Weekly Breakdown (40 hours total)

### **Week 1 (October 1-5): Autumn Awakening - Becoming Fall Investigators**
*Hours: 10 hours (includes project launch and intensive observation training)*

#### Day 1: Fall Investigation Launch (2.5 hours)
**Morning Block (1.5 hours)**:
- Autumn sensory exploration walk on school grounds
- Introduction to "Fall Investigation Journals" (individual documentation books)
- Initial observations of seasonal changes using five senses
- French autumn vocabulary introduction: automne, feuilles, changements, couleurs, récolte

**Afternoon Block (1 hour)**:
- Choice menu introduction: Students select their primary autumn investigation focus
- Project options: Leaf Collection & Classification, Weather Tracking, Animal Behavior Changes, Harvest Investigation, Migration Documentation
- Formation of investigation teams based on interests
- Beginning of personal investigation planning

#### Day 2: Scientific Observation Skills (2 hours)
**Activities**:
- Detailed leaf observation using magnifying glasses and scientific drawing techniques
- Introduction to measurement tools and data collection methods
- Beginning weather station setup and daily tracking protocols
- Creating observation templates and data recording sheets
- Practice with digital photography for documentation

#### Day 3: PEI Autumn Traditions Exploration (2 hours)
**Activities**:
- Guest speaker from PEI Museum sharing Island fall traditions
- Exploration of Acadian, Mi'kmaq, and other cultural autumn celebrations
- Introduction to thanksgiving traditions across cultures
- Beginning research on family autumn traditions and practices
- Creating cultural connection journals

#### Day 4: Technology Tool Introduction (2 hours)
**Activities**:
- iPad apps for nature identification and data collection
- Introduction to simple presentation software for project sharing
- Digital photography techniques for scientific documentation
- Beginning creation of digital investigation portfolios
- Safe research practices using educational websites

#### Day 5: Choice Project Deep Dive (1.5 hours)
**Activities**:
- Intensive work on chosen autumn investigation projects
- Peer consultation and idea sharing sessions
- Initial data collection and documentation practice
- Setting personal investigation goals for the month
- Reflection on first week's discoveries and learning

### **Week 2 (October 8-12): Deep Investigation & Data Collection**
*Hours: 10 hours (including Thanksgiving Monday exploration)*

#### Monday (October 8): Thanksgiving Traditions & Gratitude (2.5 hours)
**Special Focus: Canadian Thanksgiving Cultural Exploration**
- Multi-cultural thanksgiving traditions research and sharing
- Gratitude investigation project: documenting things we're thankful for in PEI
- Harvest celebration planning and traditional foods exploration
- Creating class gratitude display and individual thankfulness journals
- Family tradition interview project launch

#### Tuesday-Friday: Intensive Investigation Period (7.5 hours)

**Daily Structure (1.5 hours each day)**:
- **Morning Investigation Time**: Independent work on chosen projects
- **Peer Collaboration Sessions**: Sharing discoveries and seeking feedback
- **Documentation Practice**: Recording observations in multiple formats
- **Technology Integration**: Using tools for research and presentation creation

**Investigation Project Options**:

#### **Leaf Collection & Classification Project**
- Systematic collection of different leaf types from school and home areas
- Scientific classification using shape, size, color, and texture
- Creating detailed leaf identification guide for PEI trees
- Documenting color changes over time through photography
- Creating artistic leaf arrangements and preservation techniques

#### **Weather Tracking & Pattern Analysis**
- Daily temperature, precipitation, and wind observations
- Creating weather graphs and pattern identification
- Comparing October weather to previous months
- Researching how weather affects plants, animals, and human activities
- Developing weather prediction skills based on natural signs

#### **Animal Behavior Documentation**
- Observing and recording bird migration patterns around school
- Documenting how squirrels and other animals prepare for winter
- Creating identification guides for local autumn wildlife
- Researching animal adaptations to seasonal changes
- Photography project documenting animal behaviors

#### **Harvest Investigation Project**
- Researching what foods are harvested in PEI during autumn
- Visiting or virtual tours of local farms during harvest season
- Documenting apple picking, potato harvest, and other Island traditions
- Creating cookbook of simple autumn recipes using PEI ingredients
- Interviewing farmers or family members about harvest traditions

#### **Migration & Seasonal Change Documentation**
- Tracking which birds migrate from PEI and which stay for winter
- Documenting how trees and plants change throughout October
- Creating time-lapse photography projects of seasonal changes
- Researching how other animals (butterflies, marine life) respond to autumn
- Making connections between PEI's location and migration patterns

**Friday Special Focus**: Cross-Project Sharing Sessions where different investigation teams present preliminary findings to each other

### **Week 3 (October 15-19): Creative Expression & Technology Integration**
*Hours: 10 hours*

#### Focus: Transforming investigations into creative presentations and deepening technology skills

**Monday (2 hours): Digital Storytelling & Presentation Creation**
- Introduction to simple video creation apps and slideshow tools
- Planning digital presentations of investigation findings
- Beginning creation of individual or team presentation projects
- Learning basic editing skills for photos and simple videos

**Tuesday (2 hours): Autumn Arts & Natural Materials**
- Creating art projects using collected leaves, seeds, and natural materials
- Exploring autumn color palettes and seasonal artistic traditions
- Integrating scientific observations with artistic expression
- Documenting art creation process for portfolios

**Wednesday (2 hours): Cross-Curricular Investigation Extensions**
- **Math Integration**: Graphing collected data, measuring natural materials, creating patterns
- **French Integration**: Learning and using autumn vocabulary in investigation documentation
- **Social Studies Integration**: Researching how different communities celebrate autumn
- **Health Integration**: Exploring seasonal foods and their nutritional benefits

**Thursday (2 hours): Community Connections & Expert Consultations**
- Virtual or in-person interviews with local experts (farmers, naturalists, Mi'kmaq knowledge keepers)
- Connecting investigation findings to PEI community practices and traditions
- Researching local environmental organizations and autumn programs
- Planning community sharing opportunities for project results

**Friday (2 hours): Presentation Preparation & Peer Review**
- Finalizing digital presentations and creative project components
- Peer feedback sessions and collaborative improvement
- Rehearsing presentations and organizing display materials
- Preparing for family and community sharing events

### **Week 4 (October 22-26): Cultural Celebrations & Halloween Safety**
*Hours: 10 hours*

#### Focus: Celebrating autumn traditions while maintaining investigation project momentum

**Monday (2 hours): Halloween Traditions & Safety**
- Cultural exploration of Halloween traditions from different countries
- Halloween safety investigation and community safety practices
- Creating safety guides and Halloween rule sets for families
- Exploring costume traditions and creative, sustainable costume ideas

**Tuesday (2 hours): Harvest Festival Planning**
- Planning classroom harvest celebration incorporating all investigation findings
- Creating invitations and organizing presentation stations
- Preparing traditional and modern autumn games and activities
- Organizing potluck-style celebration with autumn foods

**Wednesday (2 hours): PEI Autumn Community Celebrations**
- Researching local fall festivals, farmers' markets, and community events
- Creating guide to "October in PEI" for families and visitors
- Connecting investigation projects to real community celebrations
- Planning potential field trips or community participation opportunities

**Thursday (2 hours): Investigation Project Finalization**
- Completing final documentation and presentation preparation
- Creating investigation portfolio summaries and reflection pieces
- Peer review sessions and final collaboration opportunities
- Preparing individual and team presentation materials

**Friday (2 hours): Harvest Celebration & Project Showcase**
- Classroom harvest festival with family and community guests
- Student presentation of autumn investigation projects

- Celebration of learning growth and investigation skills development

#### **Scientific Investigation Skills (25%)**
- **Level 1**: Basic observation and data collection with significant support

- **Level 4**: Advanced investigation with creative problem-solving and peer teaching

#### **Choice & Independence (25%)**
- **Level 1**: Makes choices with extensive guidance and support
- **Level 2**: Makes appropriate choices with some teacher direction
- **Level 3**: Makes thoughtful, independent choices with confidence
- **Level 4**: Makes sophisticated choices and helps others develop choice-making skills

#### **Technology Integration (25%)**
- **Level 1**: Uses technology tools with significant assistance and guidance
- **Level 2**: Uses basic technology tools appropriately with some support
- **Level 3**: Integrates technology effectively and independently into learning
- **Level 4**: Uses technology creatively and teaches others technology skills

#### **Community & Cultural Connections (25%)**

This comprehensive October unit creates authentic, engaging learning experiences that honor PEI's rich autumn traditions while developing students' investigation skills, technology literacy, and appreciation for cultural diversity. The foundation established in September's library orientation supports students' growing independence in choice-based learning, while October's seasonal focus provides meaningful context for applying skills across all subject areas.
