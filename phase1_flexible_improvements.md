## 📅 New Unit Structure (6 Perfect Units)

### 1. **September: Library Orientation & Reading Discovery**
- **Duration**: 35 hours (Sep 4-30, 2025)
- **Focus**: Library navigation, book selection, independent reading skills
- **Integration**: Français (reading), Sciences humaines (community resources), Arts visuels (illustration)
- **Technology**: Digital catalog introduction, e-book basics
- **Assessment**: Portfolio with library skills demonstration

### 2. **October: Fall Projects & Seasonal Exploration**  
- **Duration**: 40 hours (Oct 1-31, 2025)
- **Focus**: Student-led autumn projects, seasonal observations, Thanksgiving gratitude
- **Integration**: Sciences (seasonal changes), Arts (nature art), Math (counting/sorting)
- **Technology**: Digital photography, weather tracking, virtual field trips
- **Assessment**: Project showcase with multiple presentation formats

### 3. **November-December: Holiday Traditions & Community Celebrations**
- **Duration**: 50 hours (Nov 1 - Dec 20, 2025)
- **Focus**: Diverse cultural celebrations, holiday crafts, school performances
- **Integration**: Sciences humaines (cultural diversity), Arts (crafts), Music (holiday songs)
- **Technology**: Virtual cultural visits, digital holiday cards
- **Assessment**: Multicultural Holiday Festival participation

### 4. **January-February: Technology Skills & Digital Citizenship**
- **Duration**: 45 hours (Jan 5 - Feb 28, 2026)
- **Focus**: iPad basics, safe technology use, digital creativity, online safety
- **Integration**: ALL subjects enhanced by technology skills
- **Technology**: iPad navigation, creative apps, digital portfolio creation
- **Assessment**: Digital Portfolio Showcase with skills demonstration

### 5. **March-April: Research Projects & Information Exploration**
- **Duration**: 50 hours (Mar 1 - Apr 30, 2026)
- **Focus**: Student-initiated research, question development, presentation skills
- **Integration**: All subjects provide research opportunities
- **Technology**: Simple online research, digital presentations, educational databases
- **Assessment**: Research Fair with peer teaching

### 6. **May-June: Year-End Celebrations & Reflection**
- **Duration**: 55 hours (May 1 - Jun 25, 2026)
- **Focus**: Learning reflection, growth celebration, Grade 2 preparation
- **Integration**: Portfolio review across all subjects
- **Technology**: Digital portfolio compilation, reflection videos
- **Assessment**: Year-long learning celebration and goal-setting
