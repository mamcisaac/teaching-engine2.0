# Unit Plan: Nos droits et responsabilités
## Grade 1 Social Studies | October 7 - November 15, 2025 | 25 Hours

### OVERVIEW
**Unit Title (French):** Nos droits et responsabilités  
**Unit Title (English):** Our Rights and Responsibilities  
**Duration:** 6 weeks (25 hours total)  
**Primary Focus:** Understanding basic rights and responsibilities in classroom, family, and PEI community contexts

### CURRICULUM EXPECTATIONS ADDRESSED
- **1ICC.2**: Examiner les droits et responsabilités en classe et dans sa communauté
- **1ER.2**: Analyser des façons de résoudre des problèmes et de contribuer au bien-être de sa classe et de sa communauté

### BIG IDEAS
- Tous les enfants ont des droits importants
- Avec les droits viennent des responsabilités
- Nous pouvons résoudre des problèmes ensemble
- Notre communauté sur l'Île-du-Prince-Édouard nous aide et nous devons l'aider aussi

## DETAILED RIGHTS UNDERSTANDING ACTIVITIES

### Week 1: Comprendre nos droits fondamentaux

#### **Children's Rights Exploration**
**Activity Duration:** 4 sessions (45 minutes each)

**Core Rights to Explore:**
1. **Droit d'apprendre:** Right to education and learning
2. **Droit de jouer:** Right to play and rest
3. **Droit à la sécurité:** Right to safety and protection
4. **Droit d'être entendu:** Right to be heard and express opinions
5. **Droit à l'identité culturelle:** Right to cultural identity and language

**Materials:**
- Children's rights picture cards (custom made for Grade 1)
- "My Rights Book" templates
- Art supplies for rights posters
- Photos of children engaging in various activities

**Process:**
1. **Rights Discovery Circle:** Introduction through storytelling and pictures
2. **Rights Sorting Game:** Match pictures to different rights
3. **Personal Rights Reflection:** "When do I use this right?"
4. **Rights Poster Creation:** Visual representation of each right
5. **Rights Book Assembly:** Personal booklet with examples

**PEI Connections:**
- Right to learn in French and English on PEI
- Right to learn about Mi'kmaq, Acadian, and other Island cultures
- Right to play on Island beaches, parks, and playgrounds
- Right to be safe during Island weather (storms, winter conditions)

#### **Classroom Rights Charter Development**
**Activity Duration:** 3 sessions (60 minutes each)

**Democratic Process for Rights Recognition:**
1. **Brainstorming Session:** "What rights do we have in our classroom?"
2. **Discussion and Clarification:** Understanding what each right means
3. **Visual Charter Creation:** Large poster with illustrations
4. **Signing Ceremony:** All students and teacher commit to respecting rights
5. **Daily Rights Check-in:** Morning reflection on rights protection

**Classroom Rights Examples:**
- Right to learn without interruption
- Right to feel safe and respected
- Right to share ideas and opinions
- Right to make mistakes and learn
- Right to be included in activities
- Right to use French and English
- Right to celebrate our cultures

**Community Rights Connections:**
- Rights children have in PEI schools
- Rights in community centers and libraries
- Rights when visiting local places
- Rights during community events and festivals

### Week 2: Nos responsabilités importantes

#### **Understanding Responsibilities**
**Activity Duration:** 4 sessions (45 minutes each)

**Core Responsibilities to Develop:**
1. **Responsabilité envers l'apprentissage:** Taking care of our learning
2. **Responsabilité envers les autres:** Caring for classmates and community
3. **Responsabilité envers l'environnement:** Protecting our Island environment
4. **Responsabilité culturelle:** Respecting and sharing our cultures
5. **Responsabilité familiale:** Helping at home and with family

**Interactive Responsibility Activities:**
- **Responsibility Role-Play:** Acting out different scenarios
- **Problem-Solution Matching:** Connecting problems with responsible actions
- **Responsibility Pledge Creation:** Personal commitments
- **Community Helper Connections:** How helpers show responsibility
- **Responsibility Tracking Charts:** Daily self-assessment

**PEI-Specific Responsibilities:**
- Keeping Island beaches and parks clean
- Respecting traditional territories and cultural sites
- Supporting local farmers and fishers
- Learning about Island history and heritage
- Being welcoming to Island visitors and newcomers

#### **Rights and Responsibilities Balance**
**Activity Duration:** 3 sessions (60 minutes each)

**Balance Exploration Activities:**
1. **Rights-Responsibilities Scales:** Visual balance demonstration
2. **Scenario Analysis:** "If I have this right, what's my responsibility?"
3. **Classroom Examples:** Practical applications in daily routines
4. **PEI Community Examples:** How Islanders balance rights and responsibilities
5. **Personal Reflection:** "How do I balance my rights and responsibilities?"

**Practical Scenarios for Grade 1:**
- Right to play / Responsibility to include others
- Right to learn / Responsibility to listen and participate
- Right to express opinions / Responsibility to respect others' opinions
- Right to cultural identity / Responsibility to respect other cultures
- Right to safety / Responsibility to help keep others safe

## PROBLEM-SOLVING SKILLS DEVELOPMENT

### Week 4: Résoudre des problèmes ensemble

#### **Conflict Resolution Framework**
**Activity Duration:** 4 sessions (60 minutes each)

**Grade 1 Conflict Resolution Steps:**
1. **STOP:** Take a breath and calm down
2. **LISTEN:** Hear what each person has to say
3. **THINK:** What solutions could work for everyone?
4. **CHOOSE:** Pick the best solution together
5. **TRY:** Use the solution and see how it works
6. **CHECK:** Did it solve the problem? If not, try again

**Practice Scenarios Based on Grade 1 Experiences:**
- Sharing classroom materials and supplies
- Taking turns with popular playground equipment
- Including new students in games and activities
- Dealing with different languages at recess
- Resolving disagreements about game rules
- Handling accidentally hurting someone's feelings

**Cultural Conflict Resolution:**
- Learning from Mi'kmaq talking circles
- Acadian community mediation traditions
- Island community "kitchen table" problem solving
- Newcomer family communication strategies

#### **Decision-Making Processes**
**Activity Duration:** 3 sessions (45 minutes each)

**Democratic Decision-Making Practice:**
1. **Classroom Voting:** Simple majority decisions
2. **Consensus Building:** Everyone agrees solutions
3. **Compromise Solutions:** Meeting in the middle
4. **Turn-Taking Systems:** Fair rotation methods
5. **Random Selection:** When fairness requires chance

**Real Classroom Decisions:**
- Choosing classroom theme decorations
- Selecting books for reading corner
- Planning special classroom celebrations
- Organizing classroom helpers and jobs
- Deciding on field trip preferences

**PEI Community Decision Examples:**
- How communities decide on festival activities
- Choosing community improvement projects
- Planning local celebrations and events
- School community input on new programs

#### **Seeking Help Appropriately**
**Activity Duration:** 2 sessions (45 minutes each)

**When and How to Ask for Help:**
- **Classroom Problems:** Teacher, classmates, older students
- **Playground Issues:** Duty teachers, peer mediators
- **Family Concerns:** Parents, grandparents, trusted adults
- **Community Problems:** Police, firefighters, community workers
- **Learning Difficulties:** Teachers, resource teachers, parents

**Help-Seeking Role-Play:**
- Practicing polite ways to ask for help
- Understanding when problems are too big to solve alone
- Recognizing trusted adults in different settings
- Using proper channels for different types of problems

**PEI-Specific Help Resources:**
- Island Helpline services for families
- Community centers and support programs
- Local libraries as safe community spaces
- Neighborhood watch and community safety programs

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Week 6: Célébrer notre apprentissage

#### **Rights and Responsibilities Portfolio**
**Comprehensive Assessment Collection:**

**Portfolio Components:**
1. **Personal Rights Book:** Illustrated booklet with rights understanding
2. **Responsibility Tracking Chart:** Evidence of daily responsibility practice
3. **Problem-Solving Success Stories:** Examples of conflict resolution
4. **Community Helper Appreciation:** Thank you letters and reflections
5. **PEI Community Connection Map:** Personal involvement in Island community
6. **Democratic Participation Record:** Evidence of classroom decision-making involvement

**Assessment Criteria:**
- **Rights Understanding:** Can explain basic children's rights
- **Responsibility Practice:** Shows evidence of taking responsibilities seriously
- **Problem-Solving Skills:** Uses learned strategies for conflict resolution
- **Community Engagement:** Participates actively in community life
- **Democratic Participation:** Contributes to classroom decision-making

#### **Citizenship Skills Demonstration**
**Activity Duration:** 5 sessions (60 minutes each)

**Demonstration Areas:**
1. **Rights Advocacy:** Standing up for own and others' rights respectfully
2. **Responsibility Modeling:** Showing leadership in fulfilling responsibilities
3. **Conflict Mediation:** Helping others solve problems peacefully
4. **Community Contribution:** Leading or participating in service projects
5. **Cultural Bridge-Building:** Helping connect different cultural perspectives

**Assessment Methods:**
- **Peer Observation:** Students observe and report on classmates' citizenship skills
- **Self-Reflection:** Personal assessment of growth and learning
- **Teacher Documentation:** Anecdotal records of citizenship behaviors
- **Family Input:** Home observations of responsibility and rights understanding
- **Community Feedback:** Input from guest speakers and community partners

#### **Problem-Solving Skills Assessment**
**Scenario-Based Evaluation:**

**Assessment Scenarios:**
1. **Playground Conflict:** Two students want same equipment
2. **Cultural Misunderstanding:** Different family traditions cause confusion
3. **Learning Difficulty:** Student struggling with classroom work
4. **Community Problem:** Litter in local park
5. **Group Decision:** Class needs to choose between two fun activities

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- **Problem Identification:** Can recognize and define problems clearly
- **Solution Generation:** Can think of multiple possible solutions
- **Perspective Taking:** Considers others' viewpoints and feelings
- **Decision Making:** Can choose appropriate solutions
- **Implementation:** Follows through on chosen solutions
- **Evaluation:** Reflects on effectiveness and adjusts as needed

### **Transition to Unit 3:**
This rights and responsibilities foundation prepares students for future social studies learning by establishing:
- Understanding of democratic participation and citizenship
- Skills for peaceful conflict resolution and problem-solving
- Connection to local, provincial, and cultural community systems
- Appreciation for diverse approaches to governance and decision-making
- Commitment to ongoing community service and engagement

### **Ongoing Citizenship Development:**
- Daily classroom democracy practice
- Weekly community service opportunities
- Monthly community helper appreciation
- Seasonal environmental stewardship projects
- Year-round cultural celebration and bridge-building

The citizenship skills, community connections, and democratic participation developed in this unit will support all future learning and provide foundation for active, engaged citizenship throughout students' educational journey and beyond.

### **Family and Community Engagement Continuation:**
- Regular communication about citizenship learning at home
- Family involvement in community service projects
- Cultural sharing and celebration ongoing
- Community helper relationship building
- Democratic decision-making practice extension to family contexts

This comprehensive approach ensures that rights and responsibilities learning becomes integrated into daily life and supports students' development as caring, engaged citizens of their classroom, school, Island community, and broader world.
