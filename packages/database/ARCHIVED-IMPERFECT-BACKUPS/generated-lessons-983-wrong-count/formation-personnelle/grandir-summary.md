# Grandir, changer et célébrer ensemble - Unit Summary

## Overview
**Title:** Grandir, changer et célébrer ensemble  
**Subject:** Formation personnelle et sociale  
**Grade Level:** Grade 1 French Immersion  
**Total Lessons:** 20 (14 core + 6 extensions)  
**Duration:** April 25 - May 20, 2026  
**Final Score:** 92/100  

## Unit Focus Areas
- **Growth and Change:** Personal development and transformation
- **Milestones:** Celebrating achievements and progress
- **Traditions:** Family and cultural celebrations
- **Community:** Classroom and school community building

## Key Features

### Special Activities Included
- **Time Capsule Creation** (Lesson 8): Students preserve current memories for future reflection
- **Family Story Sharing** (Lesson 9): Oral tradition and family narratives
- **Growth Portfolio** (Lessons 12-13): Comprehensive documentation of learning journey
- **Community Celebration** (Lesson 19): Culminating festival bringing together all elements

### Progression Structure
**Lessons 1-4:** Activation & Personal Exploration
- Individual growth awareness
- Personal changes and achievements
- Family celebration introduction
- Baby vs. current self comparison

**Lessons 5-11:** Development & Community Building
- Peer growth recognition
- Future dreams and goals
- Classroom tradition creation
- Time capsule preservation
- Family storytelling
- Pride moment identification
- School community appreciation

**Lessons 12-14:** Synthesis & Presentation
- Learning growth celebration
- Portfolio compilation
- Growth journey presentations

**Lessons 15-20:** Extensions & Creative Practice
- Vocabulary games
- Creative art projects
- Family tradition theater
- Collaborative class book
- Community celebration
- Reflective journey conclusion
