## 📊 Integrated Assessment Strategies

### Single Observation → Multiple Subjects

#### Morning Circle Time
**One observation captures:**
- **Français**: Oral communication, vocabulary use, listening
- **Math**: Calendar skills, counting, patterns
- **FPS**: Social skills, respect, participation
- **Sciences**: Weather observations, seasonal changes

**Assessment Tool**: Single checklist with columns for each subject

#### Science Exploration Station
**One observation captures:**
- **Sciences**: Hypothesis, observation, recording
- **Français**: Scientific vocabulary, oral explanations
- **Math**: Measuring, counting, data recording
- **Arts**: Scientific drawing accuracy

#### Art Creation Time
**One observation captures:**
- **Arts**: Technique, creativity, use of materials
- **Français**: Art vocabulary, describing work
- **FPS**: Self-expression, persistence, cleanup
- **Math**: Shapes, patterns, symmetry

### Portfolio Pieces (Multi-Subject Evidence)

#### September Portfolio Entry: "All About Me"
**Single piece demonstrates:**
- **Français**: Writing development, vocabulary
- **Arts**: Self-portrait skills
- **Math**: Age, counting family members
- **FPS**: Identity awareness
- **Soc. Studies**: Family structure

### Integrated Performance Tasks

#### Week 1: "Our Classroom Community"
**Task**: Create classroom map with labels
**Assesses**:
- Fr: Labeling, vocabulary
- Math: Spatial sense, counting
- Soc. Studies: Community understanding
- Arts: Representation skills

#### Week 2: "My Learning Journey"
**Task**: Learning reflection journal entry
**Assesses**:
- Fr: Writing development
- FPS: Self-awareness
- Arts: Illustration
- Sciences: Daily observations

## 🎯 Assessment Integration Calendar

### Daily Assessment Opportunities

**Morning (8:30-10:00)**
- Circle time: Fr oral + Math calendar + FPS social
- Shared reading: Fr comprehension + Sciences/Soc. Studies content

**Mid-Morning (10:15-11:30)**
- Math block: Math skills + Fr vocabulary + Sciences data

**Afternoon (12:30-2:00)**
- Integrated projects: All subjects
- Observation time: Multiple subjects in one activity

**End of Day (2:00-2:30)**
- Reflection: Fr oral + FPS self-assessment + daily learning

### Weekly Assessment Rhythm
- **Monday**: Fresh start observations (baseline)
- **Tuesday-Thursday**: Formative checks (growth)
- **Friday**: Celebration & documentation (summative)

## 📝 Efficiency Gains Through Integration

### Time Saved
- **Word Walls**: 1 update serves 6 subjects (saves 30 min/week)
- **Assessment**: 1 observation for 3-4 subjects (saves 2 hrs/week)
- **Materials Prep**: Shared resources (saves 1 hr/week)

### Enhanced Learning
- **Vocabulary**: Used 6x more often across subjects
- **Concepts**: Reinforced throughout the day
- **Connections**: Students see learning as connected, not isolated

### Teacher Benefits
- Reduced planning time through integration
- More authentic assessment
- Clearer picture of whole child
- Simplified material management
