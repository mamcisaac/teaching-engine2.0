## 🎯 Word Wall Activities by Subject Integration

### Daily Word Wall Routines (5 minutes)

#### Monday: Math-French Connection
**Activity**: Number Word of the Day
- Choose a number word
- Find that many objects
- Use in a French sentence
- Create a math problem

#### Tuesday: Science-French Connection
**Activity**: Observation Word
- Choose a science verb (observer, mesurer, comparer)
- Use in morning weather observation
- Add to science journal
- Practice in French sentence

#### Wednesday: Arts-French Connection
**Activity**: Descriptive Word
- Choose a color/texture/size word
- Find examples in classroom art
- Use to describe today's art
- Add to art vocabulary book

#### Thursday: Social Studies-French Connection
**Activity**: Community Word
- Choose a community/family word
- Share personal connection
- Use in social studies discussion
- Add to class community book

#### Friday: Cross-Curricular Review
**Activity**: Word Connection Web
- Student picks any word
- Class identifies all subject connections
- Create visual web on chart paper
- Celebrate cross-curricular use

## 💡 Word Wall Management Tips

### Setup and Maintenance
1. **Location**: Eye level, accessible, central
2. **Organization**: Thematic over alphabetical
3. **Rotation**: Add 5-8 words weekly, retire mastered words
4. **Student Ownership**: Students add words and examples
5. **Visual Appeal**: Consistent colors, clear fonts, images

### Making It Interactive
- Velcro words for sorting activities
- Pocket charts for word of the day
- Pointer sticks for word wall walks
- Highlighting tape for connections
- Post-its for student additions

### Keeping It Fresh
- Weekly word wall photographers
- Word wall reporters (announce new words)
- Connection detectives (find subject links)
- Word artists (illustrate words)
- Word wall DJ (songs with words)

## 🎯 Success Indicators

### Students are successfully using the word wall when they:
- Reference it independently during work
- Make connections between subjects
- Use vocabulary accurately across contexts
- Add their own discoveries
- Teach words to others

### The word wall system is effective when:
- Vocabulary appears naturally in student work
- Cross-curricular connections are explicit
- All subjects are represented
- Students show ownership
- Families report vocabulary use at home
