## 📊 THE UNIVERSAL TRUTH: Daily Requirements

### Consistent Daily Structure
- **Morning Block 1:** French Language Arts (45 minutes)
- **Morning Block 2:** Mathematics in French (45 minutes)
- **Afternoon Block 1:** Science in French (45 minutes)  
- **Afternoon Block 2:** Arts in French (45 minutes)
- **Afternoon Block 3:** Social Studies/Health in French alternating (45 minutes)

**Total:** 225 minutes of instruction daily, 100% conducted in French
