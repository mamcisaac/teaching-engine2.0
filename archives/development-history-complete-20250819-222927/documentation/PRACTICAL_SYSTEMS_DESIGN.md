# 🛠️ PRACTICAL SYSTEMS DESIGN
## Daily Arts Program Implementation Framework

**Purpose:** Transform pedagogical excellence into classroom reality through practical, actionable systems for Emily McIsaac's Grade 1 French Immersion Arts program.

---

## 🎯 MATERIALS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

### **Daily Supply Distribution for 30 Students**

#### **Portable Art Caddy System**
- **6 Rolling Caddies** (5 students per caddy)
- Each caddy contains:
  - Individual supply boxes (5 per caddy)
  - Shared materials (paint, water containers)
  - Cleanup supplies (wet wipes, paper towels)
  - Portfolio folders (5 per caddy)

#### **Individual Student Supply Box Contents:**
- Personal drawing tools: pencils, erasers, glue stick
- Small sketchbook for daily warm-ups
- Personal name label in French and English
- Individual portfolio tracker sheet

#### **Shared Materials per Caddy:**
- Water containers (2 per caddy)
- Washable tempera paints (primary colors + black/white)
- Brushes (various sizes, labeled)
- Scissors (safety, age-appropriate)
- Construction paper variety pack

#### **Daily Distribution Protocol:**
1. **Minute 0-2:** Table captains retrieve caddies
2. **Minute 2-5:** Students organize personal supplies
3. **Minute 40-43:** Students return materials to caddies
4. **Minute 43-45:** Table captains return caddies to storage

### **Wet Work Drying System**

#### **Drying Station Setup:**
- **Wall-mounted drying racks** (6 racks, 5 slots each)
- Each slot labeled with student name
- Clips for securing wet artwork
- Collection trays for drip protection

#### **Drying Rotation Protocol:**
- Monday wet work → dry by Wednesday
- Wednesday wet work → dry by Friday
- Friday wet work → dry by Monday
- Immediate portfolio addition upon drying

### **Portfolio Storage and Access**

#### **Individual Portfolio System:**
- **30 individual portfolios** in accessible cubbies
- Monthly dividers (Sept-June)
- Daily addition protocol
- Weekly organization time

#### **Portfolio Organization:**
- **Growth documentation:** Before/during/after photos
- **Reflection sheets:** Simple French vocabulary prompts
- **Skill tracking:** Visual checklist with stickers
- **Family sharing:** Monthly take-home selections

## ⏱️ REALISTIC TIMING STRUCTURE

### **45-Minute Lesson Breakdown**

#### **Setup Phase (5 minutes):**
- **Minutes 0-2:** Students enter, wash hands, gather caddies
- **Minutes 2-5:** Material distribution and workspace setup
- **Goal:** Smooth transition from previous subject

#### **ETFO Instruction Phase (35 minutes):**

**Minds On (8 minutes):**
- **Minutes 5-8:** French vocabulary warm-up
- **Minutes 8-10:** Prior knowledge activation
- **Minutes 10-13:** Learning goal introduction and motivation

**Action (22 minutes):**
- **Minutes 13-15:** New technique demonstration
- **Minutes 15-30:** Guided and independent creation time
- **Minutes 30-35:** Peer sharing and support

**Consolidation (5 minutes):**
- **Minutes 35-38:** Reflection and learning summary
- **Minutes 38-40:** Portfolio addition and goal setting

#### **Cleanup Phase (5 minutes):**
- **Minutes 40-43:** Artwork preservation and material return
- **Minutes 43-45:** Workspace cleaning and preparation for next class

### **Assessment Integration Timeline**

#### **Throughout Action Phase:**
- **Minutes 15-20:** Focus group observation (5 students)
- **Minutes 20-25:** General class monitoring
- **Minutes 25-30:** Focus group interaction and feedback
- **Minutes 30-35:** Portfolio notation and planning

### **Weekly Timing Patterns**

#### **Monday - Foundation Setting:**
- Extra 2 minutes for weekly material organization
- Weekly goal setting and vocabulary introduction
- Portfolio system check

#### **Tuesday-Thursday - Skill Development:**
- Standard timing for technique building
- Focus on progressive skill development
- Individual conferencing integration

#### **Friday - Reflection and Sharing:**
- Extended consolidation (7 minutes)
- Weekly accomplishment celebration
- Portfolio organization and selection

## 🔄 CONFLICT RESOLUTION PROTOCOLS

### **Assembly/Special Event Adaptations**

#### **Short Notice Assemblies:**
- **Dry Media Day:** Switch to pencil/crayon activities
- **Quick Portfolio Work:** Individual reflection and organization
- **French Vocabulary Review:** Art term practice and games

#### **Fire Drill Procedures:**
- **Wet Work Protocol:** Immediate pause, hands cleaned
- **Material Security:** Caddies closed and pushed aside
- **Resume Protocol:** Quick restart with dry materials

### **Early Dismissal Adaptations**

#### **30-Minute Lesson Structure:**
- **Setup:** 3 minutes
- **Instruction:** 22 minutes (condensed ETFO structure)
- **Cleanup:** 5 minutes

#### **20-Minute Emergency Structure:**
- **Combined Setup/Minds On:** 5 minutes
- **Action:** 12 minutes (simplified activity)
- **Combined Consolidation/Cleanup:** 3 minutes

### **Holiday Schedule Modifications**

#### **Shortened Week Adaptations:**
- **Priority Activities:** Focus on portfolio work and reflection
- **Simplified Materials:** Use readily available supplies
- **Celebration Integration:** Connect to cultural celebrations
