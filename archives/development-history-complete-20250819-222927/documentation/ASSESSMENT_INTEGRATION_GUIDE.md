# 📊 ASSESSMENT INTEGRATION GUIDE
## Responsive Inquiry Science - Consolidation Phase Assessment

**Purpose**: Transform the 10-minute Consolidation phase into powerful, manageable assessment that captures real learning without creating extra work for Emily.

---

## 🎯 ETFO ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK INTEGRATION

### **Assessment FOR Learning** (Daily - Built into Consolidation)
**Purpose**: Immediate feedback to improve tomorrow's learning
- **Method**: 3-minute observation notes during Action phase
- **Tool**: Quick voice memo or sticky notes
- **Focus**: What students understand, what they're struggling with

### **Assessment AS Learning** (Daily - Student Reflection)
**Purpose**: Students monitor their own learning
- **Method**: Science journal reflection prompts
- **Tool**: Simple sentence starters and drawings
- **Focus**: Student self-awareness of learning

### **Assessment OF Learning** (Weekly/Unit - Portfolio Evidence)
**Purpose**: Evaluation for reporting
- **Method**: Consolidation evidence collection
- **Tool**: Photo documentation and work samples
- **Focus**: Meeting curriculum expectations

---

## ⏰ 10-MINUTE CONSOLIDATION ASSESSMENT STRUCTURE

### **Minutes 1-3: Sharing Circle (Assessment FOR Learning)**
**Purpose**: Gauge understanding and engagement

**Emily's Quick Assessment Questions:**
- "One word to describe your discovery today?"
- "What surprised you during our investigation?"
- "How did what we learned connect to yesterday?"
- "What question do you still have?"

**What Emily Listens For:**
✅ **Understanding**: Do students use appropriate vocabulary?  
✅ **Thinking**: Are they making connections and asking questions?  
✅ **Engagement**: Do they show excitement about discoveries?  
✅ **Safety**: Are they mentioning safe procedures?  

**Quick Documentation (30 seconds):**
- Voice memo: "Day 5 - Students excited about material sorting, James asked about why plastic floats"
- Mental note: "Need to review living/non-living with Sarah tomorrow"

### **Minutes 4-7: Science Journal Entry (Assessment AS Learning)**
**Purpose**: Students reflect on and document their learning

**Daily Journal Structure:**
```
Today I discovered: [Drawing + 1-2 words]
I wondered: [Simple question or drawing]
Tomorrow I want to learn: [Drawing or words]
French words I used: [1-2 vocabulary words]
```

**Grade 1 Appropriate Expectations:**
- Drawing is primary documentation method
- 1-2 words with support is sufficient
- Invented spelling is celebrated
- French words can be copied from word wall

**Emily's Role During Journal Time:**
- Circulate and have 30-second conversations with 4-5 students
- Take photos of exceptional journal entries
- Note vocabulary usage and scientific thinking
- Provide encouragement and gentle guidance

### **Minutes 8-10: Preview & Cleanup (Assessment OF Learning Collection)**
**Purpose**: Set up tomorrow and collect evidence

**Preview Connection:**
- "Tomorrow we'll test which materials are strongest. What do you predict?"
- Quick connection to today's learning
- Build excitement for next investigation

**Evidence Collection (Emily):**
- **Photo 1**: Group sharing circle moment
- **Photo 2**: Sample science journal entries  
- **Quick Note**: Safety demonstrations observed
- **Voice Memo**: Outstanding student quotes or thinking

**Student Cleanup Procedures:**
- Practice safety protocols
- Care for living materials
- Reset investigation area
- Prepare for next subject

## 📱 STREAMLINED DOCUMENTATION TOOLS

### **Daily Tools (1-2 minutes total documentation time)**

**Option 1: Voice Memos (Fastest)**
- Record while students do journal entries
- "September 8, Lesson 5: Great engagement with material sorting, need to review texture vocabulary tomorrow, James showed excellent safety with tools"
- Transcribe later or use as verbal notes for planning

**Option 2: Quick Note Template**
```
Date: _____ Lesson: _____
Understanding: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (1-5 stars)
Engagement: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 
Safety: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Notes: ________________
Tomorrow focus: __________
```

**Option 3: Phone Photography**
- 1 photo of student work
- 1 photo of group investigation
- Voice memo description while taking photos

### **Weekly Pattern (5 minutes Friday afternoon)**

**Friday Consolidation Extension (15 minutes instead of 10):**
- **Portfolio Review**: Students choose best work from week
- **French Vocabulary Review**: Practice week's science words

- **Preview Next Week**: Build anticipation

## 🏆 ASSESSMENT EVIDENCE COLLECTION

### **What Counts as Evidence (Grade 1 Appropriate)**

**Strong Evidence:**
✅ **Scientific Drawing**: Shows observation and understanding  
✅ **Vocabulary Usage**: Using French science words in conversation  
✅ **Question Asking**: Shows curiosity and thinking  
✅ **Safety Demonstration**: Follows protocols independently  
✅ **Classification Accuracy**: Sorts materials/objects correctly  
✅ **Connection Making**: Links to previous learning  

**Developing Evidence:**
📝 **Attempted Vocabulary**: Tries French words with support  
📝 **Basic Classification**: Sorts with adult guidance  
📝 **Following Safety**: Needs reminders but complies  
📝 **Simple Observations**: Notices basic features  
📝 **Participates**: Engages in investigations with encouragement  

**Emerging Evidence:**
🌱 **Shows Interest**: Wants to participate even if uncertain  
🌱 **Basic Safety**: Responds to safety reminders  
🌱 **Attempts Communication**: Uses gestures, pointing, simple words  
🌱 **Tries Investigations**: Participates with significant support  

### **Evidence Collection Schedule**

**Daily (10-minute Consolidation):**
- 1 quick observation note OR voice memo
- 1-2 photos of student work or investigations
- Mental note of vocabulary usage and safety compliance

**Weekly (Friday Consolidation + 5 minutes):**
- Review week's photos and notes
- Complete weekly assessment summary
- Plan next week's focus areas
- Select portfolio pieces with students

**Unit-End (Final lesson of unit):**
- Safety procedure demonstrations
- French vocabulary usage assessment
- Portfolio presentation by students
- Unit goal achievement documentation

## 📋 SIMPLE RUBRICS FOR CONSOLIDATION ASSESSMENT

### **Daily Quick Assessment (Circle One)**

**Understanding Today's Investigation:**
🌟 Exceeded - Made connections, asked advanced questions  
⭐ Met - Completed investigation, showed understanding  
📝 Approaching - Participated with support, some understanding  
🌱 Beginning - Participated, building foundation  

**Safety Protocol Following:**
✅ Independent - Follows all safety procedures without reminders  
👥 Supported - Follows with occasional reminders  
🔄 Developing - Needs consistent reminders but complies  
⚠️ Concern - Requires constant supervision for safety  

**French Vocabulary Usage:**
🇫🇷 Confident - Uses words correctly in conversation  
📚 Practicing - Attempts words with some accuracy  
👂 Learning - Understands words, beginning to use  
🌟 Interested - Shows interest, not yet using independently  

### **Weekly Assessment Summary (Check All That Apply)**

**This week student demonstrated:**
□ Accurate classification of materials/objects  
□ Independent safety procedure following  
□ Use of 2+ French science vocabulary words  
□ Scientific thinking through questioning  
□ Connections to previous learning  
□ Respect for living things and environment  
□ Collaboration skills during investigations  
□ Growth mindset when facing challenges

## 🎯 REPORT CARD PREPARATION

### **September Report Cards (Unit 1 Evidence)**

**Science Expectations Met:**
- **1.1.1 (Living Things)**: Portfolio of living/non-living classifications + photo evidence
- **1.1.2 (Human Impact)**: Documentation of school environment care activities

**Key Evidence Sources:**
- Safety procedure demonstration videos (30 seconds each)
- Science journal progression (first entry vs. final entry)
- French vocabulary usage recorded in weekly voice memos
- Classification accuracy documented through photos

**Report Card Comments (Sample Language):**
- "Demonstrates strong safety awareness during science investigations"
- "Shows curiosity about the natural world through thoughtful questions"
- "Beginning to use French science vocabulary in daily conversations"
- "Classifies living and non-living things with increasing accuracy"

### **Ongoing Assessment Notes (For Parent Communication)**

**Positive Examples to Document:**
- "Today [Student] noticed that the tree outside our window is losing leaves and connected it to our fall changes discussion"
- "[Student] independently reminded classmates to wash hands before science investigation"
- "[Student] used the word 'vivant' correctly when describing our classroom plant"
- "[Student] asked an excellent question: 'Why do some materials float and others sink?'"

## 🔄 DIFFERENTIATED ASSESSMENT APPROACHES

### **For Struggling Learners:**
- **Documentation**: Photo evidence of participation and effort
- **Communication**: Allow demonstration instead of verbal explanation
- **Portfolio**: Include drawings and simple manipulations
- **Success Criteria**: Focus on growth and participation over accuracy

### **For Advanced Learners:**
- **Documentation**: Record advanced questions and connections
- **Communication**: Encourage detailed explanations in French
- **Portfolio**: Include independent investigations and leadership examples
- **Success Criteria**: Focus on depth of thinking and helping peers

### **For English Language Learners:**
- **Documentation**: Value home language insights and multilingual thinking
- **Communication**: Accept combination of languages and gestures
- **Portfolio**: Include visual representations and bilingual work
- **Success Criteria**: Focus on understanding over French production

## ✅ ASSESSMENT SUCCESS INDICATORS

**The system is working when:**
✅ Emily feels confident about student progress daily  
✅ Assessment takes less than 5 minutes total per lesson  
✅ Students enjoy reflection time and see it as part of learning  
✅ Evidence clearly shows student growth over time  
✅ Report card comments are supported by concrete examples  
✅ Parents receive meaningful communication about science learning  
✅ Assessment information directly improves next day's teaching  

**Remember**: The goal is not perfect documentation but meaningful evidence that helps Emily teach responsively and celebrate student growth in scientific thinking, safety awareness, and French language development.

**Most Important**: Every student should feel successful in some way during every Consolidation phase, and Emily should leave each lesson knowing how to better support student learning tomorrow.
