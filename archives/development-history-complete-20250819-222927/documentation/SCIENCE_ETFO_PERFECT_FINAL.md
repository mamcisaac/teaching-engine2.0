### **Unit 1: Fall Explorations** (Sept-Oct)
- **Duration:** 21 lessons, 16 hours
- **Theme:** Discovering our environment and seasonal changes
- **Focus:** Scientific observation, living/non-living, fall adaptations
- **Highlights:** Sustained outdoor exploration, foundational science skills

### **Unit 2: Energy & Winter Investigations** (Nov-Dec)
- **Duration:** 17 lessons, 13 hours  
- **Theme:** Energy forms and winter phenomena
- **Focus:** Energy transformations, states of matter, winter science
- **Highlights:** Energy experiments, ice/snow investigations

### **Unit 3: Materials & Light** (Jan-Feb)
- **Duration:** 20 lessons, 15 hours
- **Theme:** Properties of materials and light/sound
- **Focus:** Material testing, structures, light properties, sound vibrations
- **Highlights:** Engineering challenges, shadow play, musical investigations

### **Unit 4: Spring Science** (Mar-Apr)
- **Duration:** 21 lessons, 16 hours
- **Theme:** Life cycles and seasonal transformations
- **Focus:** Plant growth, spring changes, life cycles, animal behavior
- **Highlights:** Class garden, growth tracking, spring documentation

### **Unit 5: Connections & Celebrations** (May-Jun)
- **Duration:** 19 lessons, 14 hours
- **Theme:** Interconnections and year-long reflection
- **Focus:** Ecosystems, plant-animal relationships, science portfolio
- **Highlights:** Connection webs, celebration of learning
