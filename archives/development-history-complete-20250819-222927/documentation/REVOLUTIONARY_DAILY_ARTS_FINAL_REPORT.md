## 📚 10-UNIT PROGRESSIVE SEQUENCE

### 🎯 Foundation Phase (Sept-Dec)

**Unit 1: Fondements Artistiques (September)** - 21 lessons
- Drawing fundamentals, tool introduction, artistic vocabulary in French
- Portfolio setup, self-expression foundations
- Expectations: AV1, AV3

**Unit 2: Lignes et Marques (October)** - 21 lessons  
- Mark-making progression, line variety, observational drawing
- Building fine motor control through daily practice
- Expectations: AV1, AV3

**Unit 3: Exploration des Couleurs (November)** - 20 lessons
- Color theory basics, primary/secondary mixing, emotional color
- Fall seasonal connections, cultural celebrations
- Expectations: AV2, AV3, AV4

**Unit 4: Art Culturel et Célébrations (December)** - 13 lessons
- Cultural art forms, winter celebrations, family traditions
- Indigenous perspectives, community connections
- Expectations: AV2, AV4

### 🎨 Development Phase (Jan-Apr)

**Unit 5: Textures et Matériaux (January)** - 20 lessons
- Tactile exploration, collage techniques, mixed media
- Sensory art experiences, material properties
- Expectations: AV2, AV3

**Unit 6: Motifs et Impression (February)** - 21 lessons
- Pattern recognition, printmaking introduction, stamping
- Mathematical connections, repetition concepts
- Expectations: AV1, AV3

**Unit 7: Exploration 3D (March)** - 21 lessons
- Beginning sculpture, construction techniques, spatial thinking
- Clay work, paper sculpture, recyclable materials
- Expectations: AV2, AV3

**Unit 8: Art Environnemental (April)** - 20 lessons
- Nature-based art, land art, environmental awareness
- Outdoor connections, Earth Day themes
- Expectations: AV1, AV4

### 🌟 Mastery Phase (May-Jun)

**Unit 9: Techniques Avancées (May)** - 21 lessons
- Integration of all previous skills, advanced projects
- Choice-based learning, independent exploration
- Expectations: AV2, AV3

**Unit 10: Célébration du Portfolio (June)** - 21 lessons
- Year-end reflection, portfolio organization, art exhibition
- Growth documentation, family sharing, artistic identity
- Expectations: AV2, AV4
