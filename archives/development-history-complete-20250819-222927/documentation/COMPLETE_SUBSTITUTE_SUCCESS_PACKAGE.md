## 🌟 THE SIMPLE EXCELLENCE STRUCTURE - EVERY LESSON

### 📚 **WONDER (5-8 minutes) - Getting Curious**

**Your Role**: Get students excited about today's discovery
- Gather students in circle area (they know where)
- Ask the wonder question provided in lesson plan
- Let 3-4 students share ideas - all answers are good!
- Show the learning goal simply and clearly

**Student Expectations**: 
- Sit in circle and listen to friends
- Share ideas when invited
- Get excited about exploring

**French Support Available**:
- Key phrases provided in every lesson plan
- Visual vocabulary cards prepared
- Students help with pronunciation
- English responses are fine when needed

**Adaptation Options**:
- **High Energy**: Add movement (stand up, sit down, wiggle fingers)
- **Normal Energy**: Follow lesson plan as written
- **Low Energy**: Keep to 5 minutes, use calm voice

### 🔬 **EXPLORE (15-25 minutes) - Hands-On Discovery**

**THE MAGIC**: Every lesson has TWO parts:

#### **CORE INVESTIGATION (Always 12-15 minutes)**
- **ALL students do this part** - it's the essential learning
- Materials are prepared and labeled "CORE"
- Step-by-step instructions provided
- Safety protocols clearly posted

#### **EXTENSION OPTIONS (0-12 minutes based on student energy)**
- **Use your judgment** based on how students are doing
- Materials labeled "EXTENSION A, B, C"
- Choose what feels right for your students today

**Decision Making Made Easy**:

🔥 **Students are excited and focused**: Use Extension A + B  
📊 **Students are working normally**: Use Extension A only  
📉 **Students seem tired/unfocused**: Core investigation only  

**Your Role During Explore**:
- Circulate and encourage discoveries
- Remind about safety when needed: "Gentle hands, scientists!"
- Ask simple questions: "What do you notice?" "How does it feel?"
- Take 1-2 photos if possible (phone is fine)

### 💬 **SHARE (5-10 minutes) - Celebrating Discoveries**

**Your Role**: Help students reflect and connect
- Bring students back to circle
- Ask reflection questions provided in lesson plan
- Help with science journal entry (drawing + simple words)
- Clean up together

**Science Journal Structure** (works for any lesson):
```
Today I discovered: [Drawing + 1-2 words]
I noticed: [Simple drawing or words]  
French words I tried: [Any vocabulary attempts]
```

**What Counts as Success**:
- Every student participates in some way
- Students share at least one discovery
- Materials are cleaned up safely
- Students feel proud of their learning

## 🚨 FOOLPROOF EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

### 🎯 **Universal Backup Lesson** (If all else fails)

**Use this for ANY science unit - it always works:**

**Wonder (5 min)**: "What do scientists notice about the world around them?"

**Explore (15 min)**: 
- Look out the window and observe (weather, trees, birds, etc.)
- OR observe something in the classroom (plants, materials, students' clothing)
- Students work in pairs to notice 3 things

**Share (10 min)**:
- Students share one thing they observed
- Draw their favorite observation in science journal
- Practice saying observations in French if they want to try

**Materials Needed**: Paper, pencils, windows or classroom objects
**Safety**: Just gentle observation, no materials needed
**Success Guaranteed**: Students love to observe and share discoveries

### 🛠️ **When Materials Are Missing**

**Red Emergency Bin Contains**:
- Backup materials for common investigations
- Simple observation tools (magnifying glasses, sorting trays)
- Emergency activity cards for each unit
- "No Materials Needed" activity options

**Quick Adaptations**:
- Outdoor lesson becomes window observation
- Sorting activity uses classroom objects
- Investigation becomes demonstration with discussion
- Hands-on becomes "predict and discuss"

### 🌧️ **Weather Complications**

**Rainy Day**: All outdoor lessons have indoor alternatives provided
**Snow Day Energy**: Use movement-based extensions to channel energy
**Hot Day Sluggishness**: Focus on core only, add calm music
**Windy Day Excitement**: Extra emphasis on safety and staying together

### 👥 **Classroom Management Support**

**If Students Are Wiggly**:
- Add movement to Wonder time: "Show me excited scientist faces!"
- Use shorter investigation time with more changes
- Give everyone a specific job during Explore time
- Extra safety reminders: "Scientists use calm hands"

**If Students Are Too Quiet**:
- Use partner work instead of individual
- Ask easier questions: "Point to something you notice"
- Celebrate any participation enthusiastically
- Allow extra time for sharing

**If Students Are Off-Task**:
- Stop and review what scientists do: "Scientists observe carefully"
- Assign specific partnerships
- Use more adult guidance during investigations
- Shorten to core activities only
