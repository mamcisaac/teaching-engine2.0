## 🔢 CONVERSION FORMULAS

**Always use these formulas:**
- **Lessons to Hours:** Lessons × 45 minutes ÷ 60 = Hours
- **Hours to Lessons:** Hours × 60 ÷ 45 = Lessons
- **Daily to Annual:** Lessons per day × 195 days = Annual lessons

**Examples:**
- French Language Arts: 1 lesson/day × 195 days = 195 lessons = 146.25 hours
- Social Studies: 0.5 lessons/day × 195 days = 97.5 lessons ≈ 97 lessons = 72.75 hours
