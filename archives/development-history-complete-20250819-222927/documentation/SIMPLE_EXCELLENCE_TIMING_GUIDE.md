# ⏰ SIMPLE EXCELLENCE TIMING GUIDE
## Wonder-Explore-Share Structure - Flexible Timing for Real Classrooms

**Purpose**: This guide helps Emily adapt every Wonder-Explore-Share lesson to real classroom conditions while maintaining learning objectives and student engagement. **Flexibility is built-in, not added on.**

---

## 🎯 THE SIMPLE EXCELLENCE TIMING FRAMEWORK

**Base Structure** (works for any day):
- **Wonder**: 5-8 minutes (curiosity activation)
- **Explore**: 15-25 minutes (core + optional extensions)  
- **Share**: 5-10 minutes (reflection and connection)

**Total Time Range**: 30-45 minutes (responsive to student needs)

**Golden Rule**: **Student engagement and safety guide timing, not the clock.**

---

## 📊 DAILY TIMING DECISION TREE

### 🌟 **STEP 1: Check Student Energy** (30 seconds before lesson)

**Look for**:
- How are students sitting/moving?
- What's their focus level right now?
- Any special circumstances today (weather, events, etc.)?

**This tells you which timing pattern to use:**

---

## 🔥 HIGH ENERGY TIMING - "They're Ready to Dive Deep!"

### **Recognition Signs:**
- Students asking questions before you start
- High focus and attention during transitions
- "What are we discovering today?" energy
- Recently returned from active time (gym, recess)
- Beautiful weather creating excitement

### **Wonder Time (6-10 minutes)**
- **Start Strong**: Jump right into wonder question
- **Let Discussion Flow**: If students are engaged, let them share more
- **Add Movement**: Stand up for wonder question, use gestures
- **Build Anticipation**: "I can see you're excited to explore!"

### **Explore Time (20-30 minutes)**
- **Core Investigation**: 15 minutes as planned
- **Use ALL Extensions**: Add Extension A, B, and even C if available
- **Follow Student Lead**: If they discover something unexpected, explore it
- **Challenge Opportunities**: Provide advanced materials for ready learners
- **Let Learning Run**: Can borrow 5 minutes from other subjects if pre-approved

### **Share Time (8-12 minutes)**
- **Rich Reflection**: Ask deeper questions about discoveries
- **Peer Teaching**: Students share discoveries with each other
- **Connection Making**: Link to other learning or real life
- **Extended Journal Time**: More detailed drawings and words
- **Preview Excitement**: Build anticipation for tomorrow's learning

**Total Time**: 35-50 minutes (may run over with approval)

---

## 📊 NORMAL ENERGY TIMING - "Steady Learning Mode"

### **Recognition Signs:**
- Students following directions appropriately
- Normal focus and participation levels
- Typical post-meal or mid-morning energy
- Standard weather and day conditions
- No special events or disruptions

### **Wonder Time (7-8 minutes)**
- **Follow Plan Exactly**: Use lesson plan timing as written
- **Standard Sharing**: 3-4 students share ideas
- **Clear Learning Goal**: State objective clearly and simply
- **Vocabulary Introduction**: Show French words as planned

### **Explore Time (20-25 minutes)**
- **Core Investigation**: Full 15 minutes as designed
- **Choose 1-2 Extensions**: Based on how core investigation goes
- **Standard Circulation**: Visit all students/groups once or twice
- **Photo Documentation**: Take 1-2 photos of learning
- **Materials Management**: Keep to planned materials and setup

### **Share Time (8-10 minutes)**
- **Circle Sharing**: Standard reflection questions as written
- **Science Journal**: Drawing + 1-2 words as typical
- **Vocabulary Practice**: Review key French words
- **Clean-up Routine**: Students follow established cleanup procedures
- **Tomorrow Preview**: Brief mention of next day's learning

**Total Time**: 35-43 minutes (right on target)

---

## 📉 LOW ENERGY TIMING - "Gentle Support Mode"

### **Recognition Signs:**
- Students seem tired or sluggish
- Difficulty focusing during transitions
- Post-lunch energy dip
- Challenging weather affecting mood
- After difficult or emotional events
- Monday morning or Friday afternoon energy

### **Wonder Time (4-6 minutes)**
- **Calm Start**: Lower voice, slower pace
- **Visual Support**: Use more pictures and concrete objects
- **Shorter Discussion**: Just 1-2 students share briefly
- **Gentle Movement**: Simple stretches or finger movements
- **Clear, Simple Goal**: One main learning objective only

### **Explore Time (12-18 minutes)**
- **Core Only**: Skip extensions, focus on essential learning
- **Smaller Groups**: Pairs instead of teams for more support
- **More Adult Guidance**: Circulate more frequently, provide more help
- **Simplified Materials**: Use easier versions of activities when available
- **Quiet Focus**: Encourage calm, focused exploration

### **Share Time (8-15 minutes)**
- **Extended Reflection**: Allow more processing time
- **Individual Work**: Science journals before group sharing
- **Calm Music**: Soft background music during journal time
- **Gentle Encouragement**: Focus on effort and participation
- **Longer Cleanup**: Use cleanup time for gentle transition

**Total Time**: 25-35 minutes (shorter but still complete learning)

---

## 🌤️ WEATHER-RESPONSIVE TIMING

### ☀️ **Beautiful Weather**
- **Wonder**: 6 minutes (build excitement for outdoor exploration)
- **Explore**: 25-30 minutes (use outdoor extensions when appropriate)
- **Share**: 8 minutes (may move sharing outside)

### 🌧️ **Rainy/Indoor Weather**
- **Wonder**: 8 minutes (acknowledge weather, adjust expectations)
- **Explore**: 20 minutes (indoor alternatives, window observations)
- **Share**: 10 minutes (cozy indoor reflection time)

### ❄️ **Snow Day Excitement**
- **Wonder**: 5 minutes (channel excitement into learning)
- **Explore**: 25 minutes (use high-energy extensions)
- **Share**: 8 minutes (acknowledge snow discoveries when relevant)

### 🌡️ **Very Hot Day**
- **Wonder**: 6 minutes (keep it calm and cool)
- **Explore**: 15-18 minutes (core only, avoid overheating)
- **Share**: 12 minutes (extended calm reflection time)

---

## 🎪 SPECIAL CIRCUMSTANCE TIMING

### 🎉 **Before Special Events** (Assembly, Field Trip, etc.)
- **Wonder**: 5 minutes (acknowledge excitement, channel into science)
- **Explore**: 15-20 minutes (high energy but shorter time)
- **Share**: 5 minutes (quick reflection before transition)

### 😴 **After Special Events**
- **Wonder**: 8 minutes (allow settling time, gentle start)
- **Explore**: 12-15 minutes (calm core activities only)
- **Share**: 12 minutes (extended reflection and processing time)

### 🤒 **High Absence Day**
- **Wonder**: 6 minutes (acknowledge who's missing, proceed gently)
- **Explore**: 18 minutes (individual work focus, document for absent students)
- **Share**: 10 minutes (share discoveries that absent students can hear about)

### 🔧 **Missing Materials/Equipment Issues**
- **Wonder**: 5 minutes (adjust expectations, problem-solve together)
- **Explore**: 10-15 minutes (demonstration + discussion focus)
- **Share**: 15 minutes (extended reflection makes up for shorter exploration)

## ⏰ TIME MANAGEMENT STRATEGIES

### **Running Short on Time**

**Priority Order**:
1. **Safety protocols** - never skip
2. **Core learning objective** - essential content
3. **Basic reflection** - quick circle sharing
4. **Science journal** - can move to next lesson
5. **Extensions** - save for another day

**Quick Adaptations**:
- Wonder: 4 minutes maximum
- Explore: Core investigation only (10-12 minutes)
- Share: Quick circle (5 minutes), journals tomorrow

### **Extra Time Available**

**Productive Uses**:
- Extend exploration if students are deeply engaged
- Add unexpected extension activity
- Deeper reflection and connection making
- Individual conferences with students
- Preview tomorrow's learning in detail
- Extra French vocabulary practice

**Avoid**:
- Rushing to fill time with busy work
- Adding activities without clear learning purpose
- Skipping reflection just because there's more time

### **Interrupted Lessons**

**Fire Drill/Emergency**:
- Note where you stopped in lesson plan
- Resume with shortened versions when returning
- Focus on safety reflection when appropriate

**Unexpected Visitor/Administrator**:
- Continue with lesson, invite observation
- Students can demonstrate their learning
- Use as opportunity to showcase Simple Excellence structure

**Technical Issues**:
- Move to backup materials immediately
- Use emergency backup lesson from red folder
- Focus on observation and discussion instead of tech

- "Scientists, we have 5 more minutes to explore!"
- "Two-minute warning for cleanup time!"
- Helps students transition smoothly

### **Mental Checkpoints**
- **15 minutes in**: How's student engagement? Adjust accordingly.
- **30 minutes in**: Are we on track for good reflection time?
- **5 minutes left**: Start gentle cleanup and transition cues

## 🎯 TIMING SUCCESS INDICATORS

### **You're Timing Well When**:
✅ Students are engaged throughout the lesson  
✅ No one feels rushed or left behind  
✅ Learning objectives are met regardless of total time  
✅ Transitions feel natural and calm  
✅ Students have time to reflect on discoveries  
✅ You feel in control rather than controlled by the clock  

### **Timing Red Flags** (adjust immediately):
⚠️ Students checking out or getting fidgety  
⚠️ Rushing through safety protocols  
⚠️ No time for student reflection  
⚠️ Materials feel chaotic or disorganized  
⚠️ You're watching the clock more than the students

## 💡 EMILY'S PERSONAL TIMING TIPS

### **Start of Year** (September-October):
- Lean toward longer Wonder times (8 minutes) to establish routines
- Keep Explore times consistent (20 minutes) to build expectations
- Extended Share times (10-12 minutes) for reflection habit building

### **Mid-Year** (November-February):
- Students know routine - can vary timing more confidently
- Use engagement levels as primary guide for timing decisions
- Trust your instincts about when students need more or less time

### **End of Year** (March-June):
- Students are timing experts - let them help guide decisions
- Can handle more complex timing variations
- Focus on celebration and connections in Share time

### **Daily Wisdom**:
- **Monday mornings**: Allow extra Wonder time for weekend brain re-entry
- **Friday afternoons**: Focus on core investigations, extended Share time
- **After lunch**: Gentle starts, be ready to adapt timing significantly
- **Before holidays**: High energy - use extensions, shorter reflection
