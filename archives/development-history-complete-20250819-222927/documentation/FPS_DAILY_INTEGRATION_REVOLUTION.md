## 📚 7 REVOLUTIONARY UNITS (98 LESSONS TOTAL)

### Unit 1: Moi et ma santé (Me and My Health) - 14 lessons
**Timeline:** September-October (every other day)  
**Focus:** Personal hygiene, self-care basics, body awareness in French  
**Vocabulary:** santé, hygiène, corps, soins, propre, brossage, dents, mains  
**Expectations:** FPS1 (personal health), FPS4 (personal competencies)

### Unit 2: Sécurité et protection (Safety and Protection) - 14 lessons  
**Timeline:** October-November (every other day)  
**Focus:** Body safety, environmental safety, trusted adults  
**Vocabulary:** sécurité, protection, confiance, adulte, danger, sûr, aide  
**Expectations:** FPS2 (safe practices), FPS3 (healthy relationships)

### Unit 3: Émotions et relations (Emotions and Relationships) - 14 lessons
**Timeline:** November-December (every other day)  
**Focus:** Emotional regulation, healthy relationships, social skills  
**Vocabulary:** émotions, sentiments, amitié, respect, partage, écoute  
**Expectations:** FPS3 (healthy relationships), FPS4 (personal competencies)

### Unit 4: Nutrition et énergie (Nutrition and Energy) - 14 lessons
**Timeline:** January-February (every other day)  
**Focus:** Healthy eating, nutrition basics, energy for learning  
**Vocabulary:** nutrition, aliments, énergie, légumes, fruits, équilibré  
**Expectations:** FPS1 (personal health), FPS4 (personal competencies)

### Unit 5: Mouvement et bien-être (Movement and Wellness) - 14 lessons
**Timeline:** February-March (every other day)  
**Focus:** Physical activity, movement, mental wellness connection  
**Vocabulary:** mouvement, exercice, bien-être, corps, activité, cœur  
**Expectations:** FPS1 (personal health), FPS4 (personal competencies)

### Unit 6: Communauté et sécurité (Community and Safety) - 14 lessons
**Timeline:** March-April (every other day)  
**Focus:** Digital safety, community helpers, environmental safety  
**Vocabulary:** communauté, aide, environnement, sécurité, numérique  
**Expectations:** FPS2 (safe practices), FPS3 (healthy relationships)

### Unit 7: Croissance et célébration (Growth and Celebration) - 14 lessons
**Timeline:** May-June (every other day)  
**Focus:** Personal growth, celebrating learning, summer safety  
**Vocabulary:** croissance, progrès, célébration, été, sécurité, fierté  
**Expectations:** All 4 FPS expectations integrated
