## 📋 LESSON 1: "Premier jour - Our First Science Adventure"
**Date**: September 2, 2025  
**Learning Goal**: Students will explore their science classroom safely and begin developing observation skills.

### **MINDS ON (8 minutes) - 1:00-1:08**
**Wonder Moment**: "Bienvenue à notre laboratoire de science!"
- Circle time in science area
- Show science journal: "This is where we record our discoveries"
- Safety first: "How do we explore safely?" (brief discussion)
- **Learning Goal**: "Today we become scientists who observe and explore safely"
- **Activation**: "What do you already know about being safe at school?"

**Materials**: Science journals, safety poster, circle time carpet

### **ACTION (27 minutes) - 1:08-1:35**

#### **CORE INVESTIGATION (15 minutes) - ALL STUDENTS**
**"Safe Science Classroom Exploration"**
- Partner walk around science area (assigned partnerships)
- **Task**: Find 3 things that help us stay safe (hand washing station, safety poster, first aid kit)
- **Vocabulary**: "sécurité" (safety), "observer" (observe), "découvrir" (discover)
- **Documentation**: Draw one safety item in science journal

**Safety Protocol**: Stay with partner, walk (don't run), gentle hands only, ask before touching

#### **EXTENSION OPTIONS (12 minutes) - IF HIGH ENGAGEMENT**
**Option A**: Detailed safety item investigation
- Choose one safety item to examine closely
- Predict why it helps us stay safe
- Share findings with another partnership

**Option B**: Living vs non-living introduction
- Find 2 living things and 2 non-living things in classroom
- Begin classification discussion

#### **ADAPTATION OPTIONS - IF LOW ENERGY**
- Shorter exploration (10 minutes)
- Adult-guided small groups
- Focus only on hand washing station and safety poster

**Materials**: Science journals, pencils, safety item identification cards, magnifying glasses (extension)

### **CONSOLIDATION (10 minutes) - 1:35-1:45**
**Reflection Circle**: "Qu'est-ce que nous avons découvert?"
- Quick share: "One thing that helps us stay safe"
- Science journal: Draw and label (with help) one discovery
- **Assessment**: Observation notes on safety understanding
- **Preview**: "Tomorrow we explore our playground safely"
- **Cleanup**: Put materials away safely (practice routine)

**Assessment Notes for Emily**:
- Who identified safety items correctly?
- Who followed safety protocols?
- Who used French vocabulary?
- Any concerns about safety understanding?

## 📋 LESSON 5: "Matériaux de notre cour - Playground Materials Investigation"
**Date**: September 8, 2025  
**Learning Goal**: Students will classify playground materials and understand their properties.

### **MINDS ON (8 minutes) - 1:00-1:08**
**Wonder Moment**: Review yesterday's playground safety discoveries
- Circle time: "Yesterday we learned playground safety rules. What did we discover?"
- Show material samples: metal swing chain, plastic slide piece, wooden bench piece, fabric from equipment
- **Question**: "Why do you think different materials are used for different playground equipment?"
- **Learning Goal**: "Today we discover how playground materials are different and why they're chosen"

**Materials**: Material samples in basket, chart paper, markers

### **ACTION (27 minutes) - 1:08-1:35**

#### **CORE INVESTIGATION (15 minutes) - ALL STUDENTS**
**"Playground Material Classification"**
- Teams of 3 with adult supervision
- **Task**: Sort collected playground materials into groups: métal, plastique, bois, tissu
- **Process**: 
  1. Touch and observe each material (safely)
  2. Place in correct sorting mat
  3. Discuss with team: "Why did you put it there?"
- **Vocabulary**: "dur" (hard), "mou" (soft), "lisse" (smooth), "rugueux" (rough)

**Safety Protocol**: No putting materials in mouth, gentle handling, wash hands after

#### **EXTENSION OPTIONS (12 minutes) - IF HIGH ENGAGEMENT**
**Option A**: Property testing
- Test materials for hardness, texture, temperature
- Record findings with simple drawings
- Predict which would work best for different playground uses

**Option B**: Sound investigation
- Gently tap materials with wooden stick
- Listen to different sounds they make
- Connect sound to material type

**Option C**: Problem-solving challenge
- "If you were building new playground equipment, which material would you choose for a slide? Why?"

#### **ADAPTATION OPTIONS - IF LOW ENERGY/WEATHER**
- Indoor version with smaller material samples
- Adult-led demonstration with whole group participation
- Focus on just 2 material types (metal vs plastic)

**Materials**: Collected playground materials, sorting mats labeled in French, recording sheets, wooden sticks for sound testing, hand sanitizer

### **CONSOLIDATION (10 minutes) - 1:35-1:45**
**Discovery Documentation**
- Teams share one interesting discovery: "We learned that..."
- Individual science journal: Draw one material and write its name in French (with support)
- **Assessment Question**: "Which material do you think is best for swings? Why?"
- **Preview**: "Tomorrow we test which materials are strongest"
- **Safety Review**: "What safety rules did we follow today?"

**Assessment Notes for Emily**:
- Accuracy of material classification
- Use of French vocabulary in discussion
- Following of safety protocols
- Evidence of scientific thinking in explanations

## 📋 LESSON 10: "Êtres vivants vs non-vivants - Living vs Non-Living Classification"
**Date**: September 15, 2025  
**Learning Goal**: Students will accurately classify school environment objects as living or non-living and explain their reasoning.

### **MINDS ON (8 minutes) - 1:00-1:08**
**Wonder Moment**: Mystery box investigation
- Present mystery box with living thing (plant) and non-living thing (rock)
- **Questions**: "What's inside? How could we find out? What might make them different?"
- Review previous vocabulary: "vivant" and "non-vivant"
- **Learning Goal**: "Today we become expert classifiers of living and non-living things"
- **Activation**: "How do we know if something is alive?"

**Materials**: Mystery box, small plant, interesting rock, classification chart from previous lessons

### **ACTION (27 minutes) - 1:08-1:35**

#### **CORE INVESTIGATION (15 minutes) - ALL STUDENTS**
**"School Environment Classification Challenge"**
- **Phase 1** (8 minutes): Collection walk around school grounds
  - Pairs collect 4 objects: 2 living, 2 non-living
  - Use collection bags provided
  - Follow safety rules: ask permission, gentle handling, no disturbing animals
  
- **Phase 2** (7 minutes): Classification activity
  - Sort collected objects on classification mat
  - Explain reasoning to partner: "This is living because..."
  - Record findings with simple drawings

**Safety Protocol**: Stay with partner, ask teacher before picking up anything, gentle hands, no disturbing wildlife

#### **EXTENSION OPTIONS (12 minutes) - IF HIGH ENGAGEMENT**
**Option A**: Advanced classification criteria
- Develop class list of "What makes something living?"
- Test borderline cases (seeds, dead leaves, etc.)
- Create detailed explanation drawings

**Option B**: Living thing care investigation
- Focus on found living things
- Discuss: "What do these living things need to survive?"
- Plan how to care for them responsibly

**Option C**: Scientific debate
- Present challenging cases for class discussion
- Use evidence to support classification decisions
- Practice scientific reasoning language

#### **ADAPTATION OPTIONS - IF LOW ENERGY/WEATHER**
- Indoor classification with pre-selected classroom objects
- Teacher-led whole group investigation
- Focus on obvious examples only (plant vs book)

**Materials**: Collection bags, classification mats (vivant/non-vivant), clipboards, pencils, hand sanitizer, outdoor safety equipment

### **CONSOLIDATION (10 minutes) - 1:35-1:45**
**Scientific Reasoning Circle**
- Teams present one interesting classification decision
- **Challenge Question**: "What if we found something that used to be living but isn't anymore?"
- Science journal: Draw and label one living and one non-living discovery
- **Assessment**: Quick check - hold up objects and students classify with thumbs up (living) or thumbs down (non-living)
- **Preview**: "Tomorrow we investigate how humans impact living things"
- **Care Protocol**: Properly return/care for any living things collected

**Assessment Notes for Emily**:
- Accuracy of living/non-living classifications
- Quality of reasoning and explanations
- Appropriate care and respect for living things
- French vocabulary usage
- Scientific thinking development

## 📋 LESSON 19: "Célébration de nos découvertes - Celebrating Our Discoveries"
**Date**: September 26, 2025  
**Learning Goal**: Students will demonstrate their learning about school environment safety and scientific observation skills.

### **MINDS ON (8 minutes) - 1:00-1:08**
**Wonder Moment**: Journey reflection
- Display collection of class discoveries from past 3 weeks
- **Question**: "How have we become better scientists?"
- Look through science journals together
- **Learning Goal**: "Today we celebrate what we've learned and share our scientific discoveries"
- **Activation**: "What's your favorite discovery from our investigations?"

**Materials**: Display of unit discoveries, science journals, digital photos from investigations

### **ACTION (27 minutes) - 1:08-1:35**

#### **CORE INVESTIGATION (15 minutes) - ALL STUDENTS**
**"Science Discovery Fair Setup"**
- **Station 1** (5 min): Safety demonstration practice
  - Students practice demonstrating 3 key safety procedures
  - Partners help each other perfect demonstrations
  
- **Station 2** (5 min): Living/non-living expertise sharing
  - Create display of best classification examples
  - Practice explaining reasoning in French
  
- **Station 3** (5 min): French vocabulary celebration
  - Review and practice 3 key vocabulary words with actions
  - Prepare to teach vocabulary to visitors

**Safety Protocol**: Review all safety procedures one final time, demonstrate proper care for any living things

#### **EXTENSION OPTIONS (12 minutes) - IF HIGH ENGAGEMENT**
**Option A**: Visitor teaching preparation
- Practice teaching safety procedures to younger students
- Prepare demonstration scripts
- Set up interactive stations

**Option B**: Scientific reflection project
- Create individual "My Science Journey" books
- Include drawings, photos, discoveries
- Prepare presentation for families

**Option C**: Problem-solving showcase
- Present solutions to school environment challenges discovered
- Share ideas with school administration
- Plan future investigations

#### **ADAPTATION OPTIONS - IF LOW ENERGY**
- Simple sharing circle format
- Gallery walk of science journals
- Individual one-on-one sharing with teacher

**Materials**: Science journals, discovery displays, demonstration materials, craft supplies for celebration, visitor chairs

### **CONSOLIDATION (10 minutes) - 1:35-1:45**
**Celebration and Transition**
- **Celebration Circle**: Each student shares one word describing their favorite discovery
- **Unit Assessment**: Teacher observation of safety demonstrations and vocabulary usage
- **Science Journal Completion**: Final entry with unit reflection drawing
- **Preview Next Unit**: "Next week we explore autumn changes - what do you think we might discover?"
- **Appreciation**: Thank you for being safe, curious scientists!

**Assessment Notes for Emily**:
- Demonstration of 3 safety procedures
- Accurate use of 3 French vocabulary words
- Evidence of scientific thinking growth
- Classification accuracy for living/non-living
- Readiness for next unit's challenges

## 🎯 RESPONSIVE FLEXIBILITY IN ACTION

### **High Engagement Day Example**
When students are highly engaged in Lesson 5 material testing:
- Extend Action phase to 30 minutes
- Use all extension options
- Shorten Consolidation to 7 minutes with quick sharing
- Continue investigation tomorrow if needed

### **Low Energy Day Example** 
When students are tired during Lesson 10:
- Shorten collection walk to 5 minutes
- Adult-guided whole group classification
- Extend Consolidation to 12 minutes with calmer reflection
- Save extension activities for another day

### **Weather Alternative Example**
Rainy day during outdoor lessons:
- Pre-planned indoor material collection available
- Virtual nature walk videos for observation practice
- Classroom-based living/non-living classification
- Maintain learning objectives with different methods

### **Substitute Teacher Support**
Each lesson includes:
- ✅ Clear timing for each phase
- ✅ All materials listed with location
- ✅ Safety protocols prominent
- ✅ Extension and adaptation options clear
- ✅ Assessment notes template
- ✅ Emergency lesson plan backup

- **Friday**: Reflection, consolidation, and preview

- Living/non-living classification accuracy
- French vocabulary usage in context
