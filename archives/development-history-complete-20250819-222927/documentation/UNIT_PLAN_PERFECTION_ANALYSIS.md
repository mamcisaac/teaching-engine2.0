## 📋 SPECIFIC UNIT-LEVEL IMPERFECTIONS

### **1. 📅 RIGID TIMING STRUCTURE**

**FLAW**: All units locked to exactly 14 lessons
- **Problem**: Real classrooms need flexibility
- **Reality**: Some health topics need 10 lessons, others need 18
- **Missing**: Buffer time for slower learners
- **Issue**: No accommodation for holidays/snow days
- **Gap**: No guidance for accelerated/remedial pacing

**PERFECTION REQUIRES**:
- ✅ Flexible timing (12-16 lesson range)
- ✅ Adaptation guidance for different paces
- ✅ Holiday/calendar adjustment protocols
- ✅ Optional extension/consolidation activities

### **2. 📖 SHALLOW CONTENT DESCRIPTIONS**

**FLAW**: High-level unit descriptions without depth
- **Problem**: Teachers can't understand what to actually teach
- **Example**: "Students explore emotions" vs "Students identify 8 specific emotions using concrete examples"
- **Missing**: Detailed learning outcome sequences
- **Gap**: No complexity indicators for different lessons

**PERFECTION REQUIRES**:
- ✅ Specific, measurable learning objectives
- ✅ Detailed content breakdowns by lesson cluster
- ✅ Complexity progression indicators
- ✅ Prerequisite knowledge identification

### **3. 🎯 VAGUE CURRICULUM EXPECTATION MAPPING**

**FLAW**: Expectations assigned to units but depth/timing unclear
- **Problem**: Which lessons address which parts of expectations?
- **Missing**: Explicit expectation coverage mapping
- **Gap**: No mastery progression indicators
- **Issue**: Assessment timing not aligned with expectation development

**PERFECTION REQUIRES**:
- ✅ Lesson-by-lesson expectation mapping
- ✅ Depth indicators (introduce/develop/consolidate)
- ✅ Mastery progression timelines
- ✅ Assessment alignment with expectation coverage

### **4. 🇫🇷 SURFACE-LEVEL FRENCH INTEGRATION**

**FLAW**: French vocabulary lists without authentic integration
- **Problem**: Lists of words without meaningful context
- **Example**: "vocabulaire: santé, corps, soins" vs detailed language development sequence
- **Missing**: Grammar/syntax development
- **Gap**: Cultural context integration
- **Issue**: No progression between units

**PERFECTION REQUIRES**:
- ✅ Authentic French context development
- ✅ Grammar/syntax progression
- ✅ Cultural connection authenticity
- ✅ Inter-unit language building sequence

### **5. 🔄 GENERIC DIFFERENTIATION STRATEGIES**

**FLAW**: Broad, non-specific differentiation approaches
- **Problem**: "Visual supports" vs "Emotion identification cards with photographs"
- **Missing**: Specific strategy descriptions

- ✅ Resource requirements for accommodations
- ✅ Success indicators for different learners

### **6. 📊 INCOMPLETE ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORKS**

**FLAW**: Assessment plans lack specificity and tools
- **Problem**: "Portfolio assessment" vs detailed rubrics and criteria

- ✅ Frequency and timing guidance

### **7. 🔗 MISSED CROSS-CURRICULAR OPPORTUNITIES**

**FLAW**: Limited connections to daily subjects
- **Problem**: Health taught in isolation vs integrated with Math/Science/Arts
