## ✅ ONLY ALLOWED APPROACH

### Manual Pedagogical Creation
Every lesson must be:
1. **Individually crafted** with specific pedagogical thought
2. **Contextually appropriate** for the specific students
3. **Manually entered** into the database
4. **Quality over quantity** - better 10 real lessons than 1000 templates

### Best Practices Required:
- ETFO three-part structure (properly implemented, not templated)
- Grade-appropriate content (thoughtfully designed)
- Real differentiation strategies (not copy-pasted)
- Authentic assessment approaches
- Meaningful cultural connections
