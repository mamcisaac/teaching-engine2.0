## 🎓 Best Practices Being Applied

Each agent is following these critical guidelines:

1. **Reading each lesson individually** to understand specific learning goals
2. **Creating unique differentiation** based on actual lesson content
3. **Writing observable assessments** that measure specific objectives
4. **Maintaining French language** throughout all content
5. **Ensuring Grade 1 appropriateness** (6-year-olds)
6. **Including safety protocols** where needed
7. **Being culturally sensitive** and trauma-informed
