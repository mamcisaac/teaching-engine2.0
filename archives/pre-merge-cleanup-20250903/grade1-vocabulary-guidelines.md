# Grade 1 French Immersion Vocabulary Guidelines

## Core Principles for 6-7 Year Olds

### 1. SIMPLICITY FIRST
- **Maximum 3-5 new words per lesson**
- Use 1-2 syllable words when possible
- Avoid compound words initially
- Choose concrete over abstract concepts

### 2. VISUAL & CONCRETE
- Words students can see, touch, or point to
- Body parts, classroom objects, animals
- Actions they can demonstrate
- Colors, shapes, numbers they can count

### 3. HIGH-FREQUENCY & USEFUL
- Words they'll use daily in French class
- Essential classroom survival vocabulary
- Common social interactions
- Basic needs and requests

### 4. PHONETICALLY SIMPLE
- Start with clear French sounds
- Avoid complex consonant clusters
- Use cognates (words similar to English)
- Build phonetic awareness gradually

## VOCABULARY CATEGORIES BY PRIORITY

### TIER 1: SURVIVAL FRENCH (Weeks 1-4)
**Essential for classroom functioning:**
- oui, non
- bonjour, au revoir
- merci, s'il vous plaît
- aide (help) - simplified from "aide-moi"
- moi, toi

### TIER 2: BASIC INTERACTIONS (Weeks 5-12)
**Daily communication:**
- comment, qui, quoi
- voici, voilà
- j'ai, tu as
- je veux, je peux
- ici, là

### TIER 3: CLASSROOM VOCABULARY (Ongoing)
**Objects and actions:**
- livre, crayon, papier
- chaise, table, porte
- écouter, regarder, dessiner
- rouge, bleu, vert (basic colors)
- un, deux, trois (basic numbers)

### TIER 4: PERSONAL VOCABULARY (Mid-year)
**About self and family:**
- maman, papa (not grand-maman/grand-papa initially)
- frère, sœur (only if relevant)
- nom, âge
- maison, école
- ami (not amie initially - keep it simple)

## VOCABULARY TO AVOID OR SIMPLIFY

### TOO ABSTRACT FOR GRADE 1:
❌ "communiquer nos pensées et nos sentiments"
✅ "parler français"

❌ "organiser notre monde"
✅ "compter nos jouets"

❌ "structures familiales"
✅ "ma famille"

### TOO COMPLEX PHONETICALLY:
❌ "grand-maman", "grand-papa"
✅ "mémère", "pépère" OR wait until later

❌ "aide-moi"
✅ "aide" (just the verb)

❌ "identifieront"
✅ "trouvent" or "voient"

### TOO MANY WORDS AT ONCE:
❌ 10 new vocabulary words per lesson
✅ 3-5 new words maximum

❌ Introducing numbers 1-20 in one unit
✅ Numbers 1-10 spread across multiple lessons

## IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGIES

### VOCABULARY INTRODUCTION PATTERN:
1. **Show** (visual/physical)
2. **Say** (clear pronunciation)
3. **Repeat** (class chorally)
4. **Use** (in simple context)
5. **Practice** (games/activities)

### REINFORCEMENT REQUIREMENTS:
- New words must appear in 3+ lessons
- Visual supports for every new word
- Kinesthetic actions when possible
- Review previous words before introducing new ones

### DIFFERENTIATION FOR VOCABULARY:
**For struggling learners:**
- Reduce to 2-3 new words per lesson
- Extra visual supports
- More repetition and practice time
- Peer buddy system

**For advanced learners:**
- Can handle 4-5 new words
- Introduce related vocabulary
- Encourage sentence creation
- Leadership roles in vocabulary games

## ASSESSMENT OF VOCABULARY LEARNING

### FORMATIVE INDICATORS:
- Student points to correct object when word is said
- Student can repeat word clearly
- Student uses word in simple phrase
- Student recognizes word in different context

### AVOID OVER-TESTING:
- No formal vocabulary quizzes in Grade 1
- Assessment through play and observation
- Focus on comprehension over production initially
- Celebrate attempts and approximations

## MONTHLY VOCABULARY GOALS

### September: 15-20 total words
Basic greetings, classroom survival, numbers 1-5

### October: 30-35 total words
Colors, body parts, simple actions, family basics

### November: 45-50 total words
Animals, food basics, more actions

### December: 60-65 total words
Holidays, weather words, clothing basics

### January-June: Continue building gradually
Never exceed 5 new words per week average

## EXAMPLES OF WELL-DESIGNED VOCABULARY LESSONS

### GOOD EXAMPLE - Colors Lesson:
**New words (3):** rouge, bleu, vert
**Context:** "Je vois du rouge" with real objects
**Practice:** Color sorting game
**Reinforcement:** Used in 3 follow-up lessons

### BAD EXAMPLE - Too Complex:
**Problem:** Introducing 8 new words about weather patterns
**Better:** Start with "soleil" and "pluie" only
**Add later:** More weather words one at a time

### GOOD EXAMPLE - Family Unit:
**Start with:** maman, papa (Week 1)
**Add next:** frère, sœur (Week 3, if relevant)
**Wait until later:** Extended family terms

This approach ensures vocabulary acquisition is sustainable, meaningful, and appropriate for 6-7 year old cognitive and linguistic development.
