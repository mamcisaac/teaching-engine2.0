## Pattern Analysis Across Unit

### ✅ Positive Patterns
1. **Intelligent Restraint**: Only 4 out of 20 lessons have decision points (20%)
2. **Mathematical Specificity**: Decision points reference concrete math concepts (counting, quantities, representations)
3. **Grade-Appropriate Language**: Uses simple "Si... alors..." structure suitable for teacher reference
4. **Progression-Aware**: Later lessons have different types of decision points than early lessons

### ⚠️ Areas for Improvement
1. **Structural Variety**: Consider varying the opening phrases beyond "Si l'élève..."
2. **Material Specificity**: Some lessons could benefit from more unique material suggestions
3. **Cross-Lesson Connections**: Decision points could occasionally reference earlier lesson concepts

## Required Fixes

### Minor Improvements
1. **Vary Decision Point Opening Phrases**: Consider alternatives to "Si l'élève..." such as:
   - "Quand les élèves..."
   - "Si vous observez..."
   - "Dans le cas où..."

2. **Enhance Material Specificity**: Add 1-2 lesson-unique materials where appropriate:
   - Lesson on measuring: rulers, measuring tapes
   - Lesson on patterns: pattern blocks, stamps
