# Music Unit 1: Finding Our Beat - Detailed Implementation Plan

## Unit Overview
**Grade**: 1 French Immersion  
**Duration**: September 4-30, 2025 (4 weeks)  
**Total Hours**: 12 hours (24 × 30-minute lessons)  
**Frequency**: 6 lessons per week  
**Quality Goal**: Enhance from 82/100 to 95+/100  

## Enhanced Learning Goals
Students will:
- Connect heartbeat to musical beat and develop internal sense of rhythm
- Master steady beat through body percussion and movement
- Develop singing voice through French songs and echo exercises
- Explore body and simple instruments as musical tools
- Express creativity through movement and cultural integration

---

## Week 1: Heartbeat to Music Connection (Sept 4-10)

### Lesson 1: "Our Musical Hearts" (Sept 4)
**French Connection**: "Mon cœur musical"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Opening Circle (5 min)**: Sit quietly, place hand on heart, feel heartbeat
- **Heartbeat Exploration (10 min)**: 
  - Count heartbeats together: "Un, deux, trois, quatre..."
  - Walk to heartbeat rhythm around classroom
  - Compare resting vs. jumping heartbeats
- **Musical Connection (10 min)**:
  - Play calm music, match heartbeat to beat
  - "The heartbeat song": Simple French chant "Mon cœur fait boom, boom, boom"
- **Sharing & Reflection (5 min)**: Each child shares their heartbeat discovery

**Materials**: Stethoscope, calm background music, heartbeat drum
**Assessment**: Observe ability to feel and count heartbeat rhythm

### Lesson 2: "Beat Walking Adventures" (Sept 5)
**French Connection**: "Aventures de marche rythmée"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Warm-up (5 min)**: Review heartbeat song with movements
- **Beat Walking (15 min)**:
  - Walk around room to steady drumbeat
  - Vary tempos: slow like elephants, medium like people, fast like mice
  - French counting while walking: "Un, deux, trois, quatre"
  - Stop and freeze when music stops
- **Creative Walking (8 min)**:
  - Animal walks to beat: bear crawl, bunny hops, bird steps
  - Student leaders take turns controlling tempo
- **Cool Down (2 min)**: Return to heartbeat walking, gentle breathing

**Materials**: Frame drum, animal picture cards, space for movement
**Assessment**: Track ability to maintain steady beat while walking

### Lesson 3: "Echo Clapping Magic" (Sept 8)
**French Connection**: "Magie des applaudissements en écho"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Opening (3 min)**: Simple French greeting song with claps
- **Echo Foundation (12 min)**:
  - Teacher claps simple patterns, students echo
  - Start with 2 claps, build to 4-clap patterns
  - Use French rhythm words: "Ta-ta" (2 claps), "Ta-ta-ta-ta" (4 claps)
- **Partner Echo (10 min)**:
  - Pairs create and echo clap patterns
  - One leads, other echoes, then switch
- **Group Challenge (5 min)**: Whole class echo increasingly complex patterns

**Materials**: None needed (hands only), pattern cards for visual support
**Assessment**: Note accuracy in echoing rhythmic patterns

### Lesson 4: "Body Percussion Fun" (Sept 9)
**French Connection**: "Percussion corporelle amusante"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Body Discovery (8 min)**:
  - Explore sounds: clap hands, pat knees, stamp feet, tap shoulders
  - French vocabulary: "claquer" (clap), "taper" (tap), "frapper" (hit)
- **Simple Patterns (12 min)**:
  - Clap-pat pattern: clap hands, pat knees
  - Add stomps for emphasis
  - "Clap-pat-stomp" pattern in French rhythm
- **Body Orchestra (8 min)**:
  - Divide class into sections: clappers, patters, stompers
  - Create simple body percussion piece together
- **Sharing (2 min)**: Volunteers demonstrate their favorite body sound

**Materials**: Body percussion instruction cards with French labels
**Assessment**: Observe coordination and pattern following

### Lesson 5: "Beat Passing Circle" (Sept 10)
**French Connection**: "Cercle de passage du rythme"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Circle Setup (3 min)**: Form circle, review beat concepts
- **Simple Passing (10 min)**:
  - Pass imaginary beat around circle with claps
  - Each person adds one clap and passes to next
  - Use French counting: "Un" (clap), pass
- **Pattern Passing (12 min)**:
  - Pass clap-clap-pat pattern
  - Add variations: loud/soft (fort/doux)
  - Speed variations: lent (slow), rapide (fast)
- **Creative Additions (5 min)**: Students add personal flair to their beat contribution

**Materials**: Circle formation space, optional rhythm sticks
**Assessment**: Track ability to maintain beat continuity in group setting

### Lesson 6: "Week 1 Beat Celebration" (Sept 11)
**French Connection**: "Célébration du rythme de la semaine 1"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Review Medley (10 min)**: Combine all week's activities in sequence
- **Student Choices (15 min)**:
  - Students vote on favorite beat activity to repeat
  - Small groups demonstrate different beat concepts
- **Celebration Circle (5 min)**: Share one thing learned about beat this week

**Materials**: All materials from week, celebration music
**Assessment**: Self-reflection on beat understanding and favorite activities

## Week 2: Singing Voice Development (Sept 15-19)

### Lesson 7: "Welcome Songs in French" (Sept 15)
**French Connection**: "Chansons de bienvenue en français"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Vocal Warm-up (5 min)**:
  - Breathing exercises: "Respirez profondément"
  - Lip trills and humming
- **Welcome Song Learning (20 min)**:
  - "Bonjour mes amis" (Hello my friends) - simple melody
  - "Bienvenue à l'école" (Welcome to school)
  - Teach phrase by phrase with hand gestures
- **Practice & Performance (5 min)**: Sing for each other in small groups

**Materials**: Song sheets with pictures, simple melody instruments
**Assessment**: Observe pitch exploration and French pronunciation

### Lesson 8: "Echo Singing Adventures" (Sept 16)
**French Connection**: "Aventures de chant en écho"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Voice Exploration (7 min)**:
  - High like birds: "Cui-cui" sounds
  - Low like bears: "Gro-gro" sounds
  - French animal sounds: "Miaou" (cat), "Ouah-ouah" (dog)
- **Echo Singing (15 min)**:
  - Teacher sings short phrases, students echo
  - Use French vowel sounds: "La-la-la", "Do-ré-mi"
  - Simple French words: "Oui" (yes), "Non" (no), "Merci" (thank you)
- **Creative Echo (8 min)**: Students create their own short phrases for others to echo

**Materials**: Pitch pipe or keyboard for reference, echo song cards
**Assessment**: Note pitch matching ability and vocal confidence

### Lesson 9: "High and Low Voice Exploration" (Sept 17)
**French Connection**: "Exploration des voix aiguës et graves"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Physical Movement (8 min)**:
  - High sounds = reach up, stand on tiptoes
  - Low sounds = crouch down, touch floor
  - Practice with French words: "Haut" (high), "Bas" (low)
- **Voice Games (12 min)**:
  - Elevator voice: start low, go high, come back down
  - Animal voices: mouse (high) vs. cow (low)
  - French counting at different pitches
- **Song Application (10 min)**:
  - Simple song with high and low parts
  - "Sur le pont d'Avignon" with pitch variations

**Materials**: Visual pitch cards, scarves for movement, simple keyboard
**Assessment**: Track understanding of pitch concepts and vocal range exploration

### Lesson 10: "Breathing for Singing" (Sept 18)
**French Connection**: "Respiration pour chanter"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Breathing Basics (8 min)**:
  - Hand on chest vs. belly breathing
  - "Smell the flower, blow out the candle" exercise
  - French breathing words: "Inspirez" (breathe in), "Expirez" (breathe out)
- **Singing Breath (12 min)**:
  - Sustained "Ah" sounds with good breath support
  - French phrase singing with proper breathing
  - Practice breathing between song phrases
- **Fun Breathing Games (10 min)**:
  - Balloon breath: imagine filling a balloon in your belly
  - Snake breath: long "Sssss" sounds
  - Singing with better breath support

**Materials**: Visual breathing guides, balloons as props, calm music
**Assessment**: Observe improved breath control and longer phrases

### Lesson 11: "French Song Repertoire" (Sept 19)
**French Connection**: "Répertoire de chansons françaises"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Song Review (5 min)**: Practice week's learned songs
- **New Song Learning (15 min)**:
  - "Frère Jacques" with actions
  - "Alouette" (simple version)
  - Focus on clear French pronunciation
- **Song Games (10 min)**:
  - Musical chairs with French songs
  - Stop and sing: freeze dance with singing instead of stopping

**Materials**: French song books, action cards, recorded versions for reference
**Assessment**: Evaluate French pronunciation and melody retention

### Lesson 12: "Voice Week Showcase" (Sept 22)
**French Connection**: "Vitrine de la semaine vocale"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Warm-up Medley (5 min)**: Quick review of all vocal exercises
- **Performance Preparation (10 min)**: Choose and practice favorite songs
- **Mini Showcase (15 min)**:
  - Each student or pair performs part of a French song
  - Audience appreciation in French: "Bravo!", "Magnifique!"

**Materials**: Simple stage area, props for songs, appreciation cards
**Assessment**: Celebrate vocal growth and confidence development

## Week 3: Instrument Exploration (Sept 23-29)

### Lesson 13: "Body as First Instrument" (Sept 23)
**French Connection**: "Le corps comme premier instrument"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Body Sound Discovery (10 min)**:
  - Explore all body percussion possibilities
  - French vocabulary: "corps" (body), "instrument", "musique"
  - Create body sound map: head to toe exploration
- **Rhythm Patterns (15 min)**:
  - Simple body percussion patterns
  - Layer different body sounds
  - French rhythm counting with body percussion
- **Body Orchestra (5 min)**: Divide class into body instrument sections

**Materials**: Body percussion notation cards, mirror for demonstration
**Assessment**: Note creativity in body sound exploration

### Lesson 14: "Simple Percussion Introduction" (Sept 24)
**French Connection**: "Introduction aux percussions simples"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Instrument Meet & Greet (10 min)**:
  - Introduce each instrument by name in French
  - "Tambour" (drum), "Maracas", "Triangle", "Bâtons rythmiques" (rhythm sticks)
- **Proper Handling (10 min)**:
  - Demonstrate correct holding and playing techniques
  - Practice "gentle hands" and instrument care
- **First Playing (10 min)**:
  - Simple rhythm patterns on each instrument
  - Pass instruments safely around circle

**Materials**: Set of classroom percussion instruments, care instruction cards
**Assessment**: Observe instrument handling skills and respect

### Lesson 15: "Rhythm Stick Activities" (Sept 25)
**French Connection**: "Activités avec bâtons rythmiques"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Stick Safety (5 min)**: How to hold and use rhythm sticks safely
- **Basic Patterns (15 min)**:
  - Tap together, tap floor, tap neighbor's sticks (gently)
  - French counting with stick taps
  - Simple songs with stick accompaniment
- **Stick Orchestra (10 min)**:
  - Create class piece using only rhythm sticks
  - Different sections play different patterns

**Materials**: Rhythm sticks (2 per student), pattern cards, French counting chart
**Assessment**: Track rhythm accuracy and safety compliance

### Lesson 16: "Classroom Instrument Rules" (Sept 26)
**French Connection**: "Règles des instruments de classe"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Rule Creation (10 min)**:
  - Discuss why we need instrument rules
  - Create class rules together in French and English
- **Practice Rules (15 min)**:
  - Role-play proper instrument use
  - Practice getting, using, and putting away instruments
- **Rule Games (5 min)**: Quiz games about instrument care and rules

**Materials**: Rule chart paper, instrument pictures, visual rule cards
**Assessment**: Understanding and application of instrument care rules

### Lesson 17: "Sound Exploration Games" (Sept 29)
**French Connection**: "Jeux d'exploration sonore"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Mystery Sounds (10 min)**:
  - Hidden instrument game: guess the instrument
  - French vocabulary practice with instrument names
- **Sound Matching (10 min)**:
  - Match instrument sounds to pictures
  - Create sound families: shakers, drums, bells
- **Sound Story (10 min)**:
  - Create simple story with instrument sound effects
  - Each student adds sound to group story

**Materials**: Instruments, blindfold for mystery game, story picture cards
**Assessment**: Note sound discrimination and creative application

### Lesson 18: "Instrument Week Orchestra" (Sept 30)
**French Connection**: "Orchestre de la semaine des instruments"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Warm-up (5 min)**: Review all instruments and techniques
- **Orchestra Setup (10 min)**:
  - Assign instruments and parts
  - Practice conducting signals in French: "Commencez" (begin), "Arrêtez" (stop)
- **Performance (15 min)**:
  - Perform simple piece using all instruments learned
  - Student conductors take turns leading

**Materials**: All instruments, conductor's baton, simple orchestral arrangements
**Assessment**: Collaborative music-making and instrument proficiency

## Week 4: Movement and Cultural Integration (Oct 1-4)

### Lesson 19: "Creative Movement to Beat" (Oct 1)
**French Connection**: "Mouvement créatif au rythme"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Free Movement Exploration (8 min)**:
  - Move however the music makes you feel
  - French movement words: "danser" (dance), "bouger" (move), "tourner" (turn)
- **Beat Movement (15 min)**:
  - Move specifically to the beat
  - Create movements that show the beat clearly
  - Partner movement mirroring
- **Movement Creation (7 min)**: Students create signature moves to share

**Materials**: Varied music playlist, scarves/ribbons, movement space
**Assessment**: Observe beat awareness through movement

### Lesson 20: "Freeze Dance Fun" (Oct 2)
**French Connection**: "Danse figée amusante"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Traditional Freeze Dance (10 min)**:
  - Stop moving when music stops
  - French commands: "Dansez!" (dance), "Arrêtez!" (stop)
- **Musical Statue Variations (15 min)**:
  - Freeze in different poses: animals, shapes, emotions
  - Freeze to different types of music: fast, slow, classical, folk
- **Student Leaders (5 min)**: Students take turns controlling music and calling commands

**Materials**: Music system, various music styles, freeze dance cards
**Assessment**: Following musical cues and creative expression

### Lesson 21: "Animal Movement Songs" (Oct 3)
**French Connection**: "Chansons de mouvement d'animaux"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Animal Sound Review (5 min)**: French animal sounds and names
- **Animal Movement Songs (20 min)**:
  - "Old MacDonald" in French with movements
  - "Walking in the Jungle" with animal actions
  - Create new verses with student animal suggestions
- **Animal Movement Game (5 min)**: Guess the animal through movement and sound

**Materials**: Animal picture cards with French names, action cards, animal music
**Assessment**: Integration of music, movement, and French vocabulary

### Lesson 22: "Mi'kmaq and Acadian Traditions" (Oct 4)
**French Connection**: "Traditions mi'kmaq et acadiennes"

**30-Minute Structure:**
- **Cultural Introduction (10 min)**:
  - Share simple information about Mi'kmaq and Acadian music
  - Respect and appreciation discussion
- **Traditional Songs/Movements (15 min)**:
  - Simple traditional song or chant (age-appropriate)
  - Basic movements that honor the culture
  - Discussion of music in cultural celebration
- **Appreciation Circle (5 min)**: Share what we learned about different musical traditions

**Materials**: Cultural music examples, respectful visual materials, guest speaker if possible
**Assessment**: Cultural appreciation and respectful participation

## Assessment Strategies

### Formative Assessment (Ongoing)
- **Observation Checklists**: Daily tracking of beat awareness, singing participation, instrument handling
- **Photo/Video Documentation**: Capture growth in movement and musical expression
- **Self-Assessment**: Simple thumbs up/down for "Did I keep the beat today?"
- **Peer Feedback**: Partner compliments on musical efforts

### Summative Assessment (End of Unit)
- **Beat Portfolio**: Collection of rhythm patterns student can perform
- **Singing Progress**: Record student singing simple French song
- **Movement Showcase**: Demonstrate creative movement to music
- **Instrument Skills Check**: Safe handling and basic playing of 3 instruments

### French Integration Assessment
- **Vocabulary Check**: Musical terms in French
- **Song Performance**: Accuracy in French pronunciation and melody
- **Cultural Appreciation**: Understanding of music in different cultures

## Differentiation Strategies

### For Emerging Learners
- **Visual Supports**: Picture cards for all activities
- **Simplified Patterns**: 2-beat patterns instead of 4-beat
- **Partner Support**: Buddy system for all activities
- **Extra Time**: Additional practice with basic concepts

### For Advanced Learners
- **Complex Patterns**: 6 or 8-beat rhythmic patterns
- **Leadership Roles**: Student conductors and teachers
- **Creative Extensions**: Compose own rhythm patterns and songs
- **Cultural Research**: Learn about additional musical traditions

### For Diverse Learners
- **Multi-sensory Approach**: Visual, auditory, kinesthetic learning
- **Choice Options**: Select instruments or movement styles
- **Flexible Grouping**: Individual, partner, small group, whole class
- **Home Connections**: Send rhythm activities home for family practice
