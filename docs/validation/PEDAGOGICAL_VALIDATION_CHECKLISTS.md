# 📋 PEDAGOGICAL VALIDATION CHECKLISTS

## Purpose
These checklists are designed for **intelligent manual assessment** of pedagogical plans based on ETFO (Elementary Teachers' Federation of Ontario) principles and educational best practices. They replace automated keyword searching with thoughtful evaluation of actual pedagogical quality.

## How to Use These Checklists
1. Read each criterion carefully
2. Review the plan holistically for evidence of the criterion
3. Consider context and grade-appropriate implementation
4. Make professional judgments based on pedagogical expertise
5. Look for authentic integration, not just surface-level mentions

---

## 1️⃣ LONG-RANGE PLAN VALIDATION CHECKLIST

### A. Curriculum Coverage & Alignment

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Complete curriculum expectations coverage**: Are all mandated curriculum expectations addressed throughout the year?
- [ ] **Logical scope and sequence**: Does the progression of learning make developmental sense?
- [ ] **Balanced subject integration**: Are all curriculum areas given appropriate time and attention?
- [ ] **Cross-curricular connections**: Are meaningful connections made between subject areas?
- [ ] **Provincial standards alignment**: Does the plan meet PEI/provincial curriculum requirements?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Learning builds progressively from September to June
- Earlier concepts support later learning
- Natural connections between subjects are leveraged
- Assessment aligns with curriculum expectations

### B. Essential Questions & Big Ideas (UbD Framework)

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Open-ended essential questions**: Do questions promote ongoing inquiry throughout the year?
- [ ] **Age-appropriate complexity**: Are questions accessible to Grade 1 students (6-year-olds)?
- [ ] **Transferable understandings**: Do big ideas apply beyond the classroom?
- [ ] **Conceptual depth**: Do questions lead to deep understanding rather than factual recall?
- [ ] **Student engagement potential**: Will questions spark genuine curiosity?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Questions cannot be answered with yes/no
- Questions connect to students' lives and experiences
- Big ideas represent enduring understandings
- Questions promote critical thinking at developmental level

### C. Assessment Framework

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Assessment FOR Learning**: Are there ongoing formative assessment strategies?
- [ ] **Assessment AS Learning**: Do students engage in self-assessment and reflection?
- [ ] **Assessment OF Learning**: Are summative assessments authentic and meaningful?
- [ ] **Multiple assessment methods**: Is there variety in how students demonstrate learning?
- [ ] **Clear success criteria**: Do students understand expectations?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Daily observational strategies specified
- Student self-reflection tools provided
- Performance tasks with real audiences
- Portfolio development opportunities
- Rubrics and criteria shared with students

### D. Differentiation & Inclusion

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Readiness differentiation**: Are there supports for different learning readiness levels?
- [ ] **Interest-based options**: Can students pursue learning through their interests?
- [ ] **Learning profile accommodations**: Are different learning styles addressed?
- [ ] **Accessibility considerations**: Can all students access the learning?
- [ ] **Extension and enrichment**: Are there challenges for advanced learners?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Specific strategies for emerging/developing/confident learners
- Choice in topics, processes, or products
- Visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learning opportunities
- Scaffolding and support structures detailed
- Open-ended tasks that allow multiple entry points

### E. Cultural Responsiveness & Community Connection

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Local cultural integration**: Is Acadian/local culture authentically represented?
- [ ] **Diverse perspectives**: Are multiple cultural viewpoints included?
- [ ] **Family engagement**: Are families meaningfully involved in learning?
- [ ] **Community connections**: Are there authentic community partnerships?
- [ ] **Identity affirmation**: Do all students see themselves reflected?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Specific cultural celebrations and traditions included
- Guest speakers and community experts planned
- Family knowledge valued and incorporated
- Local history and geography integrated
- Multiple cultural perspectives on topics

## 2️⃣ UNIT PLAN VALIDATION CHECKLIST

### A. Unit Design & Coherence

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Clear learning goals**: Are unit objectives specific and measurable?
- [ ] **Backward design**: Does planning start from desired outcomes?
- [ ] **Learning progression**: Is there a logical sequence of lessons?
- [ ] **Appropriate duration**: Is the unit length suitable (2-4 weeks)?
- [ ] **Culminating experience**: Is there a meaningful unit conclusion?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Unit goals align with long-range plan
- Daily lessons build toward unit objectives
- Skills and concepts develop progressively
- Time allocation matches complexity

### B. Three-Part Lesson Structure (ETFO Model)

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Minds On activities**: Do lessons begin with engagement and activation?
- [ ] **Action experiences**: Is there substantial learning time with varied activities?
- [ ] **Consolidation practices**: Do lessons conclude with reflection and connection?
- [ ] **Appropriate timing**: Are lesson segments appropriately timed for grade level?
- [ ] **Student voice**: Are there opportunities for student input and choice?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Minds On: 5-15 minutes, activates prior knowledge
- Action: 20-40 minutes, new learning and practice
- Consolidation: 5-15 minutes, reflection and assessment
- Transitions are smooth and purposeful

### C. Engagement Strategies (WHERETO Framework)

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **W - Where/Why**: Do students understand goals and relevance?
- [ ] **H - Hook**: Are there engaging entry points to capture interest?
- [ ] **E - Explore/Experience**: Are there hands-on learning opportunities?
- [ ] **R - Reflect/Rethink**: Are there reflection and revision opportunities?
- [ ] **E - Evaluate**: Do students self-assess their learning?
- [ ] **T - Tailor**: Is instruction differentiated for diverse learners?
- [ ] **O - Organize**: Is learning sequenced for maximum effectiveness?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Clear communication of learning goals
- Compelling hooks related to student interests
- Active exploration and discovery
- Regular reflection checkpoints
- Self-assessment tools provided

## 3️⃣ DEVELOPMENTAL APPROPRIATENESS CHECKLIST (Grade 1/6-year-olds)

### A. Cognitive Development Considerations

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Concrete thinking support**: Are abstract concepts made tangible?
- [ ] **Attention span awareness**: Are activities 15-20 minutes maximum?
- [ ] **Sequential instruction**: Are multi-step tasks broken down?
- [ ] **Visual supports**: Are instructions and concepts supported visually?
- [ ] **Repetition and practice**: Are key concepts revisited regularly?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Manipulatives and hands-on materials used
- Activities change every 15-20 minutes
- Visual schedules and picture supports
- Songs, rhymes, and movement integrated
- Consistent routines established

### B. Social-Emotional Development

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Emotional vocabulary**: Is emotional literacy developed?
- [ ] **Social skills practice**: Are cooperation skills explicitly taught?
- [ ] **Independence building**: Is self-reliance gradually developed?
- [ ] **Play-based learning**: Is play integrated into learning?
- [ ] **Safety and comfort**: Is emotional safety prioritized?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Feelings check-ins and emotion naming
- Partner and small group work structured
- Choice and decision-making opportunities
- Learning through games and play
- Predictable routines for security

### C. Physical Development

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Fine motor practice**: Are there activities for developing fine motor skills?
- [ ] **Gross motor integration**: Is movement incorporated into learning?
- [ ] **Sensory experiences**: Are multiple senses engaged?
- [ ] **Brain breaks**: Are movement breaks scheduled?
- [ ] **Workspace considerations**: Is furniture and space age-appropriate?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Writing, cutting, and manipulation activities
- Movement songs and active games
- Tactile and sensory materials
- Regular stretch and movement breaks
- Flexible seating options

## 4️⃣ FRENCH IMMERSION SPECIFIC CHECKLIST

### A. Language Development

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Oral communication priority**: Is speaking/listening emphasized?
- [ ] **Comprehensible input**: Is French input understandable?
- [ ] **Language scaffolding**: Are supports provided for French learning?
- [ ] **Authentic language use**: Is French used for real purposes?
- [ ] **Error approach**: Are errors treated as learning opportunities?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Daily oral language routines
- Visual and gestural supports
- Repetitive language structures
- Meaningful communication tasks
- Focus on communication over accuracy

### B. Cultural Integration

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Francophone culture**: Is French culture authentically integrated?
- [ ] **Local French community**: Are local francophone connections made?
- [ ] **French resources**: Are authentic French materials used?
- [ ] **Cultural celebrations**: Are French traditions celebrated?
- [ ] **Identity development**: Is French identity fostered?

#### Quality Indicators:
- French songs, stories, and games
- Connections to local French community
- Authentic French books and media
- Celebration of francophone holidays
- Pride in bilingual identity

## 5️⃣ IMPLEMENTATION FEASIBILITY CHECKLIST

### A. Resource Requirements

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Materials specified**: Are all needed materials clearly listed?
- [ ] **Resource availability**: Are resources realistically obtainable?
- [ ] **Budget considerations**: Are costs reasonable and justified?
- [ ] **Technology needs**: Are tech requirements clearly stated?
- [ ] **Space requirements**: Are space needs specified?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Complete materials lists provided
- Alternative low-cost options suggested
- Sharing and reuse strategies included
- Tech alternatives for equity
- Flexible space arrangements

### B. Teacher Preparation

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Clear instructions**: Can another teacher implement this plan?
- [ ] **Preparation time**: Is prep time reasonable and specified?
- [ ] **Professional learning**: Are PD needs identified?
- [ ] **Collaboration opportunities**: Are team planning times included?
- [ ] **Support structures**: Are mentoring/support needs addressed?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Step-by-step implementation guides
- Realistic prep time estimates
- PD resources suggested
- Team planning protocols
- Peer observation opportunities

### C. Family & Community Engagement

#### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Communication plan**: Is family communication structured?
- [ ] **Home-school connections**: Are home learning extensions provided?
- [ ] **Volunteer opportunities**: Can families contribute meaningfully?
- [ ] **Community partnerships**: Are community connections planned?
- [ ] **Cultural sensitivity**: Are diverse family structures respected?

#### Quality Indicators:
- Regular communication schedule
- Take-home activities provided
- Multiple ways to participate
- Community expert involvement
- Inclusive family engagement

## 6️⃣ LEARNING SKILLS & WORK HABITS CHECKLIST (ETFO)

### Essential Elements to Verify:
- [ ] **Responsibility**: Are there opportunities to develop accountability?
- [ ] **Organization**: Are organizational skills explicitly taught?
- [ ] **Independent Work**: Is independence gradually developed?
- [ ] **Collaboration**: Are cooperative skills practiced?
- [ ] **Initiative**: Is curiosity and initiative encouraged?
- [ ] **Self-Regulation**: Are self-management strategies taught?

### Quality Indicators:
- Clear expectations for each skill
- Explicit instruction in skill development
- Practice opportunities provided
- Progress monitoring strategies
- Student self-assessment of skills

## 📝 USING THESE CHECKLISTS

### For Plan Evaluation:
1. Review the plan document thoroughly
2. Work through each checklist systematically
3. Look for evidence of implementation, not just mention
4. Consider the coherence and integration of elements
5. Evaluate authenticity and depth, not surface compliance

### For Plan Improvement:
1. Identify unchecked items as improvement areas
2. Prioritize based on impact on student learning
3. Develop specific strategies to address gaps
4. Seek colleague input and collaboration
5. Iterate and refine based on implementation

### Key Principles:
- **Holistic Assessment**: Look at the plan as an integrated whole
- **Context Matters**: Consider school and community context
- **Student-Centered**: Focus on impact on student learning
- **Professional Judgment**: Use expertise to evaluate quality
- **Continuous Improvement**: Plans should evolve with implementation

---

## 🎯 QUALITY INDICATORS SUMMARY

A high-quality pedagogical plan will demonstrate:

1. **Coherence**: All elements work together toward clear goals
2. **Authenticity**: Real-world connections and meaningful learning
3. **Responsiveness**: Adaptation to student needs and interests
4. **Feasibility**: Realistic and implementable with available resources
5. **Evidence-Based**: Grounded in educational research and best practices
6. **Cultural Relevance**: Reflects and honors student communities
7. **Developmental Appropriateness**: Matches student developmental stages
8. **Assessment Integration**: Assessment supports and documents learning
9. **Differentiation**: Multiple pathways to success for all learners
10. **Engagement**: Captures and maintains student interest and motivation

---

*These checklists are based on ETFO planning guidelines, Understanding by Design (UbD) framework, and research-based best practices in elementary education. They are designed for thoughtful professional evaluation rather than automated scoring.*
