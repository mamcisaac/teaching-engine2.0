# Detailed Health Unit 3 Plan: "Sain et sauf"

## Unit Overview

**Unit Title:** Safe and Sound / Sain et sauf  
**Subject:** Formation personnelle et sociale (Health Education)  
**Grade:** 1  
**Duration:** November 25 - January 10, 2026 (18 hours total)  
**Linked Curriculum Expectation:** FPS3 - Démontrer sa compréhension des pratiques de sécurité personnelle

---

## Big Ideas & Essential Questions

### Big Ideas
- Safety awareness helps protect ourselves and others from harm
- We can learn to recognize and respond to different types of dangers
- Winter brings specific safety challenges that require preparation and knowledge
- Digital safety is becoming an important part of personal safety for young learners

### Essential Questions
- Comment puis-je rester en sécurité à la maison et à l'école? (How can I stay safe at home and school?)
- Quelles précautions dois-je prendre en hiver? (What precautions should I take in winter?)
- Qui peut m'aider quand j'ai besoin d'aide? (Who can help me when I need help?)
- Comment puis-je utiliser la technologie en toute sécurité? (How can I use technology safely?)

---

## Learning Goals & Success Criteria

### Learning Goals
Students will:
1. Understand home safety rules and emergency procedures
2. Practice school safety protocols and winter playground safety
3. Develop winter safety awareness and preparation strategies
4. Learn age-appropriate digital safety concepts
5. Identify community safety helpers and when to seek their assistance
6. Build confidence in emergency response skills

### Success Criteria
**Je peux...**
- identifier les règles de sécurité à la maison et à l'école (identify safety rules at home and school)
- expliquer les précautions de sécurité hivernale (explain winter safety precautions)
- reconnaître les personnes qui peuvent m'aider (recognize people who can help me)
- utiliser la technologie de manière sécuritaire (use technology safely)
- savoir quoi faire en cas d'urgence (know what to do in emergencies)

## Weekly Breakdown (18 Hours)

### Week 1: Home Safety Awareness (November 25-29) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Fire safety, kitchen safety, and stranger awareness

#### Lesson 1.1: Fire Safety Heroes (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Fire drill practice - listen for alarms and follow procedures
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about fire safety and escape plans appropriate for Grade 1
  - Practice "stop, drop, and roll" technique
  - Identify safe meeting places outside the home
  - Draw family escape plan with two exits from each room
  - Learn about smoke detectors and their important sounds
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create personal fire safety badge

#### Lesson 1.2: Kitchen Safety Smart (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Hot vs. cold sorting game with kitchen items
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Basic kitchen safety rules for 6-year-olds (hot stoves, sharp knives, electrical)
  - Practice safe behavior around kitchen appliances
  - Learn about poisonous substances and medicine safety
  - Understand importance of adult supervision in kitchen
  - Create kitchen safety reminder chart
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Kitchen safety role-play scenarios

#### Lesson 1.3: Stranger Safety Smarts (1.5 hours)
**TRAUMA-INFORMED APPROACH:**
- **Minds On (15 min):** Trusted adult identification circle
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about stranger safety using clear, non-frightening language
  - Practice saying "no" and getting away from unsafe situations
  - Identify trusted adults at home, school, and in community
  - Learn about appropriate vs. inappropriate requests from adults
  - Practice emergency contact information recall
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Safety network reminder creation

### Week 2: School Safety Practices (December 2-6) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Classroom, playground, and transportation safety

#### Lesson 2.1: Classroom Safety Champions (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Safety rule charades for classroom
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Review and practice classroom safety routines and procedures
  - Learn about emergency exits and evacuation procedures
  - Practice walking safely in hallways and on stairs
  - Understand importance of following adult instructions during emergencies
  - Create classroom safety helper responsibilities
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Safety pledge for our classroom

#### Lesson 2.2: Winter Playground Safety (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Winter clothing sorting - appropriate for playground
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn playground safety rules for winter conditions
  - Practice safe behavior on icy or snowy equipment
  - Understand importance of wearing appropriate winter gear
  - Learn about frostbite and hypothermia prevention
  - Practice buddy system for outdoor winter play
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Design winter playground safety poster

#### Lesson 2.3: Transportation Safety (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Vehicle safety equipment identification
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Practice bus safety procedures and protocols
  - Learn car seat and seatbelt safety
  - Understand pedestrian safety rules for winter
  - Practice crossing streets safely with winter conditions
  - Learn about reflective clothing for dark winter days
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Transportation safety checklist creation

### Week 3: Winter Safety Preparation (December 9-13) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Cold weather, ice/snow safety, and holiday considerations

#### Lesson 3.1: Dressing for Winter Success (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Winter clothing fashion show
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about layering clothing for warmth and comfort
  - Practice putting on winter gear independently
  - Understand importance of covering extremities (hands, feet, head)
  - Learn about waterproof vs. water-resistant clothing
  - Create personal winter clothing checklist
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Weather-appropriate dressing challenge

#### Lesson 3.2: Ice and Snow Safety (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Safe vs. unsafe winter activity sorting
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about ice safety and recognizing thin ice
  - Practice safe behavior around snow and ice
  - Understand winter weather warnings and what they mean
  - Learn about safe winter activities and sports
  - Practice falling safely on ice (protective techniques)
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Winter activity safety planning

#### Lesson 3.3: Holiday Safety Celebration (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Holiday decoration safety inspection
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about electrical safety with holiday decorations
  - Practice safe behavior around candles and lights
  - Understand food safety during holiday celebrations
  - Learn about toy safety and age-appropriate gifts
  - Create family holiday safety plan
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Holiday safety family commitment

### Week 4: Digital Safety Introduction (December 16-20) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Age-appropriate technology safety and digital citizenship

#### Lesson 4.1: Screen Time Balance (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Active vs. screen time activity sorting
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about healthy screen time limits for Grade 1
  - Understand importance of balancing screen time with other activities
  - Practice setting timers for technology use
  - Explore different types of screen time (educational vs. entertainment)
  - Create personal screen time schedule with family
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Screen time goal setting

#### Lesson 4.2: Online Stranger Awareness (1.5 hours)
**TRAUMA-INFORMED APPROACH:**
- **Minds On (15 min):** Real vs. online friends discussion
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn that people online might not be who they say they are
  - Practice never sharing personal information online
  - Understand importance of telling trusted adults about online interactions
  - Learn about appropriate vs. inappropriate online requests
  - Practice asking for help with technology problems
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Digital safety promise creation

#### Lesson 4.3: Technology Helper Skills (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Technology problem-solving scenarios
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn appropriate ways to ask for help with technology
  - Practice identifying when something doesn't feel right online
  - Understand importance of telling adults about confusing or scary content
  - Learn about being kind and respectful in digital communication
  - Create technology help-seeking plan
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Digital citizenship badge ceremony

### Week 5: Community Safety Helpers (January 6-10) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Police, firefighters, medical helpers, and emergency services

#### Lesson 5.1: Police Officers - Community Helpers (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Police officer role-play - what do they do?
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about police officers' role in community safety
  - Practice when and how to ask police for help
  - Understand that police are there to help and protect
  - Learn about calling 911 for police emergencies
  - Practice sharing important information (name, address, emergency)
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Thank you cards for local police

#### Lesson 5.2: Fire Fighters and Medical Helpers (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Emergency vehicle sound identification
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about firefighters' role beyond fighting fires
  - Understand when to call for medical help (ambulance)
  - Practice describing emergencies clearly to adults
  - Learn about paramedics and how they help people
  - Create emergency contact list with pictures
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Emergency services appreciation art

#### Lesson 5.3: Building My Safety Network (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Trusted adult web mapping
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Create comprehensive network of trusted adults
  - Practice reaching out for help in different situations
  - Learn about different types of help for different problems
  - Understand that asking for help is brave and smart
  - Role-play various help-seeking scenarios
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Safety network celebration circle

### Week 6: Safety Integration and Practice (January 13) - 2 hours
**Focus:** Practicing safety skills and celebrating learning

#### Lesson 6.1: Safety Skills Showcase (2 hours)
- **Minds On (20 min):** Safety skills reflection and demonstration prep
- **Action (80 min):**
  - Practice emergency procedures through fun simulations
  - Demonstrate winter safety preparations
  - Share digital safety commitments with families
  - Present safety network plans
  - Celebrate safety learning journey with badges and certificates
- **Consolidation (20 min):** Safety hero graduation ceremony

## Trauma-Informed Teaching Approaches

### Creating Safety
- **Predictable routines:** Consistent lesson structure to build security
- **Emotional safety:** Creating space for questions and concerns about safety topics
- **Choice and control:** Options in how students participate in safety discussions
- **Calming strategies:** Available support for students who may feel anxious about safety topics

### Sensitive Content Guidelines

#### Teaching About Personal Safety
1. **Age-appropriate language:** Clear, simple terms without graphic details
2. **Empowerment focus:** Emphasis on skills and knowledge rather than fear
3. **Cultural sensitivity:** Respect for different family approaches to safety
4. **Trauma awareness:** Recognition that some students may have safety-related trauma

#### Teaching About Stranger Safety
1. **Balanced messaging:** Safety awareness without creating excessive fear
2. **Trust building:** Focus on identifying trusted adults rather than fearing all strangers
3. **Disclosure support:** Multiple ways for students to share safety concerns
4. **Professional protocols:** Clear procedures for responding to safety disclosures

### Warning Signs Protocol
- **Behavioral changes:** Increased anxiety, withdrawal, or safety-related fears
- **Physical signs:** Unexplained injuries or reluctance to go home
- **Verbal indicators:** Concerning statements about safety or home situations
- **Immediate response:** Document concerns, report appropriately, and provide support

### Professional Support Network
- **School counselor:** Available for consultation and student support
- **Administrative team:** Informed of sensitive safety content and timing
- **Child protection services:** Contact information and reporting procedures ready
- **Community safety officers:** Available for classroom visits and education

## Assessment Rubric

### Safety Knowledge and Understanding (FPS3)
**Level 4 (Exceeding):**
- Consistently demonstrates comprehensive understanding of safety practices
- Makes independent safe choices with clear reasoning
- Connects safety learning to various real-life situations confidently

**Level 3 (Meeting):**
- Shows good understanding of basic safety concepts
- Makes safe choices with occasional reminders
- Demonstrates most safety practices independently

**Level 2 (Approaching):**
- Shows developing understanding of safety concepts
- Makes safe choices with regular guidance
- Demonstrates safety practices with support

**Level 1 (Below):**
- Shows limited understanding of safety concepts
- Requires significant support for safe choices
- Needs direct instruction for safety practices

### Emergency Response Skills
**Level 4:** Independently demonstrates emergency procedures and teaches others
**Level 3:** Consistently demonstrates emergency procedures with occasional reminders
**Level 2:** Demonstrates emergency procedures with regular prompting and support
**Level 1:** Requires direct instruction and assistance for emergency procedure completion

### Safety Planning and Prevention
**Level 4:** Creates comprehensive safety plans and identifies multiple prevention strategies
**Level 3:** Creates appropriate safety plans with minimal support
**Level 2:** Creates basic safety plans with guidance and reminders
**Level 1:** Requires significant support for safety planning and prevention understanding
