# Phase 1: Physical Education Unit Plan Improvements

## Critical Analysis Results

### Current State Issues Identified:

1. **Calendar Alignment Problems**
   - First unit starts September 5 instead of September 4
   - Last unit ends June 19 instead of June 25
   - Poor date progression with gaps and scheduling issues

2. **Insufficient Instructional Hours**
   - Current: 48 total hours (1.1 hrs/week)
   - Required: 135 hours (3.2 hrs/week)
   - **Gap**: 87 hours missing

3. **Safety Considerations Missing**
   - All 8 units lack explicit safety considerations
   - Critical for Grade 1 physical education
   - Safety must be integrated throughout

4. **Limited Physical Literacy Development**
   - Only 2/6 physical literacy components addressed
   - Missing: motivation, confidence, knowledge, understanding

5. **Data Integrity Issues**
   - One unit has null title
   - Inconsistent unit structure

6. **Seasonal Misalignment**
   - Units not optimally aligned with seasons
   - Outdoor activities in wrong seasons
