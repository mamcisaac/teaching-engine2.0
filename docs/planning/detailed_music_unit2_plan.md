# Music Unit 2: Rhythm Patterns and Autumn Songs
**Grade 1 Music Education | October 1 - November 1, 2025**

## Unit Overview
- **Duration**: 12 hours (24 sessions × 30 minutes)
- **Focus**: Rhythm pattern development and seasonal music exploration
- **Building On**: Unit 1 beat foundation
- **Culmination**: Autumn Music Celebration

## Learning Objectives

### Rhythm Development
- Recognize and clap simple rhythm patterns
- Use body percussion to express rhythms
- Introduce basic rhythm instruments
- Create original rhythm patterns
- Coordinate movement with rhythm

### Musical Knowledge
- Learn autumn-themed songs in French and English
- Explore cultural music traditions
- Understand instrument care and respect
- Develop ensemble skills
- Express creativity through movement

### Cultural Integration
- Acadian autumn music traditions
- Appropriate Mi'kmaq seasonal songs
- Family heritage music sharing
- Community music connections

## Weekly Breakdown

### Week 1 (October 1-3): Introduction to Rhythm Patterns
**Session 1: Simple Clapping Patterns**
- **Opening Circle** (5 min): "Bonjour" song with clapping
- **Rhythm Exploration** (15 min):
  - Echo clapping: teacher claps, students echo
  - Simple patterns: clap-clap-rest, clap-rest-clap
  - Use names: "Ma-rie" (clap-clap), "Jean" (clap)
- **Movement Activity** (8 min): Walking to steady beat, clapping patterns
- **Closing** (2 min): Quiet listening to autumn sounds

**Session 2: Body Percussion Introduction**
- **Opening** (3 min): Review clapping patterns from Session 1
- **Body Percussion** (18 min):
  - Demonstrate: clap, pat knees, stamp feet
  - Practice each separately
  - Simple combinations: clap-pat, stamp-clap
  - "Mon corps fait de la musique" (My body makes music)
- **Autumn Song** (7 min): Learn "L'automne arrive" (simple melody)
- **Closing** (2 min): Body percussion lullaby

### Week 2 (October 6-10): Rhythm Instruments
**Session 3: Rhythm Sticks Introduction**
- **Opening** (4 min): Body percussion warm-up
- **Instrument Introduction** (20 min):
  - Proper holding technique
  - Safety rules: "Sticks stay in front"
  - Basic patterns with sticks
  - Echo games with rhythm sticks
  - Care and storage demonstration
- **Song Practice** (4 min): "L'automne arrive" with stick accompaniment
- **Cleanup & Closing** (2 min): Instrument care routine

**Session 4: Tambourine Exploration**
- **Opening** (3 min): Rhythm stick review
- **Tambourine Activities** (18 min):
  - Gentle shaking vs. tapping
  - Loud and soft exploration
  - Passing tambourine in circle
  - "Rain Song" - tambourine as rain sounds
- **Movement** (7 min): Dance with tambourine rhythms
- **Closing** (2 min): Quiet tambourine lullaby

**Session 5: Instrument Ensemble**
- **Opening** (4 min): Choose instrument preference
- **Ensemble Practice** (20 min):
  - Small groups: sticks and tambourines
  - Simple patterns together
  - Taking turns leading
  - "Autumn Orchestra" - all instruments
- **Performance** (4 min): Share with another class
- **Reflection** (2 min): What we learned

### Week 3 (October 13-17): Autumn Songs & Cultural Music
**Session 6: French Autumn Songs**
- **Opening** (3 min): Instrument warm-up
- **New Song Learning** (20 min):
  - "Les feuilles tombent" (The leaves fall)
  - Hand motions for falling leaves
  - Verse and simple chorus
  - Melody practice
- **Creative Movement** (5 min): Be falling leaves
- **Closing** (2 min): Gentle leaf floating

**Session 7: Acadian Heritage Music**
- **Opening** (4 min): Review "Les feuilles tombent"
- **Cultural Exploration** (18 min):
  - Simple Acadian song: "C'est l'automne"
  - Story about Acadian autumn traditions
  - Listen to recorded Acadian music
  - Clap along to Acadian rhythms
- **Discussion** (6 min): Family autumn music traditions
- **Closing** (2 min): Acadian lullaby listening

**Session 8: Mi'kmaq Seasonal Music (Respectful Introduction)**
- **Opening** (3 min): Quiet reflection on nature
- **Cultural Learning** (20 min):
  - Appropriate Mi'kmaq seasonal song
  - Respect for Indigenous culture discussion
  - Simple hand drum introduction
  - Nature sounds in music
- **Nature Connection** (5 min): Listen to outdoor autumn sounds
- **Closing** (2 min): Gratitude circle

### Week 4 (October 20-24): Movement and Expression
**Session 9: Autumn Leaf Dancing**
- **Opening** (4 min): Body percussion review
- **Movement Exploration** (20 min):
  - Scarf dancing as leaves
  - Fast wind vs. gentle breeze
  - High and low movements
  - Partner leaf dancing
- **Music and Movement** (4 min): Dance to "Les feuilles tombent"
- **Cool Down** (2 min): Leaves settling to ground

**Session 10: Harvest Movement**
- **Opening** (3 min): Harvest song introduction
- **Harvest Activities** (18 min):
  - "Picking apples" movements
  - "Gathering pumpkins" actions
  - Traditional harvest song: "Autumn Harvest"
  - Group harvest dance
- **Creative Expression** (7 min): Student-created harvest movements
- **Sharing** (2 min): Show favorite harvest move

**Session 11: Halloween Fun (Appropriate)**
- **Opening** (4 min): Spooky sound exploration
- **Halloween Music** (18 min):
  - "Five Little Pumpkins" action song
  - Gentle Halloween sounds with instruments
  - "Monster March" rhythm activity
  - Safety and fun Halloween song
- **Costume Movement** (6 min): Move like friendly Halloween characters
- **Closing** (2 min): Calm after Halloween excitement

### Week 5 (October 27-31): Pattern Creation & Thanksgiving
**Session 12: Create Our Own Patterns**
- **Opening** (4 min): Review favorite rhythms
- **Pattern Creation** (20 min):
  - Students create clapping patterns
  - Teach patterns to friends
  - Combine student patterns
  - Record favorite patterns
- **Pattern Performance** (4 min): Share with class
- **Reflection** (2 min): What makes a good pattern?

**Session 13: Thanksgiving Music**
- **Opening** (3 min): Gratitude circle
- **Thanksgiving Songs** (20 min):
  - "Merci pour l'automne" (Thank you for autumn)
  - English Thanksgiving song
  - Gratitude rhythm activity
  - Family sharing song
- **Thankful Movement** (5 min): Express gratitude through movement
- **Closing** (2 min): Quiet thankfulness

**Session 14: Community Music Connections**
- **Opening** (4 min): Community helpers song
- **Music in Community** (18 min):
  - How music helps our community
  - Family music traditions sharing
  - School music events
  - Community celebration songs
- **Future Planning** (6 min): Ideas for sharing our music
- **Closing** (2 min): Community appreciation

### Week 6 (November 1): Unit Celebration
**Session 15: Autumn Music Celebration Preparation**
- **Opening** (5 min): Review all unit songs
- **Rehearsal** (20 min):
  - Choose favorite songs and activities
  - Practice instrument parts
  - Organize presentation order
  - Dress rehearsal
- **Final Preparation** (5 min): Prepare for celebration

**Session 16: Autumn Music Celebration**
- **Performance** (25 min):
  - Welcome song with actions
  - Rhythm pattern demonstration
  - Autumn song medley
  - Cultural music sharing
  - Instrument ensemble piece
- **Reflection & Celebration** (5 min): What we accomplished

## Assessment Strategies

### Formative Assessment
- Daily observation of rhythm accuracy
- Participation in movement activities
- Instrument handling skills
- Cooperation in ensemble work
- Cultural respect demonstration

### Summative Assessment
- **Rhythm Pattern Assessment**: Individual clapping test
- **Song Performance**: Solo or small group singing
- **Instrument Skills**: Proper technique demonstration
- **Movement Expression**: Creative movement showcase
- **Cultural Understanding**: Discussion about learned traditions

## Differentiation Strategies

### For Advanced Learners
- Create more complex rhythm patterns
- Lead small ensemble groups
- Teach patterns to younger students
- Explore additional cultural music

### For Beginning Learners
- Simplified rhythm patterns
- Extra visual cues for patterns
- One-on-one practice time
- Modified instrument techniques

### For Diverse Learners
- Visual rhythm cards
- Multiple ways to express patterns
- Buddy system for support
- Choice in movement expression

## Home Connections

### Family Engagement
- Send home songs to practice
- Family autumn music tradition sharing
- Halloween safety song practice
- Thanksgiving gratitude activities

### Extension Activities
- Create family rhythm patterns
- Listen to autumn music at home
- Share cultural music traditions
- Practice instrument care

## Safety Considerations

### Instrument Safety
- Proper holding techniques
- "Instruments stay in front" rule
- Gentle handling demonstration
- Storage and care routines

### Movement Safety
- Clear movement boundaries
- Safe space guidelines
- Awareness of others
- Controlled energy activities

4. **Instrument Ensemble**: Rhythm sticks and tambourines
5. **Movement Expression**: Autumn leaf dance
