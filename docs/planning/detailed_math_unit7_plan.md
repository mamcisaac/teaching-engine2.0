# Mathematics Unit 7: Mathematical Celebration
## Célébration mathématique (Mathematical Celebration)

### 📅 Unit Overview
- **Duration**: March 2 - April 17, 2026 (7 weeks, 28 instructional hours)
- **Grade**: 1 French Immersion
- **Subject**: Mathématiques
- **Unit Focus**: Portfolio development, mathematical growth celebration, learning consolidation, mathematical identity formation

### 🎯 Unit Purpose
This is an **integration and celebration unit** that does not introduce new curriculum expectations but rather:
- Consolidates all learning from Units 1-6
- Develops mathematical portfolios showcasing growth
- Celebrates mathematical identity and confidence
- Prepares for Grade 2 mathematical thinking
- Honors diverse mathematical strengths and approaches

---

## 📚 CELEBRATION-FOCUSED PROGRESSION

### Weeks 1-2 (Mar 2-13): MATHEMATICAL AUTOBIOGRAPHY
**Focus**: Reflecting on mathematical journey and identity

#### Mathematical Identity Development:
- **"I Am a Mathematician" Exploration**:
  - Students identify their mathematical strengths
  - Celebrate different ways of being mathematical
  - Create mathematical self-portraits
  - Share mathematical stories and experiences

- **Mathematical Journey Mapping**:
  - Timeline of mathematical learning from September
  - Favorite mathematical moments and discoveries
  - Challenges overcome and growth celebrated
  - Dreams for future mathematical learning

- **Cultural Mathematical Identity**:
  - Exploring mathematical identity in French culture
  - Family mathematical traditions and practices
  - Indigenous mathematical perspectives and identity
  - Celebrating multilingual mathematical thinking

#### Portfolio Foundation Activities:
- **Learning Evidence Collection**:
  - Gathering work samples from each unit (Sep-Feb)
  - Organizing mathematical thinking artifacts
  - Selecting pieces that show growth and learning
  - Creating reflection labels for each piece

### Weeks 3-4 (Mar 16-27): PORTFOLIO DEVELOPMENT WORKSHOP
**Focus**: Creating comprehensive mathematical portfolios

#### Portfolio Organization System:
- **Mathematical Growth Documentation**:
  - Before/after comparisons of mathematical understanding
  - Strategy development progression over time
  - Problem-solving growth evidence
  - Mathematical communication improvement

- **Skill Celebration Sections**:
  - **Numbers Section**: Counting, number sense, operations growth
  - **Patterns Section**: Pattern recognition and creation development
  - **Shapes Section**: Geometry understanding and spatial reasoning
  - **Measurement Section**: Measurement skill progression
  - **Problem Solving Section**: Mathematical thinking evolution

#### Digital Portfolio Integration:
- **Photo Documentation**: Pictures of mathematical work and investigations
- **Video Reflections**: Students explaining their mathematical growth in French
- **Audio Recordings**: Mathematical discussions and explanations
- **Interactive Elements**: QR codes linking to digital mathematical stories

### Weeks 5-6 (Mar 30-Apr 10): MATHEMATICAL EXHIBITION PREPARATION
**Focus**: Preparing to share mathematical learning with community

#### Exhibition Components:
- **Mathematical Museum**: Student-curated displays of learning
- **Interactive Math Stations**: Hands-on activities for visitors
- **Mathematical Performances**: Songs, poems, stories about math
- **Teaching Stations**: Students teaching mathematical concepts to visitors

#### Community Preparation:
- **Invitation Creation**: Designing invitations for families and community
- **Presentation Practice**: Rehearsing mathematical explanations
- **Station Setup**: Organizing interactive learning experiences
- **Cultural Integration**: Incorporating French mathematical presentations

### Week 7 (Apr 14-17): MATHEMATICAL CELEBRATION WEEK
**Focus**: Celebrating mathematical achievements with community

#### Celebration Activities:
- **Family Math Fair**: Showcasing mathematical learning and growth
- **Peer Teaching**: Sharing mathematical knowledge with other classes
- **Mathematical Awards**: Celebrating diverse mathematical strengths
- **Reflection Ceremonies**: Honoring mathematical journey and growth

## 📊 GROWTH-FOCUSED ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Portfolio Assessment Rubric:

#### Mathematical Growth Documentation:
**Exemplary (4)**:
- Clear evidence of significant mathematical growth across multiple areas
- Sophisticated reflection on learning journey with specific examples
- Demonstrates deep understanding of mathematical concepts
- Shows mathematical thinking evolution over time

**Proficient (3)**:
- Good evidence of mathematical growth with some specific examples
- Thoughtful reflection on learning with general examples
- Shows solid understanding of mathematical concepts
- Some evidence of mathematical thinking development

**Developing (2)**:
- Some evidence of mathematical growth with basic examples
- Basic reflection on learning with simple statements
- Shows emerging understanding of mathematical concepts
- Limited evidence of mathematical thinking changes

**Beginning (1)**:
- Minimal evidence of mathematical growth
- Very basic reflection with little detail
- Shows beginning understanding of concepts
- Little evidence of thinking development

#### French Mathematical Communication:
- **Vocabulary Use**: Appropriate use of French mathematical terms
- **Explanation Clarity**: Clear explanations of mathematical thinking in French
- **Cultural Integration**: Connection between mathematical learning and French culture
- **Communication Confidence**: Comfort sharing mathematical ideas in French

### Growth Documentation Methods:
- **Learning Progression Charts**: Visual showing skill development over time
- **Self-Assessment Reflections**: Student evaluation of own mathematical growth
- **Peer Recognition**: Classmates acknowledging each other's mathematical strengths
- **Family Feedback**: Parent/guardian observations of mathematical growth at home

## 🎯 DIFFERENTIATION FOR CELEBRATION

### Support Level (Emerging Learners):
- **Visual Growth Documentation**: Picture-heavy portfolios with minimal writing
- **Guided Reflection**: Teacher support for identifying growth and strengths
- **Peer Partnership**: Buddy system for portfolio development and sharing
- **Simplified Presentations**: Focus on one or two mathematical achievements
- **Celebration of Effort**: Emphasizing persistence and trying over correctness
- **Multi-modal Sharing**: Various ways to share mathematical learning

### Standard Level (Developing Learners):
- **Balanced Documentation**: Mix of visual and written portfolio elements
- **Independent Reflection**: Self-directed thinking about mathematical growth
- **Choice in Presentation**: Various options for sharing learning
- **Mathematical Storytelling**: Creating narratives about mathematical journey
- **Goal Setting**: Planning for continued mathematical growth
- **French Integration**: Incorporating French mathematical vocabulary

### Extension Level (Advanced Learners):
- **Sophisticated Analysis**: Deep reflection on mathematical thinking evolution
- **Leadership Roles**: Helping organize celebration events and supporting peers
- **Complex Presentations**: Multi-faceted sharing of mathematical learning
- **Cross-Curricular Connections**: Linking mathematics to other subjects
- **Future Planning**: Advanced goal setting for Grade 2 and beyond
- **Mentoring Others**: Teaching and supporting other students' growth

- Je peux identifier mes forces mathématiques
- Je peux reconnaître mes progrès depuis septembre
- Je peux expliquer ce que j'ai appris sur les mathématiques

- Je peux me fixer des objectifs pour continuer à apprendre

- Je peux présenter mon apprentissage mathématique en français
- Je peux enseigner des concepts mathématiques à d'autres
- Je peux participer aux célébrations de classe avec confiance
- Je peux expliquer pourquoi les mathématiques sont importantes
