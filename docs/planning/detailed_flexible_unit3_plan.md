# Flexible Learning Unit 3: Holiday Traditions & Community Celebrations

**Grade Level:** Grade 1 (French Immersion)  
**Duration:** November 4 - December 20, 2025 (50 hours)  
**Unit Theme:** Holiday Traditions & Community Celebrations  
**Building Foundation:** Autumn investigation and community awareness

## Unit Overview

This flexible learning unit empowers Grade 1 students to explore diverse holiday traditions and community celebrations while developing research skills, cultural awareness, and community connection. Students will investigate family heritage, engage with community members, and create meaningful projects that celebrate diversity and foster belonging.

## Learning Objectives

### Knowledge and Understanding
- Identify various cultural and religious celebrations in our community
- Understand the concept of traditions and their importance to families
- Recognize similarities and differences in holiday celebrations
- Learn about winter weather and seasonal changes

### Skills Development
- Research and inquiry skills using age-appropriate resources
- Digital literacy through technology integration
- Communication skills through presentations and community interaction
- Creative expression through multiple art forms
- Collaboration and teamwork in community projects

### Values and Attitudes
- Respect for cultural diversity and different traditions
- Appreciation for community connections and service
- Empathy and understanding for others' experiences
- Responsibility for contributing to community well-being

## 1. Cultural Celebration Research (Weeks 1-2: November 4-15)

### Family Heritage Investigations
**Student Choice Focus:** Each student selects one family tradition to investigate deeply

#### Week 1: Family Tradition Discovery
- **Home Interview Project:** Students interview family members about holiday traditions
  - Provided interview questions in English and French
  - Photo collection of family celebration items
  - Recording family stories (with permission)
  - Creating family tradition timeline

- **Cultural Artifact Sharing:** Students bring meaningful items from home
  - Show and tell presentations
  - Creating classroom "Tradition Museum"
  - Respectful handling and discussion protocols
  - Digital documentation for absent students

#### Week 2: Research Expansion
- **Community Cultural Mapping:** Identifying celebrations in our local area
  - Walking tour of community cultural centers
  - Interviewing community cultural leaders
  - Creating classroom map of local celebration venues
  - Building relationships with cultural community members

### International Holiday Exploration
**Technology Integration:** Virtual cultural exploration

- **Digital Cultural Journey:** Using age-appropriate websites and videos
  - Exploring holidays from different continents
  - Virtual museum tours of cultural celebrations
  - Connecting with pen pal classrooms globally
  - Creating digital scrapbooks of discoveries

- **Celebration Calendar Creation:** Large collaborative project
  - Researching holidays throughout November-December
  - Including religious and secular celebrations
  - Adding student family traditions
  - Displaying prominently in hallway for community viewing

## 2. Community Engagement Projects (Weeks 3-5: November 18 - December 6)

### Senior Home Visit Preparations
**Real Community Connection:** Building intergenerational relationships

#### Week 3: Preparation and Planning
- **Interview Skill Development:** Role-playing and practice
  - Learning respectful conversation techniques
  - Preparing questions about "holidays when you were young"
  - Creating comfort items for residents (bookmarks, drawings)
  - Understanding senior care and respect

- **Gift Creation Workshop:** Handmade items for sharing
  - Holiday cards with personal messages
  - Simple craft projects seniors can enjoy
  - Photo books of student families to share
  - Recording student songs and stories

#### Week 4: Community Visits and Documentation
- **Senior Home Visits:** Structured interaction sessions
  - Sharing student family traditions
  - Learning about historical holiday celebrations
  - Recording stories (with permission)
  - Building ongoing relationships

- **Story Collection Project:** Preserving community history
  - Documenting senior stories about past celebrations
  - Creating audio recordings for families
  - Writing illustrated story books
  - Preparing presentation for families

### Community Service Projects
**Choice-Based Giving:** Students select meaningful ways to contribute

#### Week 5: Service Learning Implementation
- **Food Bank Contribution Drive:** Collaborative giving project
  - Learning about food insecurity in community
  - Organizing classroom food collection
  - Creating awareness posters for school
  - Visiting food bank to understand operations

- **Community Decoration Project:** Beautifying shared spaces
  - Creating window decorations for local businesses
  - Installing student artwork in community centers
  - Preparing outdoor displays for public enjoyment
  - Partnering with municipal recreation programs

## 3. Technology Integration (Throughout Unit)

### Digital Storytelling Development
**Progressive Skill Building:** From simple to complex digital projects

- **Week 1-2: Basic Digital Skills**
  - Taking photos of family traditions
  - Using tablet apps for simple video recording
  - Creating digital voice recordings
  - Learning safe internet research practices

- **Week 3-4: Story Creation**
  - Using age-appropriate video editing apps
  - Creating photo slideshows with narration
  - Recording family tradition stories
  - Adding music and simple effects

- **Week 5-6: Presentation Preparation**
  - Preparing digital presentations for families
  - Creating QR codes linking to student projects
  - Developing classroom video documentation
  - Building digital portfolio entries

### Virtual Cultural Exploration
**Safe Online Learning:** Supervised digital discovery

- **Virtual Museum Tours:** Structured online exploration
  - National museums with holiday exhibitions
  - International cultural centers
  - Religious institution virtual tours
  - Community cultural organization websites

- **Global Classroom Connections:** Building international awareness
  - Video calls with classrooms celebrating different holidays
  - Exchanging digital postcards internationally
  - Sharing tradition videos with global pen pals
  - Creating world map of celebration connections

## 4. Choice-Based Holiday Learning (Weeks 6-7: December 9-20)

### Student-Selected Celebration Focus
**Individual Passion Projects:** Deep dive into personal interests

#### Multiple Presentation Format Options:
1. **Traditional Poster Presentation**
   - Research and create informational poster
   - Present to classmates and families
   - Include interactive elements

2. **Digital Storytelling Project**
   - Create video documentary about chosen celebration
   - Include interviews and personal reflection
   - Share with wider school community

3. **Dramatic Performance**
   - Create short play about holiday tradition
   - Include costumes and simple props
   - Perform for multiple audiences

4. **Art Installation**
   - Create 3D display representing chosen holiday
   - Include interactive learning stations
   - Guide tours for other classes

5. **Community Action Project**
   - Plan and implement service project
   - Connect celebration learning to helping others
   - Document impact and learning

### Collaborative Community Showcase
**Culminating Celebration:** Bringing learning together

- **Community Celebration Fair:** Multi-station learning event
  - Student presentation stations throughout school
  - Community members invited as learners
  - Interactive activities for all ages
  - Celebration food sharing (cultural dishes)

- **Digital Documentation:** Creating lasting memories
  - Professional photographer documenting event
  - Student-created video highlights
  - Digital scrapbook for families
  - Online gallery for extended community

## 5. Winter Preparation Activities (Ongoing Integration)

### Weather and Safety Investigation
**Practical Life Skills:** Connecting learning to daily experience

- **Winter Weather Monitoring:** Daily weather observation project
  - Recording temperature, precipitation, daylight hours
  - Graphing weather patterns throughout unit
  - Predicting weather impact on holiday celebrations
  - Learning appropriate winter clothing and safety

- **Community Winter Activity Research:** Exploring seasonal opportunities
  - Investigating local winter festivals and events
  - Learning about winter sports and activities
  - Understanding how different cultures adapt to winter
  - Planning winter celebration activities

### Indoor Project Development
**Extended Learning:** Preparing for weather-dependent days

- **Classroom Winter Workshop:** Creative indoor activities
  - Setting up rotation stations for choice-based learning
  - Developing cozy reading and research areas
  - Creating warm, welcoming celebration spaces
  - Planning indoor movement and activity options

## Safety and Inclusion Considerations

### Cultural Sensitivity
- Ensuring all family structures and traditions are valued
- Providing alternative activities for students who don't celebrate certain holidays
- Focusing on family traditions rather than commercial celebrations
- Including secular and religious perspectives respectfully

### Safety Protocols
- Supervised community visits with multiple adults
- Permission forms for all photo and video documentation
- Safe technology use guidelines
- Allergy awareness for food-related activities

### Inclusion Strategies
- Multiple format options for all learners
- Visual, auditory, and kinesthetic learning opportunities
- Collaboration support for different ability levels
- Flexible timing and expectations
