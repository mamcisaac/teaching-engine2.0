# Detailed Health Unit 1 Plan: "Moi, moi-même et je"

## Unit Overview

**Unit Title:** Me, Myself, and I / Moi, moi-même et je  
**Subject:** Formation personnelle et sociale (Health Education)  
**Grade:** 1  
**Duration:** September 4 - October 11, 2025 (18 hours total)  
**Linked Curriculum Expectation:** FPS4 - Démontrer sa compréhension de ses compétences et aptitudes personnelles

---

## Big Ideas & Essential Questions

### Big Ideas
- I am unique and special with my own strengths and interests
- Understanding myself helps me grow and learn
- Taking care of myself shows self-respect and helps me thrive

### Essential Questions
- Qui suis-je? (Who am I?)
- Qu'est-ce qui me rend unique? (What makes me unique?)
- Comment puis-je prendre soin de moi? (How can I take care of myself?)

---

## Learning Goals & Success Criteria

### Learning Goals
Students will:
1. Develop self-awareness and personal identity
2. Recognize their unique strengths and interests
3. Practice self-care and hygiene routines
4. Understand basic personal safety concepts
5. Build emotional awareness and regulation skills

### Success Criteria
**Je peux...**
- identifier mes forces personnelles (identify my personal strengths)
- nommer et exprimer mes sentiments (name and express my feelings)
- décrire ce qui me rend unique (describe what makes me unique)
- pratiquer de bonnes habitudes d'hygiène (practice good hygiene habits)
- identifier des adultes de confiance (identify trusted adults)

---

## Curriculum Expectation Coverage

### FPS4 Indicators
- **décrire ses compétences et intérêts personnels** (describe personal skills and interests)

## Weekly Breakdown (18 Hours)

### Week 1: Getting to Know Me (September 4-8) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Personal identity and uniqueness

#### Lesson 1.1: My Special Self (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Mirror activity - "What do you see?"
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Create "All About Me" posters with personal information
  - Practice saying full name, age, favorite things
  - Share family traditions and cultural backgrounds
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Gallery walk of personal posters

#### Lesson 1.2: My Body, My Home (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Body part song in French
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Label body parts using proper anatomical terms
  - Discuss body functions and why our body is special
  - Introduction to appropriate vs. inappropriate touch concepts
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Body appreciation circle

#### Lesson 1.3: Feelings Detective (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Emotion charades
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Identify basic emotions using feeling cards
  - Practice facial expressions and body language
  - Create personal feeling journal
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Share one feeling from today

### Week 2: My Strengths and Interests (September 11-15) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Discovering personal capabilities and preferences

#### Lesson 2.1: What Am I Good At? (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Strength spotting game
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Create strength inventory using pictures and words
  - Interview classmates about their strengths
  - Practice positive self-talk phrases in French
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Strength celebration circle

#### Lesson 2.2: My Favorite Things (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Interest sorting activity
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Create interest collages from magazines
  - Explore why we like different things
  - Share interests through show and tell
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Interest connection web

#### Lesson 2.3: Goal Setting for Little Learners (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** "I want to learn..." sharing
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Understand what goals are using concrete examples
  - Set simple, achievable personal goals
  - Create visual goal tracking charts
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Goal commitment ceremony

### Week 3: Taking Care of Myself (September 18-22) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Self-care and hygiene routines

#### Lesson 3.1: Clean and Healthy Habits (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Germ detective game
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Proper handwashing technique practice
  - Tooth brushing demonstration and practice
  - Create daily hygiene routine charts
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Hygiene routine role play

#### Lesson 3.2: Healthy Food Choices (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Food sorting - healthy vs. treats
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Explore Canada's Food Guide for children
  - Plan healthy snacks and meals
  - Practice reading food labels (simple)
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Healthy lunch planning

#### Lesson 3.3: Rest and Movement (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Energy level check-in
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Understand importance of sleep for growth
  - Practice calming strategies for bedtime
  - Explore different types of physical activity
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create bedtime routine plan

### Week 4: Safety and Trust (September 25-29) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Personal safety and trusted adults

#### Lesson 4.1: My Safety Network (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Trusted adult identification
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Create circles of trust diagram
  - Practice asking for help appropriately
  - Learn when and how to say "no" to unsafe situations
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Safety network sharing

#### Lesson 4.2: Safe vs. Unsafe Touch (1.5 hours)
**TRAUMA-INFORMED APPROACH:**
- **Minds On (15 min):** Personal space activity
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Understand body belongs to them
  - Distinguish safe/unsafe touch using clear examples
  - Practice disclosure responses with non-judgmental language
  - Know it's never their fault if someone touches inappropriately
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Safety affirmations

#### Lesson 4.3: Emergency Skills (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Emergency scenarios discussion
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Practice calling 911 with important information
  - Memorize full name, address, parent names
  - Identify dangerous substances and situations
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Emergency information review

### Week 5: Emotional Awareness and Regulation (October 2-6) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Understanding and managing emotions

#### Lesson 5.1: Big Feelings, Little Body (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Emotion intensity thermometer
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Explore different emotion intensities
  - Practice identifying emotions in self and others
  - Learn that all feelings are okay, but some behaviors aren't
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Feeling check-in routine

#### Lesson 5.2: Calm Down Strategies (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Breathing exercise introduction
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Practice deep breathing techniques
  - Create personal calm down kits
  - Learn to use calm down spaces appropriately
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Calm down strategy sharing

#### Lesson 5.3: My Happy Place (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Visualization warm-up
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Guided visualization to safe, happy places
  - Draw or describe personal happy places
  - Practice using visualization for self-soothing
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Happy place meditation

### Week 6: Celebration and Integration (October 9-11) - 2 hours
**Focus:** Celebrating growth and integrating learning

#### Lesson 6.1: My Growth Celebration (2 hours)
- **Minds On (20 min):** Growth reflection activity
- **Action (80 min):**
  - Present "All About Me" portfolios to families
  - Demonstrate learned self-care routines
  - Share personal goals and progress
  - Celebrate unique qualities of each student
- **Consolidation (20 min):** Circle of appreciation

## Trauma-Informed Teaching Approaches

### Creating Safety
- **Consistent routines:** Predictable lesson structure and classroom procedures
- **Clear boundaries:** Explicit explanation of personal space and respect
- **Choice and control:** Options in activities and expression methods
- **Calming environment:** Designated quiet spaces and sensory tools

### Sensitive Content Guidelines

#### Teaching About Safe/Unsafe Touch
1. **Use age-appropriate language:** Simple, clear terms without graphic details
2. **Normalize body ownership:** "Your body belongs to you"
3. **Emphasize not their fault:** Clear messaging about adult responsibility
4. **Provide multiple disclosure opportunities:** Various ways to communicate concerns
5. **Professional support ready:** School counselor and administration informed

#### Warning Signs Protocol
- **Behavioral changes:** Withdrawal, aggression, regression, anxiety
- **Physical signs:** Unexplained injuries, hygiene issues, sexualized behavior
- **Verbal indicators:** Concerning statements about home or safety
- **Immediate response:** Document, report, support, and refer

### Professional Support Network
- **School counselor:** Available for immediate consultation
- **Administrative team:** Informed of sensitive content timeline
- **Public health nurse:** Resource for health and safety questions
- **Child protection services:** Contact information readily available

## Assessment Rubric

### Personal Identity (FPS4)
**Level 4 (Exceeding):**
- Confidently describes multiple personal strengths and interests
- Makes connections between interests and future goals
- Demonstrates sophisticated self-awareness for age

**Level 3 (Meeting):**
- Identifies several personal strengths and interests
- Sets realistic personal goals with support
- Shows good self-awareness and reflection

**Level 2 (Approaching):**
- Identifies some personal strengths with prompting
- Needs support to set appropriate goals
- Shows developing self-awareness

**Level 1 (Below):**
- Requires significant support to identify strengths
- Difficulty setting goals even with assistance
- Limited self-awareness demonstrated

### Self-Care Practices
**Level 4:** Independently demonstrates all hygiene routines and explains importance
**Level 3:** Demonstrates most routines independently with occasional reminders
**Level 2:** Demonstrates routines with regular prompting and support
**Level 1:** Requires direct instruction and assistance for routine completion

### Safety Understanding
**Level 4:** Clearly articulates safety concepts and applies to new situations
**Level 3:** Demonstrates good understanding of taught safety concepts
**Level 2:** Shows basic understanding with some confusion in application
**Level 1:** Limited understanding requiring repeated instruction and support
