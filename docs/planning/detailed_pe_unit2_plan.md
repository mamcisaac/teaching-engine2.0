# PE Unit 2: Locomotor Skills and Fall Activities
**Grade 1 - Physical Education with Specialist Teacher**

## Unit Overview
- **Duration**: October 7 - November 1, 2025 (15 hours)
- **Format**: 30-minute blocks, 3 times per week
- **Focus**: Locomotor skill development with autumn-themed activities
- **Building on**: Unit 1 body awareness foundation

## Unit Goals
1. Develop fundamental locomotor skills (running, skipping, galloping, jumping, hopping, sliding, leaping)
2. Integrate seasonal autumn movement activities
3. Introduction to basic ball skills and partner cooperation
4. Expand French movement vocabulary
5. Promote outdoor learning and nature connection

## Safety Protocols
- Weather-appropriate clothing checks
- Outdoor equipment inspection
- Clear boundaries for activities
- Emergency procedures for outdoor spaces
- Allergy awareness for nature materials

---

## Lesson Plans (30 minutes each)

### **Lesson 1: Running Technique Refinement**
**Date**: October 7, 2025
**Location**: Gymnasium/Outdoor field

**Warm-up (5 minutes)**
- Marching in place with high knees
- Arm circles and shoulder rolls
- Dynamic stretching: leg swings

**Main Activity (20 minutes)**
- **Running Technique Focus**: Proper arm swing, foot placement, posture
- **Autumn Leaf Chase**: Students run to collect colored paper leaves scattered around space
- **Red Light, Green Light**: Practice stopping and starting with control
- **Partner Running**: Side-by-side jogging with matching pace

**Cool Down (5 minutes)**
- Walking laps with deep breathing
- Gentle stretching

**French Vocabulary**: courir (to run), vite (fast), lentement (slowly), arrêt (stop)

---

### **Lesson 2: Skipping and Galloping Practice**
**Date**: October 9, 2025
**Location**: Gymnasium

**Warm-up (5 minutes)**
- Skip in place with exaggerated movements
- Practice step-hop pattern slowly

**Main Activity (20 minutes)**
- **Skipping Technique**: Step-hop on same foot, alternate sides
- **Galloping Horses**: Lead foot forward, trailing foot follows
- **Pony Express Relay**: Galloping between cone markers
- **Musical Skipping**: Skip to autumn-themed music, freeze when music stops

**Cool Down (5 minutes)**
- Slow walking with gentle stretching
- Balance activities on one foot

**French Vocabulary**: sautiller (to skip), galoper (to gallop), cheval (horse), musique (music)

---

### **Lesson 3: Jumping and Hopping Variations**
**Date**: October 11, 2025
**Location**: Gymnasium

**Warm-up (5 minutes)**
- Two-foot jumps in place
- Ankle circles and calf raises

**Main Activity (20 minutes)**
- **Two-Foot Jumping**: Over low obstacles, into hoops
- **One-Foot Hopping**: Hop on right foot, then left foot
- **Bunny Hop Obstacle Course**: Navigate through autumn-themed course
- **Frog Jumps**: Squat jumps with hands on ground
- **Kangaroo Hops**: Large forward jumps with arms

**Cool Down (5 minutes)**
- Gentle stretching focusing on legs
- Deep breathing exercises

**French Vocabulary**: sauter (to jump), sautiller (to hop), grenouille (frog), kangourou (kangaroo)

---

### **Lesson 4: Leaf Gathering Relay Races**
**Date**: October 14, 2025
**Location**: Outdoor field/Gymnasium

**Warm-up (5 minutes)**
- Nature walk around activity area
- Gentle jogging and stretching

**Main Activity (20 minutes)**
- **Leaf Collection Relay**: Teams gather different colored leaves (or paper leaves)
- **Autumn Treasure Hunt**: Find and collect specific items while moving with different locomotor skills
- **Rake and Run**: Use child-safe rakes to gather leaves, then run to deposit
- **Leaf Pile Jump**: Safe jumping into prepared leaf piles

**Cool Down (5 minutes)**
- Quiet nature observation
- Stretching under trees (if outdoors)

**French Vocabulary**: feuille (leaf), automne (autumn), ramasser (to gather), équipe (team)

---

### **Lesson 5: Sliding and Leaping Introduction**
**Date**: October 16, 2025
**Location**: Gymnasium

**Warm-up (5 minutes)**
- Side steps and lateral movements
- Dynamic warm-up with arm swings

**Main Activity (20 minutes)**
- **Sliding Technique**: Step-together-step pattern sideways
- **Animal Sliding**: Crab walk sideways, bear crawl side steps
- **Leaping Practice**: Run and leap over low obstacles
- **River Crossing**: Leap across "rivers" (mats or ropes)
- **Sideways Movement Games**: Musical chairs with sliding movements

**Cool Down (5 minutes)**
- Slow walking with controlled movements
- Balance challenges

**French Vocabulary**: glisser (to slide), sauter par-dessus (to leap over), côté (side), rivière (river)

## Assessment Strategies

### Formative Assessment
- Daily observation of locomotor skill development
- Partner cooperation and communication
- Following safety protocols
- French vocabulary usage

### Summative Assessment
- Demonstration of all locomotor skills
- Participation in cooperative activities
- Use of French movement vocabulary
- Safety awareness in outdoor activities

## Differentiation Strategies

### For Advanced Learners
- Complex movement sequences
- Leadership roles in partner activities
- Extended vocabulary challenges
- Creative movement combinations

### For Beginning Learners
- Simplified movement patterns
- Additional practice time
- Peer partner support
- Modified equipment (larger balls, lower obstacles)

### For English Language Learners
- Visual cues and demonstrations
- Bilingual peer partners
- Movement vocabulary cards
- Gesture-based communication

## Materials Needed
- Various sized soft balls and foam balls
- Hoops and cones for marking boundaries
- Low obstacles for jumping/leaping
- Autumn-themed props (leaves, "pumpkins")
- Music for movement activities
- Safety equipment for outdoor activities
- French vocabulary cards with pictures

## Cross-Curricular Connections
- **Science**: Seasonal changes, animal movements, weather patterns
- **French Language**: Movement vocabulary, directional commands
- **Mathematics**: Counting, patterns in movement, spatial awareness
- **Social Studies**: Cooperation, following rules, community activities

## Weather Contingency Plans
- Indoor alternatives for all outdoor activities
- Modified equipment for space constraints
- Adapted activities for different weather conditions
- Safety protocols for various environmental factors

---

**Unit Completion**: Students will demonstrate improved locomotor skills, seasonal movement awareness, basic ball handling abilities, and expanded French movement vocabulary while showing increased cooperation and safety consciousness.
