# Unit Plan: Ma famille et notre classe
## Grade 1 Social Studies | September 4 - October 4, 2025 | 20 Hours

### OVERVIEW
**Unit Title (French):** Ma famille et notre classe  
**Unit Title (English):** My Family and Our Class  
**Duration:** 5 weeks (20 hours total)  
**Primary Focus:** Identity, belonging, and community building with strong PEI cultural connections

### CURRICULUM EXPECTATIONS ADDRESSED
- **1ICC.1**: Décrire l'unicité des personnes et la diversité des langues et des modes de vie de sa famille et des familles de la classe
- **1ER.1**: Démontrer sa compréhension de ses besoins et ses désirs et ceux des autres

### BIG IDEAS
- Chaque personne est unique et spéciale
- Les familles sont différentes et merveilleuses  
- Nous avons tous des besoins et des désirs
- Notre classe est une communauté accueillante

---

## DETAILED IDENTITY ACTIVITIES

### Week 1: Qui suis-je? (Identity Foundation)

#### **All About Me Books Creation**
**Activity Duration:** 3 sessions (45 minutes each)

**Materials:**
- Blank booklets (8 pages each)
- Family photos from home
- Mirrors for self-portraits
- Crayons, markers, colored pencils
- Template pages with prompts

**Process:**
1. **Page 1: Mon visage** - Self-portrait with mirror observation
2. **Page 2: Ma famille** - Family photo and drawing
3. **Page 3: Ma maison** - Where I live (apartment, house, farm)
4. **Page 4: Mes langues** - Languages spoken at home
5. **Page 5: Mes traditions** - Special family celebrations
6. **Page 6: Mes talents** - What I'm good at
7. **Page 7: Mes rêves** - What I want to be/do
8. **Page 8: Ce qui me rend spécial** - Unique qualities

**PEI Connections:**
- Include "Mon Île" page showing connection to PEI
- Traditional Island symbols (lighthouse, potato fields, red shores)
- Island weather and seasons experiences

#### **Family Portrait Projects**
**Activity Duration:** 2 sessions (60 minutes each)

**Three Options for Family Representation:**
1. **Traditional Drawing:** Family members with labels
2. **Photo Collage:** Bringing family pictures to create display
3. **Digital Creation:** Using classroom tablets for family trees

**PEI Elements to Include:**
- Where family came from to live on PEI
- How long family has been on the Island
- Favorite PEI places family visits together
- Traditional Island foods family enjoys

---

### Week 2: Nos familles diverses (Family Diversity)

#### **Heritage Sharing Circles**
**Activity Duration:** Daily 20-minute circles for one week

**Circle Structure:**
- **Lundi:** Langues de ma famille (Family languages)
- **Mardi:** Traditions spéciales (Special traditions)
- **Mercredi:** Nourriture culturelle (Cultural foods)
- **Jeudi:** Fêtes et célébrations (Holidays and celebrations)
- **Vendredi:** Histoires familiales (Family stories)

**PEI Heritage Focus:**
- **Acadian Heritage:** Bonjour/Salut traditions, kitchen parties, traditional music
- **Mi'kmaq Heritage:** Welcome protocols, traditional teachings, seasonal celebrations
- **Scottish Heritage:** Highland Games connections, traditional music, clan symbols
- **Irish Heritage:** Storytelling traditions, music, cultural celebrations
- **Newcomer Heritage:** Journey stories to PEI, cultural bridges

#### **Cultural Integration Activities**

##### **Mi'kmaq Welcome Ceremonies (Culturally Appropriate)**
**Partnership Required:** Local Mi'kmaq cultural educator or Elder

**Activities:**
- Learning appropriate greeting protocols
- Understanding traditional territory acknowledgment
- Exploring concepts of extended family and community
- Seasonal celebration connections (harvest, winter preparation)
- Traditional games that build community

**Cultural Sensitivity Notes:**
- All activities led by or approved by Mi'kmaq educators
- Focus on shared values of community and respect
- Acknowledge traditional territory appropriately
- Connect to contemporary Mi'kmaq families on PEI

##### **Acadian Heritage Exploration**
**Partnership:** Acadian Museum of PEI or local Acadian community center

**Activities:**
- Traditional Acadian greetings and phrases
- Kitchen party concept - community gathering traditions
- Traditional foods and their significance
- Acadian flag and symbols meaning
- Connection to current Acadian families on PEI

##### **Newcomer Family Story Sharing**
**Inclusive Approach for New Islanders:**

**Activities:**
- Family journey maps showing travel to PEI
- Traditional foods from home countries
- Language sharing (counting 1-10 in home languages)
- Cultural clothing or artifact sharing
- Bridge-building between cultures

## COMMUNITY BUILDING ACTIVITIES

### Week 3: Notre classe, notre communauté

#### **Classroom Rules Co-Creation**
**Activity Duration:** 3 sessions (45 minutes each)

**Democratic Process:**
1. **Brainstorming Session:** What makes us feel safe and happy?
2. **Categorizing:** Grouping similar ideas together
3. **Consensus Building:** Agreeing on 5-7 core rules
4. **Visual Creation:** Making colorful rule posters
5. **Signing Ceremony:** All students and teacher sign classroom charter

**PEI Democratic Connections:**
- Understanding how PEI legislature makes decisions
- Local community meetings and decision-making
- Town halls and community input processes

#### **Conflict Resolution Practice**
**Activity Duration:** 2 sessions (30 minutes each)

**Scenarios Based on Grade 1 Experiences:**
- Sharing art supplies
- Playground equipment turn-taking
- Different opinions about games
- Including everyone in activities
- Helping when someone is upset

**Problem-Solving Steps:**
1. **Stop and breathe** (self-regulation)
2. **Listen to both sides** (empathy)
3. **Think of solutions together** (collaboration)
4. **Choose the best solution** (decision-making)
5. **Try it and check** (evaluation)

#### **Sharing Circle Routines**
**Daily Implementation:** 15 minutes each morning

**Weekly Themes:**
- **Monday:** Weekend stories and experiences
- **Tuesday:** Acts of kindness we noticed
- **Wednesday:** Challenges we solved
- **Thursday:** Things we're grateful for
- **Friday:** Celebrations and accomplishments

**Circle Guidelines:**
- Talking stick or special object
- "Listen with your heart and mind"
- Everyone has a turn (or can pass)
- What's shared in circle, stays in circle
- Respectful responses only

#### **Helping Hands Projects**
**Community Service Learning:**

**School-Based Projects:**
- Buddy reading with kindergarten classes
- Helping in school garden or greenhouse
- Creating welcome cards for new students
- Organizing classroom library
- Assisting with school recycling program

**Community Connections:**
- Cards for Manoir Belle Vue or QEH residents
- Food bank donation organization
- Beach or park cleanup participation
- Letters to Island firefighters or police
- Sharing garden vegetables with food bank

## PEI-SPECIFIC CONTENT INTEGRATION

### Week 4: Notre Île bien-aimée

#### **Island Families Throughout History**
**Historical Connections Activity:**

**Time Periods to Explore:**
1. **Mi'kmaq Families:** Traditional Island life, seasonal movements, family structures
2. **Early Acadian Families:** Settlement patterns, community cooperation, traditions
3. **Loyalist Families:** Coming to PEI, building new communities, heritage preservation
4. **Immigration Waves:** Irish, Scottish, and other groups building Island life
5. **Modern Families:** Contemporary diversity, newcomers, blended traditions

**Activities:**
- Historical family photos from PEI Museum collection
- Timeline creation showing different family groups
- Traditional homes comparison (wigwam, cottage, farmhouse, modern)
- Family occupation connections (farming, fishing, tourism, technology)

#### **Local Community Helpers**
**PEI-Specific Focus:**

**Community Helpers to Study:**
- **Island Farmers:** Potato, carrot, and vegetable growers
- **Fishers:** Lobster, mussel, and oyster harvesters  
- **Lighthouse Keepers:** Navigation and marine safety
- **Ferry Workers:** Connecting PEI to mainland
- **Tourism Guides:** Sharing Island heritage
- **Healthcare Workers:** Island hospitals and clinics
- **Teachers:** French immersion and English education
- **Municipal Workers:** Keeping communities running

**Guest Speaker Program:**
- Rotate weekly guest speakers from different professions
- Interactive demonstrations when possible
- Q&A sessions about their work helping the community
- Connection to family members who might have similar jobs

#### **PEI Landmarks and Features**
**Geography and Community Connection:**

**Key Landmarks to Explore:**
- **Confederation Bridge:** Connecting families across distances
- **Province House:** Where important decisions are made
- **Anne of Green Gables sites:** Tourism and cultural heritage
- **Red sand beaches:** Unique Island geography
- **Potato fields:** Agriculture and Island economy
- **Lighthouses:** Safety and navigation history
- **National Park:** Environmental protection and recreation

**Activities:**
- Create classroom map of PEI with important places
- Family connection mapping (where do relatives live?)
- Seasonal landmark changes (beaches in summer vs winter)
- Transportation connections between communities

#### **Cultural Celebrations on PEI**
**Year-Round Island Traditions:**

**Major Celebrations:**
- **Canada Day:** Charlottetown Founding Festival
- **Potato Blossom Festival:** Agricultural heritage celebration
- **Highland Games:** Scottish heritage and community gathering
- **Festival Acadien:** Acadian culture and music
- **Fall Flavours:** Food tourism and local harvest
- **Christmas traditions:** Light displays and community events

**Classroom Celebration Planning:**
- Mini-festival highlighting different PEI cultural groups
- Traditional games from different heritage backgrounds
- Food sharing representing Island diversity
- Music and dance from various cultural traditions

## ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Week 5: Celebrating Our Learning

#### **Identity Portfolio Development**
**Comprehensive Assessment Collection:**

**Portfolio Components:**
1. **All About Me Book** (completed and illustrated)
2. **Family Heritage Display** (photos, artifacts, stories)
3. **Classroom Contribution Record** (helping hands tracking)
4. **Conflict Resolution Success Stories** (written or drawn)
5. **PEI Connection Map** (personal landmarks and places)
6. **Community Helper Appreciation Cards** (thank you notes)

**Assessment Criteria:**
- **Communication:** Can explain personal identity and family uniqueness
- **Critical Thinking:** Can distinguish between needs and wants
- **Collaboration:** Participates positively in classroom community
- **Cultural Understanding:** Shows respect for diverse backgrounds
- **PEI Connection:** Demonstrates understanding of Island community

#### **Community Participation Rubrics**

**Participation Levels:**
- **Emerging:** Participates with support, beginning to share
- **Developing:** Participates independently, shares appropriately
- **Extending:** Takes leadership, supports others in participation

**Assessment Areas:**
- **Sharing Circle Participation:** Listening, sharing, responding respectfully
- **Collaborative Work:** Working well with others, including everyone
- **Conflict Resolution:** Using learned strategies, helping others
- **Cultural Sharing:** Respectful participation in heritage activities
- **Community Helping:** Contributing to classroom and school community

#### **Cultural Sharing Evaluations**

**Heritage Sharing Assessment:**
- **Preparation:** Brought materials, practiced presentation
- **Delivery:** Clear communication, proud sharing
- **Listening:** Respectful attention to others' sharing
- **Questions:** Appropriate, interested questions
- **Connections:** Found similarities and appreciated differences

#### **Belonging Indicators Checklist**

**Social-Emotional Indicators:**
- **Safety:** Feels safe to share personal information
- **Acceptance:** Accepts and celebrates differences in others
- **Contribution:** Finds ways to help and contribute
- **Connection:** Forms positive relationships with classmates
- **Pride:** Shows pride in own heritage and identity
- **Empathy:** Shows understanding of others' feelings and experiences

## DIFFERENTIATION STRATEGIES

### **For Emerging Learners:**
- **Visual Supports:** Picture cards, graphic organizers, visual schedules
- **Peer Partners:** Buddy system for activities and sharing
- **Simplified Choices:** Fewer options, concrete examples
- **Family Support:** Extra involvement from home
- **Modified Sharing:** Drawing instead of writing, short presentations

### **For Developing Learners:**
- **Structured Support:** Graphic organizers, sentence starters
- **Independent Work:** Some solo tasks, personal goal setting
- **Peer Collaboration:** Small group work, peer editing
- **Extension Options:** Additional research, deeper connections
- **Leadership Opportunities:** Helping emerging learners

### **For Extending Learners:**
- **Research Projects:** Independent investigation of family heritage
- **Teaching Roles:** Sharing expertise with classmates
- **Complex Connections:** Multi-generational family trees, cultural research
- **Community Outreach:** Connecting with local cultural organizations
- **Creative Expression:** Original songs, poems, or stories about identity

- **Level 1:** Can describe basic personal characteristics

- **Level 3:** Can compare and contrast family characteristics
- **Level 4:** Can analyze uniqueness and celebrate diversity

### **Community Participation (25%)**
- **Level 1:** Participates with significant support
- **Level 2:** Participates with some support
- **Level 3:** Participates independently and positively
- **Level 4:** Takes leadership in building community

### **Cultural Understanding (25%)**
- **Level 1:** Shows awareness of different cultures
- **Level 2:** Shows respect for cultural differences
- **Level 3:** Shows appreciation and interest in diversity
- **Level 4:** Makes connections between cultures and finds common ground

### **PEI Connection (25%)**
- **Level 1:** Recognizes PEI as home
- **Level 2:** Can name some PEI features and landmarks
- **Level 3:** Understands connection between families and Island community
- **Level 4:** Can explain how families contribute to PEI culture and community

## REFLECTION AND NEXT STEPS

### **Unit Reflection Questions:**
1. How has our understanding of family diversity grown?
2. What makes our classroom community special?
3. How are we connected to PEI and its heritage?
4. What have we learned about helping others?
5. How can we continue building a welcoming community?

### **Transition to Unit 2:**
This identity and community foundation prepares students for Unit 2: "Nos droits et responsabilités" by establishing:
- Strong classroom community relationships
- Understanding of individual and family uniqueness
- Appreciation for diversity and inclusion
- Skills for respectful communication and conflict resolution
- Connection to local community and Island heritage

The relationships built and cultural understanding developed in this unit will support all future social studies learning and cross-curricular connections throughout Grade 1.
