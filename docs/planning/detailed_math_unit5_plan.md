# Mathematics Unit 5: Measurement Exploration
## Explorer la mesure (Exploring Measurement)

### 📅 Unit Overview
- **Duration**: January 5-30, 2026 (4 weeks, 19 instructional hours)
- **Grade**: 1 French Immersion
- **Subject**: Mathématiques
- **Unit Focus**: Non-standard measurement, comparison of length/height/capacity, measurement vocabulary

### 🎯 Curriculum Expectations Covered
- **1.FE1**: Mesurer et comparer la longueur, la hauteur, la largeur et la distance en utilisant des unités non conventionnelles
(Measure and compare length, height, width, and distance using non-standard units)

---

## 📚 CONCRETE-PICTORIAL-ABSTRACT PROGRESSION

### Week 1 (Jan 5-9): CONCRETE Phase - Hands-On Measurement
**Focus**: Direct comparison and non-standard unit exploration

#### Specific Manipulatives & Activities:
- **Direct Comparison Exploration**:
  - Comparing student heights (Who is taller?)
  - Comparing book lengths (Which book is longer?)
  - Comparing classroom objects (desk vs. chair height)
  - Arranging items by length/height order

- **Non-Standard Unit Introduction**:
  - Paper clips for measuring length
  - Unifix cubes for height measurement
  - Hand spans for width measurement
  - Footsteps for distance measurement
  - Pencils for desk length measurement

- **Measurement Stations**:
  - Station 1: Measure with paper clips
  - Station 2: Measure with blocks
  - Station 3: Measure with string/yarn
  - Station 4: Compare heights with towers

#### Daily Timing Structure:
- **10 minutes**: Morning measurement routine (daily object to measure)
- **25 minutes**: Main measurement investigation
- **10 minutes**: Sharing measurement discoveries
- **5 minutes**: Measurement journal (recording findings)

### Week 2 (Jan 12-16): PICTORIAL Phase - Recording Measurements
**Focus**: Documenting measurement through drawings and representations

#### Measurement Recording Activities:
- **Measurement Charts**:
  - Create charts showing object + number of units
  - Draw pictures of items being measured
  - Use tally marks to record measurements
  - Compare measurements on pictorial charts

- **Measurement Maps**:
  - Draw classroom with relative sizes
  - Create treasure maps using step measurements
  - Illustrate measurement stories
  - Photo documentation of measurement activities

#### Visual Measurement Tools:
- **Measurement strips** (paper showing unit lengths)
- **Picture measurement cards** (visual reference for units)
- **Measurement journals** with drawings and numbers
- **Digital photos** of measurement investigations

### Week 3 (Jan 19-23): SYMBOLIC Connection - Recording with Numbers
**Focus**: Using numbers to represent measurement results

#### Number-Based Measurement:
- **Measurement equations**: "The desk = 8 paper clips"
- **Comparison statements**: "My height = 6 blocks"
- **Greater than/less than** with measurements
- **Number lines** showing measurement differences

#### Mathematical Thinking Activities:
- **Estimation before measuring**: "I think it will be ___ units"
- **Measurement predictions**: "What would be longer/shorter?"
- **Unit efficiency discussions**: "Which unit is better to use?"
- **Problem-solving with measurements**: "How many more blocks?"

### Week 4 (Jan 26-30): PRACTICE & CONSOLIDATION
**Focus**: Integrating all measurement approaches

#### Integrated Measurement Activities:
- **Measurement fair**: Students teach measurement to kindergarten
- **Real-world applications**: Measuring for classroom improvements
- **Measurement choices**: Students choose appropriate units
- **Assessment through authentic tasks**: Planning a garden bed

### Measurement Discussion Starters:
- "Combien d'unités?" (How many units?)
- "Quel objet est plus long?" (Which object is longer?)
- "Comment as-tu mesuré?" (How did you measure?)
- "Quelle unité as-tu utilisée?" (Which unit did you use?)
- "Est-ce plus ou moins?" (Is it more or less?)
- "Peux-tu estimer d'abord?" (Can you estimate first?)

### Math Journal Prompts in French:
- "J'ai mesuré ___ avec ___" (I measured ___ with ___)
- "L'objet le plus long est ___" (The longest object is ___)
- "Ma hauteur = ___ blocs" (My height = ___ blocks)
- "J'ai découvert que ___" (I discovered that ___)

## 🎯 DIFFERENTIATION STRATEGIES

### Support Level (Emerging Learners):
- **Simplified comparisons**: Focus on longer/shorter only
- **Larger units**: Use hand spans instead of paper clips
- **Partner support**: Work with measurement buddy
- **Visual supports**: Picture cards showing measurement vocabulary
- **Extra practice**: Additional concrete comparison activities
- **Guided recording**: Teacher helps with number writing

### Standard Level (Developing Learners):
- **Multiple unit types**: Paper clips, blocks, pencils
- **Independent measuring**: Measure and record solo
- **Estimation practice**: Guess first, then measure
- **French vocabulary**: Use measurement words in discussions
- **Journal writing**: Record discoveries with pictures + words

### Extension Level (Advanced Learners):
- **Multiple attributes**: Measure length AND width of objects
- **Unit efficiency**: Compare which units work best for different objects
- **Problem creation**: Design measurement challenges for classmates
- **Precise vocabulary**: Use specific measurement terms in French
- **Teaching role**: Help younger students with measurement
- **Investigation projects**: Explore why different units give different numbers
