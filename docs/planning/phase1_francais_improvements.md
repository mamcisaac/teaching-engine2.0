## 🔍 Unit-by-Unit Transformation

### Unit 1: Bienvenue à l'école! (September)
**New Focus:** Numbers 0-10, classroom vocabulary, phonological awareness with mathematical patterns

**Key Improvements:**
- **Specific Objective:** Students learn 20 essential classroom phrases + counting to 10
- **Math Integration:** Daily number talks in French, shape identification (cercle, carré), counting classroom supplies
- **Assessment:** 4-point scale for morning greetings, can identify initial sounds in 8/10 French words
- **Differentiation:** Individual picture cards for emerging, number cards 1-5 for developing, numbers 6-10 for extending

### Unit 2: Ma famille et moi (October) 
**New Focus:** Family vocabulary + numbers 11-20, mathematical family comparisons

**Key Improvements:**
- **Specific Objective:** 15 family terms + comparative language (plus que/moins que)
- **Math Integration:** Family graphing activities, number comparison, addition stories with family members
- **Assessment:** Family presentation rubric, running record with "Une famille ordinaire", math-French checklist
- **Differentiation:** Family photos with number labels, sentence frames with mathematical language

### Unit 3: Les fêtes d'automne (November-December)
**New Focus:** Writing process introduction + ordinal numbers + calendar mathematics

**Key Improvements:**
- **Specific Objective:** Complete 4-stage writing process, use ordinal numbers for sequencing
- **Math Integration:** Calendar mathematics (days in months), ordinal numbers for events, pattern work with autumn leaves
- **Assessment:** Writing process checklist, story retelling with sequence words, calendar mathematics assessment
- **Differentiation:** Picture writing with teacher scribe, sentence frames for celebrations, bilingual calendar creation

### Unit 4: L'hiver magique (January)
**New Focus:** Post-holiday consolidation + temperature/measurement + comparative language

**Key Improvements:**
- **Specific Objective:** Review numbers 1-20, learn temperature vocabulary, mathematical winter descriptions
- **Math Integration:** Daily temperature graphing, snow measurement in cm, symmetry in snowflakes, time duration calculations
- **Assessment:** Mid-year consolidation (numbers, vocabulary, phrases), winter journal with mathematical observations
- **Differentiation:** Visual thermometer support, temperature tracking choices, winter weather projects

### Unit 5: Nos amis les animaux (February)
**New Focus:** Critical thinking through animal classification + data collection + pictographs

**Key Improvements:**
- **Specific Objective:** Animal classification using mathematical criteria, create and interpret pictographs
- **Math Integration:** Animal data surveys → pictographs → bar graphs, measurement comparisons, skip counting by animal legs
- **Assessment:** Classification chart with mathematical criteria, pictograph creation/interpretation, informational writing with numerical evidence
- **Differentiation:** Animal sorting mats with manipulatives, guided pictograph creation, complex animal data tables

### Unit 6: Ma communauté (March)
**New Focus:** Community geography + mapping + spatial reasoning + coordinate systems

**Key Improvements:**
- **Specific Objective:** Create community maps with coordinates, calculate distances, write mathematical directions
- **Math Integration:** Coordinate graphing, distance measurement with rulers, 2D shape identification in buildings, directional language
- **Assessment:** Community map with accurate placement/scale, direction-giving performance task, shortest route calculations
- **Differentiation:** Simple grid maps with pictures, guided coordinate practice, complex route planning

### Unit 7: Le printemps en fleurs (April-May)
**New Focus:** Multiple writing genres + plant growth data + measurement + graphing

**Key Improvements:**
- **Specific Objective:** Master procedure, narrative, and informational writing with mathematical evidence
- **Math Integration:** Plant growth measurement/graphing, growth rate calculations, fraction work with plant parts, geometric patterns in nature
- **Assessment:** Multi-genre portfolio, math-writing integration assessment, metacognitive mathematical reflection
- **Differentiation:** Picture-supported procedures with numbered steps, choice in measurement tools, complex data displays

### Unit 8: Célébrons nos apprentissages (June)
**New Focus:** Data analysis of learning + quantified growth evidence + mathematical self-reflection

**Key Improvements:**
- **Specific Objective:** Analyze learning data, create growth graphs, present mathematical evidence of progress
- **Math Integration:** Year-long data analysis, vocabulary growth charts, reading progress graphs, quantified achievements
- **Assessment:** Portfolio conference with quantitative evidence, mathematical self-reflection presentation
- **Differentiation:** Visual timelines with number supports, choice in data presentation format, mathematical goal setting
