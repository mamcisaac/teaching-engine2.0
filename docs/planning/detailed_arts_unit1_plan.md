# 🎨 Arts Unit 1: Lines and Shapes Discovery
**Grade 1 French Immersion | September 4-30, 2025 | 14 Hours Total**

## 📚 Unit Overview

### Grandes idées / Big Ideas
- **FR**: Les lignes et les formes sont les éléments de base de tout art visuel
- **EN**: Lines and shapes are the basic building blocks of all visual art

### Questions essentielles / Essential Questions
- Comment les lignes et les formes nous entourent-elles dans notre monde?
- How do lines and shapes surround us in our world?
- Que pouvons-nous créer avec des lignes et des formes simples?
- What can we create with simple lines and shapes?

### Expectations Covered
- **AV1**: Reconnaître la valeur de son environnement visuel
- **AV2**: Utiliser la création artistique afin de communiquer des idées ou des sentiments
- **AV3**: Utiliser une variété d'outils, de matériaux, de styles et de techniques

---

## 🎯 Unit Success Criteria / Critères de réussite

### Je peux... / I can...
- ✅ **Identifier** différents types de lignes dans mon environnement
- ✅ **Reconnaître** les formes géométriques de base (cercle, carré, triangle, rectangle)

- ✅ **Utiliser** correctement les outils artistiques de base
- ✅ **Exprimer** mes idées à travers l'art visuel
- ✅ **Apprécier** l'art dans ma communauté et ma culture

## 🎨 SPECIFIC ART EXPLORATIONS

### 1. LINE WALKING AND DRAWING ACTIVITIES

#### **Promenade des lignes / Line Walk**
**Duration**: 45 minutes
- **Minds-On**: Explorer l'école pour trouver différents types de lignes
- **Action**: Dessiner les lignes trouvées avec le doigt dans l'air, puis sur papier
- **Consolidation**: Galerie de marche pour partager les découvertes

**Materials Needed**:
- Clipboards and paper
- Pencils and crayons
- Digital camera for documentation

#### **Ligne corporelle / Body Lines**
**Duration**: 30 minutes
- Use bodies to create different line types: straight, curved, zigzag, spiral
- Partner work to create line sculptures
- Photograph body lines for art portfolio

#### **Dessin de lignes aveugles / Blind Contour Drawing**
**Duration**: 45 minutes
- Draw self-portraits without looking at paper
- Focus on continuous line movement
- Celebrate the unique, expressive results

### 2. SHAPE HUNTING EXPEDITIONS

#### **Chasse aux formes / Shape Hunt**
**Duration**: 60 minutes
- **Indoor Hunt**: Classroom and school shapes identification
- **Outdoor Hunt**: Playground and natural shapes discovery
- **Documentation**: Shape photography and sketching journal

**Shape Focus by Week**:
- Week 1: Circles (cercles) - wheels, clocks, plates
- Week 2: Squares (carrés) - windows, books, tiles  
- Week 3: Triangles - rooftops, pizza slices, signs
- Week 4: Rectangles - doors, phones, tables

#### **Forme mystérieuse / Mystery Shape Game**
**Duration**: 30 minutes
- Tactile shape exploration in mystery bags
- Shape riddles in French
- Shape creation with play dough

### 3. TEXTURE RUBBINGS WITH NATURAL MATERIALS

#### **Frottages de texture naturelle / Natural Texture Rubbings**
**Duration**: 90 minutes (outdoor session)

**Materials Collection**:
- Tree bark varieties (rough, smooth, ridged)
- Leaves (different shapes and vein patterns)
- Stone textures (smooth river rocks, rough granite)
- Flower petals and pressed materials
- Fabric textures (burlap, silk, corduroy)

**Process**:
1. **Exploration Phase** (20 min): Touch and describe textures
2. **Collection Phase** (30 min): Gather safe natural materials
3. **Rubbing Creation** (30 min): Paper over texture, crayon rubbing
4. **Gallery Creation** (10 min): Display and appreciate variety

**French Vocabulary Development**:
- *Textures*: doux, rugueux, lisse, piquant, bosselé
- *Natural materials*: écorce, feuille, pierre, pétale, branche

### 4. COLOR MIXING INVESTIGATIONS

#### **Laboratoire de couleurs / Color Laboratory**
**Duration**: 3 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Couleurs primaires / Primary Colors**
- Red, blue, yellow exploration
- Primary color paintings
- Color emotion connections

**Session 2: Mélange magique / Magical Mixing**
- Red + Blue = Purple discovery
- Blue + Yellow = Green experiments  
- Yellow + Red = Orange investigations
- Record discoveries in color journals

**Session 3: Couleurs dans la nature / Colors in Nature**
- Nature color matching
- Seasonal color observations
- Create natural color palette

**Materials**:
- Washable tempera paints
- Large brushes and small detail brushes
- Mixing palettes (paper plates)
- Water containers and sponges
- Color theory wheels (simplified)

### 5. SELF-PORTRAIT PROJECTS

#### **Mon autoportrait artistique / My Artistic Self-Portrait**
**Duration**: 4 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Observation et esquisse / Observation and Sketch**
- Mirror self-observation
- Basic proportions discussion
- Pencil sketch creation

**Session 2: Lignes et formes du visage / Face Lines and Shapes**
- Identify face shapes (oval, round, square)
- Eye shapes, nose shapes, mouth shapes
- Add personal details and expressions

**Session 3: Couleurs personnelles / Personal Colors**
- Skin tone mixing practice
- Hair color exploration
- Eye color matching
- Clothing color choices

**Session 4: Détails et personnalité / Details and Personality**
- Add personal interests (pets, hobbies)
- Background design with favorite shapes
- Final touches and artist statement

## 🛠️ SKILL DEVELOPMENT ACTIVITIES

### 1. FINE MOTOR WARM-UPS
**Daily 10-minute activities**:

#### **Les doigts dansent / Dancing Fingers**
- Finger painting exercises
- Clay pinching and rolling
- Bead threading activities
- Scissor control practice

#### **Yoga artistique / Art Yoga**
- Hand and wrist stretches
- Shoulder relaxation
- Finger independence exercises
- Breathing for focus

### 2. BRUSH CONTROL EXERCISES

#### **École de pinceaux / Brush School**
**Week 1-2 focus**:
- Proper brush holding (like holding a bird)
- Brush loading techniques
- Different stroke types: dots, lines, broad strokes
- Brush care and cleaning

#### **Techniques de pinceau / Brush Techniques**
- **Pointillisme**: Dot painting technique
- **Traits longs**: Long smooth strokes
- **Coups secs**: Quick, confident marks
- **Mélange humide**: Wet-on-wet color blending

### 3. CUTTING AND PASTING PRACTICE

#### **Atelier découpage / Cutting Workshop**
**Progressive skill building**:
- Week 1: Straight line cutting
- Week 2: Curved line cutting  
- Week 3: Shape cutting (circles, squares)
- Week 4: Complex shape cutting

#### **Techniques de collage / Collage Techniques**
- Glue stick application (not too much!)
- Paper overlap methods
- Layering for depth
- Texture combination strategies

### 4. PENCIL GRIP DEVELOPMENT

#### **Prise de crayon parfaite / Perfect Pencil Grip**
**Daily reinforcement**:
- "Pinch and flip" technique
- Pencil grip aids when needed
- Alternative grip options for different needs
- Writing/drawing endurance building

### 5. TOOL EXPLORATION STATIONS

#### **Stations rotatives / Rotating Stations**
**20 minutes per station, 4 stations**:

**Station 1: Dessin / Drawing**
- Pencils, crayons, markers, pastels
- Different paper textures
- Blending and shading practice

**Station 2: Peinture / Painting**
- Watercolors, tempera paints
- Various brush sizes
- Sponge painting techniques

**Station 3: Construction / Building**
- Clay, play dough, modeling materials
- 3D shape creation
- Sculpting tools exploration

**Station 4: Collage / Mixed Media**
- Magazine cutouts, fabric scraps
- Natural materials, textured papers
- Adhesive options exploration

## 🌍 CULTURAL CONNECTIONS

### 1. MI'KMAQ BASKET PATTERNS

#### **Motifs de paniers mi'kmaq / Mi'kmaq Basket Patterns**
**Duration**: 3 sessions with cultural educator

**Session 1: Cultural Context**
- Mi'kmaq history and culture presentation
- Traditional basket making overview
- Pattern significance and meanings
- Respect and appreciation discussion

**Session 2: Pattern Analysis**
- Examine authentic Mi'kmaq baskets
- Identify recurring patterns and motifs
- Understand pattern symbolism
- Sketch pattern elements

**Session 3: Respectful Creation**
- Create artwork inspired by (not copying) patterns
- Use similar geometric elements
- Modern interpretation with traditional respect
- Artist statement about cultural appreciation

**Community Partnership**:
- Invite Mi'kmaq elder or artist
- Possible field trip to cultural center
- Authentic resource materials
- Ongoing cultural learning commitment
