# Detailed Health Unit 2 Plan: "Moi en santé"

## Unit Overview

**Unit Title:** Healthy Me / Moi en santé  
**Subject:** Formation personnelle et sociale (Health Education)  
**Grade:** 1  
**Duration:** October 14 - November 22, 2025 (18 hours total)  
**Linked Curriculum Expectation:** FPS1 - Démontrer sa compréhension des pratiques de santé personnelle

---

## Big Ideas & Essential Questions

### Big Ideas
- Healthy habits help us feel good and grow strong
- We can make choices that keep our bodies and minds healthy
- Taking care of ourselves shows self-respect and helps us thrive

### Essential Questions
- Comment puis-je garder mon corps en santé? (How can I keep my body healthy?)
- Quelles habitudes m'aident à me sentir bien? (What habits help me feel good?)
- Pourquoi la santé est-elle importante? (Why is health important?)

---

## Learning Goals & Success Criteria

### Learning Goals
Students will:
1. Understand and practice good hygiene habits
2. Recognize healthy food choices and understand nutrition basics
3. Appreciate the importance of physical activity and movement
4. Develop healthy sleep and rest routines
5. Learn illness prevention strategies
6. Build mental health and wellbeing practices

### Success Criteria
**Je peux...**
- pratiquer une bonne hygiène personnelle (practice good personal hygiene)
- faire des choix alimentaires sains (make healthy food choices)
- expliquer l'importance du sommeil et de l'exercice (explain the importance of sleep and exercise)
- identifier les moyens de prévenir la maladie (identify ways to prevent illness)
- utiliser des stratégies pour me sentir bien (use strategies to feel good)

## Weekly Breakdown (18 Hours)

### Week 1: Nutrition and Healthy Eating (October 14-18) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Understanding food groups and making healthy choices

#### Lesson 1.1: Food Groups Adventure (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Food rainbow hunt - find foods of every color
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Explore Canada's Food Guide with age-appropriate visuals
  - Sort foods into groups: fruits/vegetables, grains, proteins, dairy
  - Create balanced meal plates using food models
  - Discuss cultural foods from different families
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Plan one healthy snack for tomorrow

#### Lesson 1.2: Smart Food Choices (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** "Everyday" vs "Sometimes" food sorting
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about foods that give us energy vs treats
  - Practice reading simple food labels with pictures
  - Create healthy lunch box plans
  - Explore different cultural approaches to healthy eating
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Share one new healthy food to try this week

#### Lesson 1.3: Kitchen Safety Heroes (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Kitchen safety detective game
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Basic kitchen safety rules (hot/cold, sharp objects, clean hands)
  - Practice safe food handling with demonstrations
  - Explore food storage and why it matters
  - Simple cooking safety with adult supervision
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create personal kitchen safety badge

### Week 2: Physical Activity and Movement (October 21-25) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Understanding the importance of movement for health

#### Lesson 2.1: My Amazing Moving Body (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Heart rate exploration - feel pulse before and after jumping
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn how exercise makes our heart and muscles stronger
  - Explore different types of movement: running, dancing, sports
  - Try various physical activities to find favorites
  - Discuss how movement makes us feel happy
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Choose one new physical activity to try

#### Lesson 2.2: Active vs. Sedentary (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Movement charades - act out active and quiet activities
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Sort activities into "get moving" and "rest time" categories
  - Create daily movement schedule with balance
  - Explore active transportation (walking, biking)
  - Plan fun family movement activities
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Set daily movement goal for the week

#### Lesson 2.3: Strength and Flexibility Fun (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Animal movement yoga poses
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Practice simple strength exercises (wall push-ups, bear crawls)
  - Learn basic stretching for flexibility
  - Understand how strength and flexibility help us
  - Create personal fitness routine with favorite exercises
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Demonstrate favorite exercise for the class

### Week 3: Sleep and Rest (October 28 - November 1) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Understanding the importance of sleep and rest

#### Lesson 3.1: Why Sleep Matters (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Energy level check-in - how do you feel when tired vs rested?
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn how sleep helps our body grow and repair
  - Understand how sleep affects our mood and learning
  - Explore how much sleep Grade 1 students need
  - Compare sleep needs of different animals
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Draw what happens to your body during sleep

#### Lesson 3.2: Creating Healthy Sleep Routines (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Bedtime routine sequence activity
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Design personal bedtime routine with visual schedule
  - Practice calming activities: gentle stretches, deep breathing
  - Learn about sleep environment (dark, quiet, comfortable)
  - Create "bedtime buddy" comfort plan
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Commit to trying new bedtime routine

#### Lesson 3.3: Rest and Relaxation (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Guided relaxation with calming music
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn difference between sleep and rest
  - Practice quiet time activities for relaxation
  - Explore mindful breathing and gentle movement
  - Create personal "calm down" toolkit
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Practice favorite relaxation technique

### Week 4: Illness Prevention (November 4-8) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Learning to prevent illness and stay healthy

#### Lesson 4.1: Germ Busters (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Invisible germ experiment with glitter
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn about germs and how they spread
  - Master proper handwashing technique (20-second song)
  - Practice covering coughs and sneezes properly
  - Understand when to stay home from school
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create personal germ-fighting superhero identity

#### Lesson 4.2: When to Get Help (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Feeling thermometer - rate how you feel
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Recognize signs of feeling unwell
  - Practice communicating symptoms to adults
  - Learn about helpful medicines vs. things that aren't medicine
  - Explore when to see a doctor or nurse
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Role-play asking for help when not feeling well

#### Lesson 4.3: Staying Healthy Together (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Healthy classroom pledge
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn how to keep our classroom healthy
  - Practice sharing without spreading germs
  - Understand taking care of others when they're sick
  - Create classroom health helper roles
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Design healthy classroom poster

### Week 5: Mental Health and Wellbeing (November 11-15) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Building emotional wellness and stress management

#### Lesson 5.1: Stress Detectives (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Body scan - notice where you feel stress
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn what stress feels like in our bodies
  - Identify common Grade 1 stressors (new situations, conflicts)
  - Practice recognizing stress signals early
  - Explore how stress affects our health
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create personal stress signal awareness chart

#### Lesson 5.2: Calm Down Strategies (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Breathing buddies - stuffed animal breathing exercise
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn various calming strategies: deep breathing, counting, visualization
  - Practice progressive muscle relaxation for kids
  - Create personal calm down kit with favorite strategies
  - Explore mindful moments throughout the day
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Choose favorite calming strategy to practice daily

#### Lesson 5.3: Building Positive Mental Health (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Gratitude sharing circle
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Practice positive self-talk phrases in French and English
  - Learn about growth mindset ("I can't do this YET")
  - Explore activities that boost mood and confidence
  - Create daily habits for mental wellness
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Set one positive mental health goal

### Week 6: Celebrating Healthy Choices (November 18-22) - 2 hours
**Focus:** Integrating learning and celebrating wellness

#### Lesson 6.1: My Healthy Day Plan (2 hours)
- **Minds On (20 min):** Healthy habits reflection
- **Action (80 min):**
  - Create comprehensive "Healthy Day" timeline from wake-up to bedtime
  - Include all learned health practices: nutrition, movement, hygiene, rest
  - Present healthy day plans to families
  - Set health goals for the rest of the school year
  - Celebrate health learning journey
- **Consolidation (20 min):** Health hero graduation ceremony

## Trauma-Informed Teaching Approaches

### Creating Safety
- **Food security awareness:** Sensitive discussion of food access and family situations
- **Body positive language:** Inclusive approach to different body types and abilities
- **Cultural food practices:** Respect for diverse family food traditions
- **Non-judgmental health discussions:** Focus on choices, not criticism

### Sensitive Content Guidelines

#### Teaching About Illness Prevention
1. **Avoid fear-based messaging:** Focus on positive health practices rather than scary germs
2. **Cultural sensitivity:** Respect different approaches to health and medicine
3. **Family diversity:** Acknowledge different family structures and resources
4. **Accessibility:** Adapt for students with different physical abilities

#### Supporting Diverse Learners
- **Food allergies:** Careful attention to students with dietary restrictions
- **Economic considerations:** Sensitive to family financial situations
- **Physical limitations:** Adapt activities for students with mobility challenges
- **Sensory needs:** Accommodate students with sensory processing differences

## Assessment Rubric

### Health Knowledge and Understanding (FPS1)
**Level 4 (Exceeding):**
- Consistently demonstrates comprehensive understanding of health practices
- Makes independent healthy choices with clear reasoning
- Connects health learning to real-life situations confidently

**Level 3 (Meeting):**
- Shows good understanding of basic health concepts
- Makes healthy choices with occasional reminders
- Demonstrates most health practices independently

**Level 2 (Approaching):**
- Shows developing understanding of health concepts
- Makes healthy choices with regular guidance
- Demonstrates health practices with support

**Level 1 (Below):**
- Shows limited understanding of health concepts
- Requires significant support for healthy choices
- Needs direct instruction for health practices

### Health Practices Application
**Level 4:** Independently applies all health practices and teaches others
**Level 3:** Consistently demonstrates health practices with occasional reminders
**Level 2:** Demonstrates health practices with regular prompting and support
**Level 1:** Requires direct instruction and assistance for health practice completion

### Health Goal Setting and Self-Regulation
**Level 4:** Sets realistic health goals and monitors progress independently
**Level 3:** Sets appropriate health goals with minimal support
**Level 2:** Sets basic health goals with guidance and reminders
**Level 1:** Requires significant support for goal setting and progress monitoring
