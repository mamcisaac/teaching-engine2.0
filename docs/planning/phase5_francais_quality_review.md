### 5. ASSESSMENT AND DIFFERENTIATION (25/25 points)

#### **Outstanding Assessment Design:**
✅ **Authentic Assessment Strategies**
- Daily observations with systematic rotation (5 students/day)
- Portfolio development with student choice and reflection
- Performance-based assessment through presentations and celebrations
- Self-assessment tools appropriate for Grade 1 (emoji scales, visual thermometers)

✅ **Comprehensive Differentiation**
- **For Anxiety/Transitions**: Comfort objects, predictable routines, emotional support
- **For Non-francophones**: Visual supports, gestural communication, bilingual bridges
- **For Advanced Learners**: Leadership roles, complex projects, mentoring opportunities
- **For IEP Needs**: Sensory adaptations, attention supports, communication alternatives

✅ **Trauma-Informed Approaches**
- September anxiety strategies comprehensive and sensitive
- Emotional regulation tools integrated throughout
- Choice and agency provided in participation formats
- Cultural sensitivity in all family discussions

#### **Assessment Excellence:**
- Four-point rubrics developmentally appropriate
- Clear success criteria for students and parents
- Multiple ways to demonstrate learning
- Growth-focused rather than deficit-based

### 6. GAPS AND IMPROVEMENT AREAS (15/20 points)

- Could leverage more interactive digital tools for Grade 1

🔶 **Cross-Curricular Connections**

- Global citizenship concepts could be expanded

🔶 **Assessment Documentation**

🔶 **Practical Implementation**

#### **Recommendations for Enhancement:**

1. **Technology Integration**: Develop specific apps/tools for each unit
2. **Documentation**: Create detailed tracking systems for teacher ease
3. **Resource Management**: Provide alternative material suggestions
4. **Professional Development**: Include cultural competency support materials
