# Phase 3: Music Integration - Making Music the Heartbeat of Learning

**Mission**: Create musical connections and rhythm across ALL Grade 1 subjects in French Immersion

**Context**: PEI Grade 1 French Immersion - 8 music expectations serving as universal learning enhancement

## Current Music Expectations (Foundation)

### Creative Composition (CC)
- **CC 1.1**: Create music using voice, body, and instruments through musical play
- **CC 1.2**: Compose simple musical ideas using invented notations, standard notations, and technology

### Musical Elements (ME)
- **ME 1**: Demonstrate the elements of music through musical play

### Musical Arts (MA)
- **MA 1.1**: Demonstrate proper technique playing pitched and non-pitched percussion instruments
- **MA 1.2**: Demonstrate voice in a variety of contexts through musical play

### Cultural Connections (CCC)
- **CCC 1**: Demonstrate understanding of diverse musical genres, styles, and cultural contexts

### Sharing Performance (SP)
- **SP 1**: Perform musical pieces for a variety of audiences

### Reflection/Response/Analysis (RRA)
- **RRA 1**: Refine live and recorded performances using the creative musical process

## Cross-Curricular Integration Harmonies

### 🎵 Music + Français (Immersion) - "Chantons en français"

**Connections with French Language Expectations:**

#### Phonological Awareness (1CO.0)
- **Musical Integration**: Use rhythm patterns to teach French phonemes
- **Specific Activities**:
  - Clapping syllables in French words while singing
  - "Chanson des sons" - songs for each French sound
  - Rhythmic pronunciation practice: "Ba-ba-ba-nan-ne" (CC 1.1)

#### Listening Comprehension (1CO.1-1CO.4)
- **Musical Integration**: French songs as listening comprehension vehicles
- **Specific Songs**:
  - "Bonjour mes amis" - daily greeting song
  - "Les jours de la semaine" - days of the week chant
  - "Comptons ensemble" - counting in French with melody
- **Cross-Curriculum Code**: CC 1.1 + 1CO.1

#### Oral Expression (1CO.5)
- **Musical Integration**: Performance builds confidence in French speaking
- **Activities**:
  - French nursery rhymes with actions
  - Call-and-response songs in French
  - "Show and Tell" presentations with musical introductions (SP 1)

### 🎵 Music + Mathématiques - "Les mathématiques rythmées"

**Connections with Math Expectations:**

#### Number Recognition & Counting
- **Musical Integration**: Counting songs in French and English
- **Specific Songs**:
  - "Un, deux, trois, nous voilà" (1-10 counting song)
  - "Les dix petits doigts" (finger counting with rhythm)
  - Skip counting by 2s, 5s, 10s with clapping patterns (ME 1)

#### Patterns & Relationships
- **Musical Integration**: Musical patterns mirror mathematical patterns
- **Activities**:
  - ABAB rhythm patterns with instruments (MA 1.1)
  - Growing patterns: clap-stomp, clap-clap-stomp, clap-clap-clap-stomp
  - Pattern composition using body percussion (CC 1.2)

#### Measurement & Time
- **Musical Integration**: Beat as time measurement
- **Activities**:
  - "La chanson du temps" - telling time through songs
  - Measuring song duration with counting
  - Fast/slow tempo comparisons (ME 1)

### 🎵 Music + Sciences de la nature - "La symphonie de la nature"

**Connections with Science Expectations:**

#### Sound Investigation
- **Musical Integration**: Exploring sound properties through music
- **Activities**:
  - "Les sons autour de nous" - identifying environmental sounds
  - Making instruments from natural materials (CC 1.1)
  - High/low pitch experiments with homemade instruments (MA 1.1)

#### Seasonal Changes
- **Musical Integration**: Seasonal songs and nature sounds
- **Specific Songs**:
  - "L'automne arrive" - fall sounds and colors
  - "L'hiver blanc" - winter sound effects
  - "Le printemps chante" - spring awakening sounds
- **Activities**: Sound mapping outdoor environments (CC 1.2)

#### Animal Studies
- **Musical Integration**: Animal sounds and movements
- **Activities**:
  - "La ferme musicale" - farm animal sound symphony
  - Movement songs about different animals
  - Creating animal rhythm patterns (ME 1)

### 🎵 Music + Sciences humaines - "Notre communauté chante"

**Connections with Social Studies Expectations:**

#### Cultural Heritage (CCC 1)
- **Mi'kmaq Cultural Music**:
  - Traditional drumming rhythms (respectfully taught)
  - "Honor Song" listening and appreciation
  - Indigenous instrument exploration
- **Acadian Cultural Music**:
  - "Evangeline" traditional melodies
  - Acadian folk songs and fiddle music
  - Community celebration songs

#### Family & Community
- **Musical Integration**: Songs about family and community helpers
- **Specific Songs**:
  - "Ma famille" - family member identification song
  - "Les métiers" - community helpers with actions
  - "Notre école" - school community song (SP 1)

#### Celebrations & Traditions
- **Musical Integration**: Holiday and seasonal celebration music
- **Activities**:
  - Christmas carols in French and English
  - Birthday celebration songs
  - Harvest festival music

### 🎵 Music + Arts visuels - "Créer avec tous nos sens"

**Connections with Visual Arts Expectations:**

#### Color & Visual Elements
- **Musical Integration**: Colors have musical personalities
- **Activities**:
  - "La chanson des couleurs" - each color has its own sound
  - Painting to different musical styles
  - Creating visual representations of musical pieces (CC 1.2)

#### Creative Expression
- **Musical Integration**: Multi-sensory art creation
- **Activities**:
  - Drawing while listening to different genres
  - Making instruments that are also visual art
  - Costume design for musical performances (SP 1)

#### Art Appreciation
- **Musical Integration**: Connecting visual and musical arts
- **Activities**:
  - Listening to music inspired by famous paintings
  - Creating art gallery "soundtrack" compositions
  - Musical interpretation of different art styles

### 🎵 Music + Éducation physique - "Bouger en musique"

**Connections with Physical Education Expectations:**

#### Movement & Coordination
- **Musical Integration**: Rhythm develops motor skills
- **Activities**:
  - "Marche et arrête" - stop and go with musical cues
  - Obstacle courses set to different tempos
  - Balancing activities with steady beat (ME 1)

#### Dance & Expression
- **Musical Integration**: Dance as musical expression
- **Activities**:
  - Folk dances from different cultures (CCC 1)
  - Creative movement to various musical styles
  - Partner dances for cooperation skills (MA 1.2)

#### Games & Cooperation
- **Musical Integration**: Musical games build teamwork
- **Activities**:
  - "Musical chairs" variations that include everyone
  - Rhythm passing games in circles
  - Team composition projects (CC 1.1)

### 🎵 Music + Formation personnelle et sociale - "Grandir ensemble en musique"

**Connections with Personal & Social Development:**

#### Emotional Regulation
- **Musical Integration**: Music as emotional expression and regulation
- **Activities**:
  - "Les émotions en musique" - songs for different feelings
  - Calming music for transitions and rest time
  - Energizing music for active times (MA 1.2)

#### Social Skills & Cooperation
- **Musical Integration**: Ensemble playing teaches cooperation
- **Activities**:
  - Small group rhythm ensembles
  - Taking turns as musical leaders
  - Listening and responding to others in musical conversations (RRA 1)

#### Self-Expression & Confidence
- **Musical Integration**: Performance builds self-esteem
- **Activities**:
  - Solo performance opportunities (age-appropriate)
  - Sharing personal musical creations
  - Celebrating individual musical strengths (SP 1)

## Assessment Integration

### Musical Portfolio Assessment
- **Monthly recordings** of integrated performances
- **Cross-curricular projects** combining music with other subjects
- **Reflection journals** about musical learning connections
- **Performance documentation** across all subject areas

### Formative Assessment Strategies
- **Musical exit tickets**: Humming the day's learning
- **Rhythm checks**: Clapping back subject-specific patterns
- **Song summary**: Creating songs about learned concepts
- **Musical peer feedback**: Students helping each other improve

### Culminating Performance Opportunities
- **Monthly showcases** featuring cross-curricular music
- **Subject-specific concerts**: Music highlighting other subject learning
- **Community performances**: Sharing integrated learning with families
- **Digital portfolios**: Recording growth across all subjects
