# Mathematics Unit 6: Mental Math Strategies
## Stratégies de calcul mental (Mental Math Strategies)

### 📅 Unit Overview
- **Duration**: February 2-27, 2026 (4 weeks, 18 instructional hours)
- **Grade**: 1 French Immersion
- **Subject**: Mathématiques
- **Unit Focus**: Mental math strategies, equality concepts, developing computational fluency

### 🎯 Curriculum Expectations Covered
- **1.N9**: Démontrer une compréhension de l'addition (jusqu'à 20) et de la soustraction (jusqu'à 10) en utilisant diverses stratégies personnelles informelles
(Demonstrate understanding of addition to 20 and subtraction to 10 using various personal informal strategies)

- **1.RR3**: Identifier l'égalité et l'inégalité en utilisant la balance
(Identify equality and inequality using balance)

## 📚 CONCRETE-PICTORIAL-ABSTRACT PROGRESSION

### Week 1 (Feb 2-6): CONCRETE Phase - Strategy Development
**Focus**: Building mental math strategies through manipulative experiences

#### Specific Manipulatives & Strategy Building:
- **Doubles Strategy Foundation**:
  - Physical doubles activities (2 towers of same height)
  - Mirror work (reflecting manipulative arrangements)
  - Body doubles (clap-clap patterns, jump-jump sequences)
  - Edible doubles (crackers, grapes in matching groups)

- **Counting On Strategy**:
  - Starting with larger number (concrete demonstration)
  - Using number lines with physical movement
  - "Hide and count" games with manipulatives
  - Finger counting strategies with visual supports

- **Make 10 Strategy Introduction**:
  - Ten frames with loose parts (beans, counters)
  - Two-color beans exploration (7 red + 3 blue = 10)
  - Building to 10 with different materials
  - Ten frame addition activities

#### Equality Exploration with Balance:
- **Physical balance scales**:
  - Equal groups on both sides
  - Adding/removing to maintain balance
  - Discovering what makes balance work
  - Recording balance discoveries

#### Daily Timing Structure:
- **10 minutes**: Mental math warm-up (number talks)
- **25 minutes**: Strategy investigation and practice
- **10 minutes**: Strategy sharing and discussion
- **5 minutes**: Mental math journal (strategy recording)

### Week 2 (Feb 9-13): PICTORIAL Phase - Visual Strategy Representations
**Focus**: Drawing and representing mental math strategies

#### Visual Strategy Documentation:
- **Strategy Drawing Activities**:
  - Drawing doubles (2 groups of same amount)
  - Number line jumping for counting on
  - Ten frame pictures showing make-10 strategies
  - Before/after pictures showing balance states

- **Mathematical Illustrations**:
  - Problem-solving through pictures
  - Story problems with visual solutions
  - Strategy comparison charts
  - Student-created strategy posters

#### Balance Representation:
- **Drawing balance scales** showing equality/inequality
- **Recording equations** from balance discoveries
- **Visual equality statements** (pictures = pictures)
- **Inequality representations** (more than/less than visuals)

### Week 3 (Feb 16-20): SYMBOLIC Connection - Number Representations
**Focus**: Connecting strategies to number equations and symbols

#### Strategy Equations:
- **Doubles equations**: 4 + 4 = 8, 3 + 3 = 6
- **Near doubles**: 4 + 5 = 4 + 4 + 1 = 9
- **Counting on equations**: 6 + 3 = 6, 7, 8, 9
- **Make 10 equations**: 7 + 3 = 10, 8 + 2 = 10

#### Equality and Inequality Symbols:
- **Equal sign introduction** (=) as "the same as"
- **Not equal exploration** (≠) as "not the same as"
- **Balance equations**: 5 + 2 = 3 + 4
- **True/false number statements**

#### Mathematical Thinking Activities:
- **Number talks**: "How did you solve 6 + 4?"
- **Strategy debates**: "Which way is easier and why?"
- **Mental math challenges**: Timed practice with strategies
- **Equation creation**: Students make their own problems

### Week 4 (Feb 23-27): PRACTICE & INTEGRATION
**Focus**: Applying all mental math strategies fluently

#### Integrated Strategy Activities:
- **Strategy choice**: Students pick best method for each problem
- **Speed and accuracy**: Fluency building with chosen strategies
- **Real-world application**: Mental math in daily situations
- **Peer teaching**: Students explain strategies to others

#### Assessment Integration:
- **Strategy interviews**: Individual conferences about methods
- **Portfolio assembly**: Collecting strategy evidence
- **Self-assessment**: Students evaluate their strategy preferences
- **Goal setting**: Planning next steps in mental math development

### Strategy-Specific Vocabulary:
- **Compter en continuant** (Counting on) - [kom-TAY ahn kon-tee-new-AHN]
- **Faire dix** (Make 10) - [FEHR DEES]
- **Presque doubles** (Near doubles) - [PRESK DOO-bluh]
- **Décomposer** (Break apart) - [day-kom-po-ZAY]
- **Équilibre** (Balance) - [ay-kee-LEE-bruh]
- **Même quantité** (Same amount) - [MEM kan-tee-TAY]

### Mental Math Discussion Starters:
- "Quelle stratégie utilises-tu?" (Which strategy do you use?)
- "Comment penses-tu dans ta tête?" (How do you think in your head?)
- "Peux-tu expliquer ta méthode?" (Can you explain your method?)
- "Y a-t-il une façon plus rapide?" (Is there a faster way?)
- "Comment sais-tu que c'est égal?" (How do you know it's equal?)
- "Qu'est-ce qui est plus facile pour toi?" (What is easier for you?)

### Math Journal Prompts in French:
- "Ma stratégie préférée est ___ parce que ___" (My favorite strategy is ___ because ___)
- "Quand je vois 6 + 4, je pense ___" (When I see 6 + 4, I think ___)
- "Les doubles que je connais sont ___" (The doubles I know are ___)
- "Pour équilibrer, j'ai besoin de ___" (To balance, I need ___)

## 🎯 DIFFERENTIATION STRATEGIES

### Support Level (Emerging Learners):
- **Smaller numbers**: Focus on sums to 10 only
- **Single strategy**: Master one strategy (like doubles) first
- **Extended concrete**: More time with manipulatives before pictorial
- **Guided practice**: Teacher works alongside for strategy development
- **Visual supports**: Strategy anchor charts with pictures and words
- **Simplified vocabulary**: Basic French terms for addition and equality

### Standard Level (Developing Learners):
- **Multiple strategies**: Learn 3-4 different mental math strategies
- **Sums to 20**: Work with full range of Grade 1 addition
- **Strategy choice**: Begin choosing best strategy for different problems
- **Independent practice**: Solo work with strategies and balance
- **French integration**: Use strategy vocabulary in discussions
- **Self-assessment**: Reflect on strategy effectiveness

### Extension Level (Advanced Learners):
- **Complex problems**: Two-digit addition with mental strategies
- **Strategy efficiency**: Compare and contrast different approaches
- **Teaching others**: Explain strategies to classmates
- **Problem creation**: Design problems that use specific strategies
- **Advanced vocabulary**: Sophisticated French mathematical discourse
- **Investigation projects**: Research real-world applications of mental math

## 🎯 SUCCESS CRITERIA - "Je peux..."

### Mental Math Strategy Mastery:
- Je peux utiliser la stratégie des doubles (4+4, 5+5, etc.)
- Je peux compter en continuant à partir du plus grand nombre
- Je peux utiliser "faire dix" pour additionner facilement
- Je peux résoudre des "presque doubles" (6+7 comme 6+6+1)
- Je peux choisir la meilleure stratégie pour différents problèmes

### Computational Fluency:
- Je peux résoudre les faits d'addition jusqu'à 20 rapidement
- Je peux résoudre les faits de soustraction jusqu'à 10 précisément
- Je peux vérifier mes réponses avec différentes stratégies
- Je peux corriger mes erreurs de calcul mental
- Je peux expliquer comment j'ai trouvé ma réponse

### Equality Understanding:
- Je peux utiliser la balance pour montrer l'égalité
- Je peux créer des équations vraies avec =
- Je peux trouver le nombre manquant dans une équation
- Je peux expliquer ce que "égal" signifie
- Je peux reconnaître quand deux quantités ne sont pas égales

### French Mathematical Communication:
- Je peux expliquer mes stratégies de calcul mental en français
- Je peux utiliser le vocabulaire des mathématiques en français
- Je peux poser des questions sur les stratégies en français
- Je peux participer aux discussions mathématiques en français
- Je peux enregistrer ma pensée mathématique en français
