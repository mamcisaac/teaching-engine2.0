# Detailed Health Unit 4 Plan: "Amis et sentiments"

## Unit Overview

**Unit Title:** Friends and Feelings / Amis et sentiments  
**Subject:** Formation personnelle et sociale (Health Education)  
**Grade:** 1  
**Duration:** January 13 - February 28, 2026 (18 hours total)  
**Linked Curriculum Expectation:** FPS2 - Démontrer sa compréhension des relations interpersonnelles positives

---

## Big Ideas & Essential Questions

### Big Ideas
- Healthy friendships are built on respect, kindness, and understanding
- Everyone has complex emotions that deserve recognition and validation
- Communication skills help us build strong relationships with others
- Conflict is normal in relationships and can be resolved peacefully
- We can learn to understand and support others while maintaining our own boundaries

### Essential Questions
- Qu'est-ce qui fait un bon ami? (What makes a good friend?)
- Comment puis-je exprimer mes sentiments de manière appropriée? (How can I express my feelings appropriately?)
- Comment puis-je résoudre les conflits avec mes amis? (How can I resolve conflicts with my friends?)
- Comment puis-je aider les autres quand ils sont tristes? (How can I help others when they are sad?)

---

## Learning Goals & Success Criteria

### Learning Goals
Students will:
1. Identify qualities of healthy friendships and positive relationships
2. Develop empathy and understanding for others' emotions and experiences
3. Practice effective communication skills including active listening
4. Learn peaceful conflict resolution strategies appropriate for Grade 1
5. Build emotional vocabulary and regulation strategies
6. Understand inclusion, diversity, and respect for differences

### Success Criteria
**Je peux...**
- décrire les qualités d'un bon ami (describe the qualities of a good friend)
- reconnaître et nommer des émotions complexes (recognize and name complex emotions)
- écouter activement quand les autres parlent (actively listen when others speak)
- résoudre des conflits de manière pacifique (resolve conflicts peacefully)
- inclure les autres et célébrer les différences (include others and celebrate differences)
- demander de l'aide quand j'en ai besoin (ask for help when I need it)

## Weekly Breakdown (18 Hours)

### Week 1: What Makes a Good Friend? (January 13-17) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Exploring friendship qualities and relationship building

#### Lesson 1.1: Friendship Recipe (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Friendship web activity - share names of friends
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Create a "recipe" for good friendship using ingredients like kindness, sharing, listening
  - Explore different types of friendships: school friends, neighborhood friends, family friends
  - Read bilingual books about friendship (French/English)
  - Practice friendship skills through role-play scenarios
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Draw one friendship quality to practice this week

#### Lesson 1.2: Making New Friends (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** "Getting to know you" speed introductions
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn conversation starters in French and English
  - Practice introducing themselves and asking questions about others
  - Explore how to join group activities respectfully
  - Discuss overcoming shyness and building confidence
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Set a goal to talk to someone new this week

#### Lesson 1.3: Friendship Challenges (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** "What if..." friendship scenario cards
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Identify common friendship problems (feeling left out, disagreements, misunderstandings)
  - Practice using "I" statements to express feelings
  - Learn the difference between tattling and reporting
  - Explore when to ask adults for help with friendship problems
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create a friendship problem-solving poster

### Week 2: Understanding Big Emotions (January 20-24) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Expanding emotional vocabulary and awareness

#### Lesson 2.1: The Feeling Wheel (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Emotion intensity thermometer activity
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Explore complex emotions beyond happy/sad/angry (frustrated, disappointed, excited, nervous)
  - Create personalized feeling wheels with French and English emotion words
  - Practice identifying emotions in literature characters and peers
  - Learn that multiple emotions can exist simultaneously
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Choose three new emotion words to practice using

#### Lesson 2.2: Emotion Regulation Toolbox (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Calming strategies brainstorm
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Expand toolkit with new strategies: progressive muscle relaxation, guided imagery, mindful movement
  - Practice different breathing techniques (balloon breath, flower breath, ocean breath)
  - Create personal emotion regulation plans with visual supports
  - Learn to recognize early warning signs of emotional overwhelm
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Practice chosen calming strategy with a partner

#### Lesson 2.3: Empathy Building (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** "How would you feel?" picture scenarios
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Practice perspective-taking through stories and role-play
  - Learn to recognize emotions in others through facial expressions and body language
  - Explore how our actions affect others' feelings
  - Discuss different cultural expressions of emotions
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Share one way to show empathy to someone today

### Week 3: Communication Champions (January 27-31) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Developing effective communication skills

#### Lesson 3.1: Active Listening Superheroes (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Good listener vs. poor listener demonstration
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn active listening skills: eye contact, body facing speaker, asking questions
  - Practice summarizing what others say using "I heard you say..."
  - Explore non-verbal communication and its impact
  - Play listening games and activities to build skills
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create active listening superhero badges

#### Lesson 3.2: Speaking Up and Speaking Kind (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Kind words vs. hurtful words sorting activity
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Practice assertive communication using respectful tone and words
  - Learn when and how to say "no" to things that don't feel right
  - Explore giving and receiving compliments appropriately
  - Practice asking for what they need clearly and politely
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Partner practice giving genuine compliments

#### Lesson 3.3: Non-Verbal Communication (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Emotion charades - guess feelings without words
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Explore how body language, facial expressions, and tone communicate messages
  - Practice matching non-verbal communication with verbal messages
  - Learn about personal space and appropriate physical boundaries
  - Discuss cultural differences in non-verbal communication
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Practice greeting someone using positive body language

### Week 4: Conflict Resolution Heroes (February 3-7) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Learning to handle disagreements peacefully

#### Lesson 4.1: When Friends Disagree (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Conflict scenario sorting - big problems vs. small problems
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Normalize that all relationships have conflicts sometimes
  - Learn the difference between conflict and bullying
  - Practice using "stop, think, talk" strategy for conflict resolution
  - Explore different perspectives using simple mediation techniques
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Role-play resolving a classroom disagreement

#### Lesson 4.2: Problem-Solving Partners (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Problem-solving steps poster creation
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn win-win problem solving: both people get something they need
  - Practice compromise and finding middle ground solutions
  - Use "peace table" approach for structured conflict resolution
  - Create classroom agreements for handling disagreements
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Sign class peace agreement and practice handshake

#### Lesson 4.3: When to Get Help (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Help-seeking scenarios - when is adult help needed?
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Identify situations requiring adult intervention vs. peer resolution
  - Practice asking teachers, parents, and other adults for support
  - Learn about reporting vs. tattling distinction
  - Explore what to do if someone is being mean or hurtful
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create "help-seeking" plan with trusted adults identified

### Week 5: Inclusion and Diversity Celebration (February 10-14) - 4.5 hours
**Focus:** Building inclusive communities and celebrating differences

#### Lesson 5.1: Everyone Belongs Here (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** "What makes us unique?" sharing circle
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Explore how differences make communities stronger and more interesting
  - Practice inclusive language and behaviors
  - Learn to interrupt exclusion and invite others to join activities
  - Discuss different abilities, cultures, languages, and family structures
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create inclusion promises for classroom community

#### Lesson 5.2: Standing Up for Others (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Upstander vs. bystander role-play
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn age-appropriate ways to support peers who are being excluded or treated unfairly
  - Practice saying "That's not OK" and getting adult help
  - Explore how to be an ally to classmates who are different from them
  - Role-play intervention scenarios appropriate for Grade 1
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Create upstander action plan with simple strategies

#### Lesson 5.3: Kindness Spreaders (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Random acts of kindness brainstorm
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Plan and execute kindness projects for classroom and school community
  - Create kindness journals to track giving and receiving kindness
  - Explore how kindness affects the giver and receiver
  - Practice noticing and acknowledging kindness in others
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Commit to one daily act of kindness

### Week 6: Relationship Maintenance and Growth (February 17-21) - 2.5 hours
**Focus:** Maintaining friendships and building relationship skills

#### Lesson 6.1: Friendship Check-ups (1.5 hours)
- **Minds On (15 min):** Friendship health check using visual scale
- **Action (60 min):**
  - Learn how to repair friendships after disagreements or misunderstandings
  - Practice apologizing sincerely and accepting apologies gracefully
  - Explore how to maintain friendships even when apart
  - Discuss what to do when friendships change or end
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Write letter of appreciation to a friend

#### Lesson 6.2: Building Our Friendship Community (1 hour)
- **Minds On (10 min):** Community building warm-up activity
- **Action (35 min):**
  - Plan and implement friendship celebration for classroom
  - Create friendship awards highlighting each student's unique contribution to class community
  - Establish ongoing friendship support systems
  - Set class goals for maintaining positive relationships
- **Consolidation (15 min):** Friendship community commitment ceremony

### Week 7: Integration and Celebration (February 24-28) - 2 hours
**Focus:** Integrating learning and celebrating relationship growth

#### Lesson 7.1: Friendship and Feelings Showcase (2 hours)
- **Minds On (20 min):** Reflection on relationship growth since January
- **Action (80 min):**
  - Present friendship portfolios to families showcasing learning and growth
  - Demonstrate conflict resolution and communication skills
  - Share emotion regulation strategies and empathy development
  - Celebrate diverse friendship styles and inclusive community building
- **Consolidation (20 min):** Circle of gratitude for friendship learning journey

## Trauma-Informed Teaching Approaches

### Creating Emotional Safety
- **Predictable routines:** Consistent structure for relationship and emotion learning
- **Choice and autonomy:** Options in sharing personal experiences and friendship stories
- **Co-regulation opportunities:** Adult modeling of calm, respectful communication
- **Safe spaces:** Designated areas for emotional regulation and conflict resolution

### Sensitive Content Guidelines

#### Teaching About Conflict and Disagreement
1. **Normalize conflict:** All relationships have disagreements sometimes
2. **Emphasize repair:** Focus on how relationships can be strengthened through working through problems
3. **Safety first:** Clear distinction between normal conflict and harmful situations
4. **Multiple strategies:** Various approaches recognizing different comfort levels and needs
5. **Adult support:** Clear pathways for seeking help when needed

#### Emotion Regulation and Big Feelings
- **Validate all emotions:** All feelings are acceptable, some behaviors may not be
- **Individual differences:** Recognize different emotional expressions and needs
- **Cultural sensitivity:** Honor diverse family approaches to emotional expression
- **Gradual exposure:** Build emotional vocabulary slowly and with support

### Supporting Students with Relationship Difficulties
- **Additional practice:** Extra opportunities to rehearse social skills
- **Visual supports:** Picture schedules and social stories for friendship skills
- **Peer buddies:** Structured friendship support systems
- **Family collaboration:** Coordinated approach between home and school

## Assessment Rubric

### Friendship Skills (FPS2)
**Level 4 (Exceeding):**
- Demonstrates sophisticated understanding of friendship qualities and consistently applies skills
- Shows exceptional empathy and regularly helps others with relationship challenges
- Independently resolves conflicts and helps peers with problem-solving

**Level 3 (Meeting):**
- Identifies key friendship qualities and applies them regularly
- Shows good empathy and supports peers appropriately
- Uses conflict resolution strategies with minimal adult support

**Level 2 (Approaching):**
- Identifies some friendship qualities with support
- Shows developing empathy and sometimes supports peers
- Needs adult guidance to resolve conflicts effectively

**Level 1 (Below):**
- Requires significant support to identify friendship qualities
- Limited empathy demonstration and peer support
- Requires direct adult intervention for conflict resolution

### Emotional Awareness and Regulation
**Level 4:** Consistently identifies complex emotions in self and others, uses multiple regulation strategies independently
**Level 3:** Identifies various emotions and uses regulation strategies with occasional support
**Level 2:** Identifies basic emotions and uses regulation strategies with regular prompting
**Level 1:** Requires significant support for emotion identification and regulation strategy use

### Communication Skills
**Level 4:** Consistently demonstrates active listening, assertive communication, and includes others naturally
**Level 3:** Usually demonstrates good communication skills and inclusive behaviors
**Level 2:** Shows developing communication skills with some prompting needed
**Level 1:** Requires direct instruction and support for effective communication

## Professional Learning Notes

### Key Takeaways for Teacher
- **Social-emotional development:** Grade 1 students need concrete, visual supports for abstract relationship concepts
- **Cultural responsiveness:** Honor diverse family approaches to friendship, emotion, and conflict resolution
- **Individual differences:** Some students will need more intensive social skills support and practice
- **Trauma sensitivity:** Relationship difficulties may stem from complex home or past experiences
- **Growth focus:** Celebrate progress in social skills while maintaining realistic expectations

- How did students respond to complex emotion vocabulary and regulation strategies?
- Which differentiation strategies were most effective for diverse learners?
- How can families be better supported in extending relationship learning at home?

- How can cross-curricular connections be strengthened to reinforce relationship skills?
