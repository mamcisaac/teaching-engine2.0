## 1. PE-Français Integration: "Bouger avec les Mots"

### Movement-Based Language Learning

#### Phonological Awareness Through Movement
**Curriculum Connection**: 1CO.0 - Différencier à l'oral les éléments reliés à la conscience phonologique

**Movement Activities**:
- **Sons et Mouvements**: Children perform specific movements for each phoneme
  - /a/ = jumping jacks ("a-a-a" with each jump)
  - /i/ = pointing up high ("i-i-i")
  - /o/ = making big circles with arms ("o-o-o")
- **Segmentation Locomotrice**: Walk/step for each phoneme in words
  - "chat" = step-step (ch-at)
  - "école" = step-step-step (é-co-le)
- **Substitution Danse**: Change movements when phonemes change
  - "rat" → "chat" (change from crawling to walking like cats)

#### Action Vocabulary Development
**Curriculum Connection**: 1CO.1 - Gérer son écoute pour répondre à ses besoins

**Movement Activities**:
- **Écoute Active Physique**: Full-body listening activities
  - "Jacques a dit" en français (French Simon Says)
  - Movement storytelling with action verbs
  - Partner mirroring with French movement commands
- **Vocabulaire d'Action**: Build French movement vocabulary
  - Locomoteur: marcher, courir, sauter, sautiller, galoper
  - Non-locomoteur: étirer, plier, tourner, balancer, se pencher
  - Manipulatif: lancer, attraper, frapper, pousser, tirer

#### Story Acting and Dramatization
**Cross-Curricular Activities**:
- **Contes en Mouvement**: Act out French stories with full-body movement
- **Théâtre Physique**: Express emotions and characters through movement
- **Récitation Rythmée**: Combine poems with rhythmic movement patterns

### Brain Breaks for French Learning
**Frequency**: Every 15-20 minutes during French instruction
- **Réveil Cérébral**: 2-minute movement sequences to reset attention
- **Danse des Voyelles**: Quick vowel sound dance
- **Étirements Bilingues**: Stretching with French directional vocabulary

## 2. PE-Mathématiques Integration: "Mathématiques en Mouvement"

### Embodied Mathematics Learning

#### Number Concepts Through Movement
**Curriculum Connection**: 1N5 - Comparer des ensembles comportant jusqu'à 20 éléments

**Movement Activities**:
- **Formation de Nombres**: Children form numbers with their bodies
  - Individual: make "3" shape with body position
  - Group: 5 children create the number "5" formation
- **Ensemble Vivant**: Human pictographs and data collection
  - Students group by movement preferences
  - Physical voting with movement to corners
  - Living bar graphs with children as data points
- **Comparaison Active**: Greater than/less than with body positioning
  - Jump forward for "greater than"
  - Step backward for "less than"
  - Stay in place for "equal to"

#### Spatial Relationships and Geometry
**Movement Activities**:
- **Géométrie Corporelle**: Create shapes with bodies
  - Individual shapes: circle (curl up), triangle (pointed position)
  - Partner shapes: rectangles, diamonds
  - Group shapes: large circles, squares, triangles
- **Directions et Orientations**: Directional movement games
  - Droite/gauche navigation courses
  - Devant/derrière follow-the-leader
  - Dessus/dessous obstacle courses

#### Patterns and Sequences
**Movement Activities**:
- **Séquences Motrices**: Create movement patterns
  - A-B patterns: jump-clap, jump-clap
  - A-B-C patterns: step-hop-turn, step-hop-turn
- **Rythmes Mathématiques**: Mathematical rhythms
  - Count by 2s with partner jumping
  - Count by 5s with 5-person movement sequences

### Math Brain Breaks
**Frequency**: Every 20 minutes during math instruction
- **Calcul Corporel**: Use fingers, toes, and body parts for counting
- **Course aux Nombres**: Quick number recognition races
- **Géométrie Express**: 1-minute shape-making challenges

## 3. PE-Sciences Integration: "Corps et Nature"

### Body Systems Awareness
**Curriculum Connection**: Health-Related Fitness (PE1-1.1)

**Movement Activities**:
- **Exploration du Cœur**: Heart rate activities
  - Measure resting heart rate by feeling pulse
  - Exercise and measure heart rate changes
  - "Heart Beat Dance" - move to rhythm of heartbeat
- **Système Respiratoire**: Breathing and movement
  - Deep breathing exercises before physical activity
  - "Balloon Lungs" - expand and contract like lungs
  - Running and monitoring breathing changes

#### Forces and Motion Through Movement
**Movement Activities**:
- **Forces en Action**: Explore push, pull, gravity
  - Partner tug-of-war (pulling forces)
  - Pushing activities with gym equipment
  - Jumping and landing (gravity exploration)
- **Équilibre et Stabilité**: Balance challenges
  - Static balance on various surfaces
  - Dynamic balance while moving
  - Partner balance challenges (counterbalancing)

#### Outdoor Science Movement
**Movement Activities**:
- **Exploration de la Nature**: Movement-based nature study
  - Animal movement imitation outdoors
  - Weather movement activities (wind, rain, snow responses)
  - Seasonal movement changes and adaptations
- **Course d'Observation**: Active nature scavenger hunts
  - Find and move like different animals
  - Collect natural objects while hiking/walking
  - Nature movement challenges

### Science Brain Breaks
**Frequency**: Every 25 minutes during science instruction
- **Mouvement Animal**: Quick animal movement imitations
- **Système Corporel**: Brief body system awareness activities
- **Nature Minute**: Quick outdoor movement if possible

## Brain Break System: "Pauses Actives Intelligentes"

### Strategic Movement Breaks for Optimal Learning

#### Brain Break Schedule by Subject
- **Français**: Every 15-20 minutes (language requires frequent brain refreshing)
- **Mathématiques**: Every 20 minutes (cognitive processing support)
- **Sciences**: Every 25 minutes (hands-on learning support)
- **Études Sociales**: Every 20 minutes (discussion processing support)
- **Arts**: Every 15 minutes (creative flow support)
- **Formation Personnelle**: Every 20 minutes (emotional processing support)

#### Quick Brain Break Activities (2-3 minutes)
1. **Réveil Corporel**: Full body wake-up sequence
2. **Respiration Énergisante**: Energizing breathing with movement
3. **Étirements Rapides**: Quick stretching sequence
4. **Coordination Express**: Hand-eye coordination challenges
5. **Équilibre Éclair**: Quick balance challenges

#### Extended Brain Breaks (5-10 minutes)
1. **Danse Libre**: Free dance to French music
2. **Parcours Minute**: Quick obstacle course
3. **Yoga pour Enfants**: Child-friendly yoga sequences
4. **Jeux Coopératifs**: Quick cooperative games
5. **Course de Relais**: Relay race variations

### Brain Break French Vocabulary
- **Commandes**: "Levez-vous", "Asseyez-vous", "Bougez", "Arrêtez"
- **Mouvements**: "Sautez", "Courez", "Marchez", "Tournez"
- **Directions**: "Droite", "Gauche", "Devant", "Derrière"
- **Intensité**: "Doucement", "Rapidement", "Fort", "Léger"
