# 🍂 Arts Unit 2: Colours and Fall Explorations
**Grade 1 French Immersion | October 1-31, 2025 | 16 Hours Total**

## 📚 Unit Overview

### Grandes idées / Big Ideas
- **FR**: Les couleurs racontent des histoires et expriment nos émotions, spécialement dans la beauté de l'automne
- **EN**: Colours tell stories and express our emotions, especially in the beauty of autumn

### Questions essentielles / Essential Questions
- Comment les couleurs nous font-elles ressentir différentes émotions?
- How do colours make us feel different emotions?
- Comment la nature change-t-elle ses couleurs à l'automne?
- How does nature change its colours in autumn?
- Que pouvons-nous créer en mélangeant les couleurs?
- What can we create by mixing colours?

### Expectations Covered
- **AV1**: Reconnaître la valeur de son environnement visuel (autumn colour observation)
- **AV2**: Utiliser la création artistique afin de communiquer des idées ou des sentiments (emotion through colour)
- **AV3**: Utiliser une variété d'outils, de matériaux, de styles et de techniques (colour mixing, autumn materials)
- **AV4**: Reconnaître la valeur de l'art en tant qu'élément de notre culture (Mi'kmaq and Acadian autumn traditions)

## 🎨 COLOUR THEORY EXPLORATIONS

### 1. PRIMARY COLOUR DISCOVERIES

#### **Les couleurs magiques / Magical Colours**
**Duration**: 3 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Rouge comme... / Red Like...**
- **Minds-On**: Red hunt around school - what do we see?
- **Action**: Create red emotion paintings (anger, love, excitement)
- **Materials**: Red tempera paint, various brush sizes, large paper
- **Vocabulary**: rouge, passion, force, énergie, amour

**Session 2: Bleu comme... / Blue Like...**
- **Minds-On**: Blue observation walk (sky, water, shadows)
- **Action**: Blue calm paintings (ocean, sky, peaceful feelings)
- **Materials**: Blue watercolors, sponges, salt for texture
- **Vocabulary**: bleu, calme, océan, ciel, paisible

**Session 3: Jaune comme... / Yellow Like...**
- **Minds-On**: Yellow happiness discussion (sun, joy, warmth)
- **Action**: Yellow celebration paintings (sunshine, happiness)
- **Materials**: Yellow acrylics, texture tools, gold glitter
- **Vocabulary**: jaune, bonheur, soleil, joie, chaleur

### 2. COLOUR MIXING LABORATORY

#### **Laboratoire de mélange / Mixing Laboratory**
**Duration**: 4 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Orange Creation Magic**
- **Discovery**: Red + Yellow = Orange experiments
- **Activity**: Pumpkin colour mixing practice
- **Documentation**: Colour recipe cards in French
- **Materials**: Primary paints, mixing palettes, recipe cards

**Session 2: Purple Power Discovery**
- **Discovery**: Red + Blue = Purple investigations
- **Activity**: Grape cluster colour exploration
- **Documentation**: Purple emotion wheel creation
- **Materials**: Primary paints, grape references, emotion cards

**Session 3: Green Growing Experiments**
- **Discovery**: Blue + Yellow = Green explorations
- **Activity**: Fall leaf green variations
- **Documentation**: Green family tree (light to dark)
- **Materials**: Primary paints, leaf samples, color gradation sheets

**Session 4: Secondary Colour Celebration**
- **Activity**: Create autumn rainbow using only mixed colours
- **Assessment**: Demonstrate mixing knowledge to a partner
- **Materials**: All previous materials, large collaborative paper

### 3. WARM AND COOL COLOUR INVESTIGATIONS

#### **Couleurs chaudes et froides / Warm and Cool Colours**
**Duration**: 3 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Warm Colour Autumn Fire**
- **Exploration**: Red, orange, yellow emotion connections
- **Activity**: Autumn bonfire painting (warm colours only)
- **Cultural Connection**: Harvest celebrations and warmth
- **Materials**: Warm paints, flame-shaped brushes, dark paper

**Session 2: Cool Colour Winter Preparation**
- **Exploration**: Blue, purple, green emotion connections
- **Activity**: Early winter scene (cool colours only)

- **Materials**: Cool paints, winter scene references

**Session 3: Warm vs Cool Contrast Art**

### 4. COLOUR EMOTION CONNECTIONS

#### **Les émotions en couleurs / Emotions in Colours**

## 🍁 AUTUMN ARTISTIC PROJECTS

### 1. LEAF PRINTING AND RUBBING TECHNIQUES

#### **Impressions de feuilles / Leaf Impressions**
**Duration**: 4 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Leaf Collection and Study**
- **Outdoor Activity**: Collect variety of autumn leaves
- **Classification**: Sort by size, shape, colour, texture
- **Documentation**: Leaf identification journal in French
- **Materials**: Collection bags, clipboards, identification sheets

**Session 2: Leaf Rubbing Techniques**
- **Technique Learning**: Paper over leaf, crayon rubbing
- **Colour Exploration**: Use autumn colours for rubbings
- **Pattern Creation**: Multiple leaf overlapping designs
- **Materials**: Crayons, thin paper, collected leaves

**Session 3: Leaf Printing with Paint**
- **Technique Learning**: Paint on leaf, press to paper
- **Colour Mixing**: Create leaf-specific colour mixtures
- **Composition**: Arrange prints into autumn scenes
- **Materials**: Tempera paints, brushes, leaves, printing paper

**Session 4: Mixed Media Leaf Art**
- **Combination**: Use both rubbing and printing techniques
- **Enhancement**: Add backgrounds and details
- **Storytelling**: Create autumn scenes with leaf art
- **Materials**: All previous materials plus drawing tools

### 2. PUMPKIN PAINTING EXPLORATIONS

#### **Explorations de citrouilles / Pumpkin Explorations**
**Duration**: 4 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Pumpkin Observation Drawing**
- **Study**: Real pumpkin observation and sketching
- **Shape Practice**: Round forms and proportion
- **Colour Mixing**: Create perfect pumpkin orange
- **Materials**: Real pumpkins, drawing paper, observation tools

**Session 2: Traditional Pumpkin Painting**
- **Technique**: Basic pumpkin painting skills
- **Variation**: Different pumpkin sizes and shapes
- **Details**: Stems, ridges, shadows
- **Materials**: Orange, brown, green paints, round brushes

**Session 3: Creative Pumpkin Characters**
- **Imagination**: Pumpkin faces and personalities
- **Storytelling**: Create pumpkin character stories
- **Expression**: Happy, scary, silly pumpkin faces
- **Materials**: Various paints, fine brushes, imagination prompts

**Session 4: Pumpkin Patch Scenes**
- **Composition**: Multiple pumpkins in autumn setting
- **Background**: Autumn sky, ground, environment
- **Storytelling**: Harvest time community scenes
- **Materials**: Large paper, full paint palette, composition guides

### 3. AUTUMN TREE COLLAGE PROJECTS

#### **Collages d'arbres d'automne / Autumn Tree Collages**
**Duration**: 5 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Tree Structure Study**
- **Observation**: Real tree structure drawing
- **Understanding**: Trunk, branches, crown shapes
- **Sketching**: Basic tree forms in different seasons
- **Materials**: Drawing materials, tree observation guides

**Session 2: Autumn Colour Tissue Paper**
- **Preparation**: Tear tissue paper in autumn colours
- **Technique**: Layering and overlapping methods
- **Colour Theory**: How colours blend when overlapped
- **Materials**: Autumn-coloured tissue paper, glue

**Session 3: Tree Trunk Creation**
- **Texture**: Brown paper bark texture techniques
- **Structure**: Trunk and branch construction
- **Foundation**: Prepare base for leaf addition
- **Materials**: Brown construction paper, texture tools

**Session 4: Leaf Application**
- **Technique**: Apply tissue paper leaves
- **Composition**: Create autumn colour gradients
- **Depth**: Layering for three-dimensional effect
- **Materials**: Prepared trunks, tissue paper, glue

**Session 5: Background and Details**
- **Environment**: Sky, ground, seasonal details
- **Enhancement**: Add birds, clouds, fallen leaves
- **Finishing**: Final details and artist statement
- **Materials**: Additional papers, drawing tools

### 4. HARVEST STILL LIFE DRAWINGS

#### **Natures mortes de récolte / Harvest Still Life**
**Duration**: 4 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Autumn Harvest Collection**
- **Gathering**: Collect autumn fruits, vegetables, leaves
- **Arrangement**: Learn still life composition basics
- **Observation**: Study shapes, colours, textures
- **Materials**: Harvest items, arrangement cloths

**Session 2: Shape and Proportion Drawing**
- **Skill**: Draw basic shapes of harvest items
- **Practice**: Proportion and size relationships
- **Foundation**: Prepare for colour addition
- **Materials**: Drawing paper, pencils, erasers

**Session 3: Colour Addition**
- **Technique**: Add realistic harvest colours
- **Mixing**: Create specific fruit and vegetable colours
- **Shading**: Simple light and shadow concepts
- **Materials**: Coloured pencils, pastels, references

**Session 4: Details and Finishing**
- **Enhancement**: Add textures and fine details
- **Background**: Simple background elements
- **Completion**: Artist statement about harvest
- **Materials**: All drawing materials, reflection prompts

### 5. THANKSGIVING GRATITUDE ART

#### **Art de gratitude / Gratitude Art**
**Duration**: 3 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Gratitude Tree Creation**
- **Concept**: Tree trunk with gratitude leaves
- **Brainstorming**: What are we thankful for?
- **Foundation**: Create collaborative class tree
- **Materials**: Large paper, brown paint, brushes

**Session 2: Personal Gratitude Leaves**
- **Individual**: Create personal gratitude expressions
- **Colour Choice**: Colours that represent thankfulness
- **Writing**: Simple gratitude words in French/English
- **Materials**: Leaf templates, autumn colours, markers

**Session 3: Gratitude Assembly and Celebration**
- **Community**: Attach leaves to class tree
- **Sharing**: Present gratitude expressions
- **Celebration**: Thanksgiving art gallery walk
- **Materials**: Completed leaves, display materials
