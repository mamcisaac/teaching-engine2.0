## 🇫🇷 1. MATH-FRANÇAIS INTEGRATION (DAILY APPLICATION)

### **Core Philosophy: Mathematical Storytelling and Language**
Every Français lesson becomes an opportunity for mathematical thinking and French mathematical vocabulary development.

#### **1.1 Number Vocabulary and Counting in French Context**

**Mathematical Expectations Integrated:**
- 1.N1: Counting 0-100 in literary contexts
- 1.N2: Subitizing in story illustrations  
- 1.N3: Counting concepts through narrative

**Daily Integration Activities:**

**Semaine 1-4: Septembre (Counting Stories)**
- **"Livres de nombres"** - Create counting books with original stories
- **"Comptines mathématiques"** - Traditional French counting rhymes daily
- **"Chasse aux nombres"** - Number hunts in read-aloud texts
- **"Problèmes en histoire"** - Story problems embedded in literature

**Mathematical French Vocabulary Development:**
- **Story Numbers:** premier, deuxième, troisième... vingtième
- **Quantity in Stories:** plusieurs, quelques, beaucoup, peu, aucun
- **Sequence Language:** d'abord, ensuite, puis, finalement
- **Literary Math Phrases:** "Il était une fois [nombre]...", "Tous les [nombre]..."

#### **1.2 Mathematical Storytelling Structure**

**Mathematical Expectations Integrated:**
- 1.N8: Addition/subtraction through story problems
- 1.RR1: Pattern recognition in story structure
- 1.RR3: Equality concepts in narrative balance

**Story Structure Mathematical Patterns:**
```
Début (Beginning) → Milieu (Middle) → Fin (End)
   ↓                    ↓               ↓
Situation initiale → Problème/Action → Résolution
   [nombre A]      + [changement]   = [nombre B]
```

**Weekly Implementation:**
- **Lundi:** Read story, identify mathematical elements
- **Mardi:** Retell story focusing on number changes
- **Mercredi:** Create mathematical story problems from literature
- **Jeudi:** Write original mathematical stories
- **Vendredi:** Share mathematical stories with calculations

#### **1.3 Pattern Poems and Songs**

**Mathematical Expectations Integrated:**
- 1.RR1: Repeating patterns in verse structure
- 1.RR2: Converting patterns between formats (audio/visual/movement)

**Poetry Mathematics Integration:**
- **Syllable Counting:** "Comptons les syllabes" (mathematical syllable analysis)
- **Rhyme Patterns:** ABAB, AABB pattern recognition and creation
- **Rhythm Mathematics:** Beat counting and pattern prediction
- **Verse Structure:** Stanza patterns and repetition mathematics

**French Mathematical Poetry Vocabulary:**
- **Pattern Terms:** "rime", "rythme", "répétition", "structure"
- **Counting Language:** "syllabe", "vers", "strophe", "mesure"
- **Poetry Math Phrases:** "Ça rime avec...", "Le rythme fait...", "Je compte..."

#### **1.4 Measurement in Reading Contexts**

**Mathematical Expectations Integrated:**
- 1.FE1: Measurement comparison in story settings
- 1.N7: More/less concepts in character descriptions

**Reading Measurement Activities:**
- **Character Comparison:** "Plus grand que", "Plus petit que" character analysis
- **Setting Descriptions:** Spatial and measurement vocabulary in context
- **Time in Stories:** Beginning understanding of duration and sequence
- **Story Maps:** Measuring and comparing story elements

**Assessment Integration in Français:**
- **Portfolio Conferences:** Mathematical discussions about favorite number books
- **Reading Comprehension:** Include mathematical questions about quantities in texts
