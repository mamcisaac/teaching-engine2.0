# Mathematics Unit 1: Number Sense Foundations
## Les nombres tout autour de nous (Numbers All Around Us)

### 📅 Unit Overview
- **Duration**: September 4-30, 2025 (4 weeks, 24 instructional hours)
- **Grade**: 1 French Immersion
- **Subject**: Mathématiques
- **Unit Focus**: Numbers 0-10, counting, subitizing, number recognition

### 🎯 Curriculum Expectations Covered
- **1.N1**: Démontrer une compréhension du dénombrement en reconnaissant « combien » dans des ensembles comportant jusqu'à 20 éléments
- **1.N2**: Comparer directement deux ensembles comportant jusqu'à 20 éléments
- **1.N3**: Estimer « combien » dans des ensembles comportant jusqu'à 10 éléments

---

## 📚 CONCRETE-PICTORIAL-ABSTRACT PROGRESSION

### Week 1 (Sept 4-8): CONCRETE Phase - Hands-On Exploration
**Focus**: Manipulative-based number experiences

#### Specific Manipulatives & Activities:
- **Counting Collections**: 
  - Natural materials (acorns, shells, leaves)
  - Classroom manipulatives (bear counters, blocks, buttons)
  - Edible items (crackers, raisins) for snack math
  - Daily 15-minute rotation stations

- **Subitizing Games**:
  - "Flash Cards" with real objects (2-5 items)
  - "Grab and Tell" with small objects in bags
  - "Quick Look" activities with manipulatives under cloth

- **Number Recognition Hunt**:
  - Physical numerals (sandpaper, magnetic, foam)
  - Number cards hidden around classroom
  - Body formation of numbers 0-5

#### Daily Timing Structure:
- **10 minutes**: Morning number routine (calendar, counting)
- **25 minutes**: Main lesson with manipulatives
- **10 minutes**: Math talk and reflection
- **5 minutes**: Number journal (drawing collections)

### Week 2 (Sept 11-15): PICTORIAL Phase - Visual Representations
**Focus**: Bridging concrete to abstract through pictures

#### Ten Frame Investigations:
- **Physical ten frames** (egg cartons, ice cube trays)
- **Drawing ten frames** with dots, stamps, stickers
- **Interactive ten frames** (digital and magnetic)
- **Daily ten frame challenges** (Show me 7! Show me 3!)

#### Dot Pattern Activities:
- **Dice patterns** (standard and non-standard arrangements)
- **Domino explorations** (counting dots, matching quantities)
- **Creating patterns** with stamps and stickers
- **Pattern recognition games** (same/different quantities)

#### Visual Number Stories:
- **Picture books** with countable elements
- **Student-created** counting books (photos + drawings)
- **Digital stories** using classroom tablet/computer
- **Illustrated problems** (3 birds + 2 more = ?)

### Week 3 (Sept 18-22): SYMBOLIC Introduction - Numerals 0-10
**Focus**: Connecting quantities to written symbols

#### Number Formation Practice:
- **Multi-sensory writing**:
  - Sand trays for finger tracing
  - Playdough number creation
  - Air writing with large movements
  - Chalk on individual whiteboards

- **Numeral Recognition**:
  - Number matching games (quantity to symbol)
  - Number line building activities
  - Missing number puzzles
  - Number sequence songs and chants

#### Mathematical Thinking Activities:
- **Number talks** (How do you see 6?)
- **Estimation jars** (guess, count, verify)
- **Number of the day** investigations
- **Math journals** with number representations

### Week 4 (Sept 25-30): PRACTICE & CONSOLIDATION
**Focus**: Combining all three approaches

#### Integrated Activities:
- **Number stations** (concrete, pictorial, abstract at each)
- **Assessment through play** (observations during games)
- **Student choice** in representation method
- **Peer teaching** opportunities

## 🗣️ FRENCH VOCABULARY INTEGRATION

### Core Number Vocabulary:
| Français | English | Pronunciation Guide |
|----------|---------|-------------------|
| zéro | zero | [ZEH-ro] |
| un/une | one | [uhn/oon] |
| deux | two | [duh] |
| trois | three | [twah] |
| quatre | four | [KAH-truh] |
| cinq | five | [sank] |
| six | six | [sees] |
| sept | seven | [set] |
| huit | eight | [wheet] |
| neuf | nine | [nuhf] |
| dix | ten | [dees] |

### Mathematical Thinking Vocabulary:
- **Combien?** (How many?) - Central question
- **Plus que** (More than) / **Moins que** (Less than)
- **Autant que** (Same as) / **Égal** (Equal)
- **Beaucoup** (A lot) / **Un peu** (A little)
- **Compter** (To count) / **Dénombrer** (To count/enumerate)
- **Ensemble** (Set/group) / **Collection** (Collection)

### Problem-Solving Discussion Starters:
- "Comment le sais-tu?" (How do you know?)
- "Peux-tu me l'expliquer?" (Can you explain it to me?)
- "As-tu une autre façon?" (Do you have another way?)
- "Qu'est-ce que tu remarques?" (What do you notice?)
- "Que penses-tu?" (What do you think?)

### Math Journal Prompts in French:
- "Aujourd'hui j'ai compté..." (Today I counted...)
- "J'ai vu le nombre ___ quand..." (I saw the number ___ when...)
- "Mon nombre préféré est ___ parce que..." (My favorite number is ___ because...)
- "J'ai trouvé ___ objets..." (I found ___ objects...)

## 📊 ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

### Daily Observation Checklist:
**Student**: _________________ **Date**: _________

#### Counting Skills:
- [ ] Counts objects accurately 1-10
- [ ] Uses one-to-one correspondence
- [ ] Knows counting sequence 1-10
- [ ] Can count backwards from 10
- [ ] Recognizes "how many" without recounting

#### Number Recognition:
- [ ] Identifies numerals 0-10
- [ ] Matches quantity to numeral
- [ ] Writes numerals 0-10 legibly
- [ ] Orders numbers 0-10

#### Subitizing:
- [ ] Instantly recognizes 1-3 items
- [ ] Beginning to recognize 4-5 items
- [ ] Uses patterns to see quantity
- [ ] Explains thinking in French

### Concrete Manipulation Assessment:
**Week 2 Individual Conference (10 minutes per student)**

#### Materials: 
- 15 mixed manipulatives, number cards 0-10, ten frame

#### Tasks:
1. **Counting**: "Compte tous les objets" (Count all objects)
2. **Subitizing**: Flash 3, then 5 items quickly
3. **Comparison**: Show 6 and 4 items - "Lequel a plus?" (Which has more?)
4. **Number matching**: Match quantities to numerals
5. **Creation**: "Montre-moi 7" (Show me 7)

#### Recording:
- ✓ Secure, ◐ Developing, ○ Beginning
- Anecdotal notes on strategies used
- Language observations (French vocabulary used)

### Number Recognition Rubric:

| Criteria | Beginning (1) | Developing (2) | Secure (3) |
|----------|---------------|----------------|------------|
| **Identifies numerals 0-10** | 0-3 numerals | 4-7 numerals | 8-10 numerals |
| **Writes numerals clearly** | 0-3 legible | 4-7 legible | 8-10 legible |
| **Matches quantity to numeral** | With support | Some independence | Independent |
| **French vocabulary use** | Gestures/pointing | Single words | Short phrases |

### Math Talk Evaluation:
**Weekly Math Circle Assessment**

#### Communication Indicators:
- Uses French number words accurately
- Explains thinking process
- Asks mathematical questions
- Responds to peers' ideas
- Uses mathematical vocabulary

#### Recording Method:
- Voice recordings (30 seconds per child weekly)
- Photo documentation of work
- Sticky note observations
- Student self-assessment with simple emojis

## 🔄 DIFFERENTIATION SPECIFICS

### Support Level: Extended Concrete Phase

#### Adaptations:
- **Numbers 0-5 focus** instead of 0-10
- **Extended manipulative time** (3 weeks instead of 1)
- **Simplified language** (gestures + key words)
- **Visual supports** (number line on desk)
- **Peer partnership** for counting activities

#### Specific Strategies:
- One-to-one counting practice with adult support
- Large manipulatives for fine motor challenges
- Touch and count strategies emphasized
- French number songs with actions
- Success celebrated at child's level

#### Assessment Modifications:
- Portfolio approach (photos of concrete work)
- Longer time for tasks
- Multiple opportunities to demonstrate learning
- Focus on growth over grade level standards

### Standard Level: Balanced Progression

#### Teaching Approach:
- Follow 4-week CPA progression as outlined
- Mix of independent and guided practice
- French vocabulary integrated naturally
- Variety of manipulatives and activities
- Regular check-ins and adjustments

#### Expectations:
- Count and recognize quantities 0-10
- Match numerals to quantities
- Begin subitizing 1-5
- Use basic French mathematical vocabulary
- Show mathematical thinking through multiple representations

### Extension Level: Patterns and Relationships

#### Advanced Opportunities:
- **Explore numbers to 20** during choice time
- **Pattern creation** with numbers and quantities
- **Teaching others** (peer tutoring opportunities)
- **Complex problems** (story problems with multiple steps)
- **Mathematical connections** (numbers in art, music, movement)

#### Enrichment Activities:
- Number pattern investigations (2, 4, 6...)
- Creating number stories for younger students
- Exploring different number representations (tally marks, ten frames, dice patterns)
- Mathematical games leadership
- French number poetry and song creation

#### Challenge Projects:
- **Number museum**: Create displays showing numbers in different ways
- **Counting book**: Write and illustrate for kindergarten students
- **Math teaching**: Lead number activities for younger classes
- **Pattern investigations**: Explore growing and shrinking patterns

## 🕒 DETAILED LESSON TIMING & IMPLEMENTATION

### Daily Schedule (50 minutes total):
- **5 min**: Entrance routine with number practice
- **10 min**: Morning number routine (calendar, counting, number of day)
- **25 min**: Main mathematical investigation
- **5 min**: Math talk and reflection
- **5 min**: Math journal recording

### Weekly Schedule Overview:

#### Monday: New Learning
- Introduce weekly focus concept
- Whole group mini-lesson (10 min)
- Guided practice with teacher (15 min)
- Individual/partner exploration

#### Tuesday: Practice & Application
- Review previous learning
- Station rotations (3 stations, 8 min each)
- Independent practice time
- Small group interventions as needed

#### Wednesday: Investigation Day
- Extended mathematical exploration
- Problem-solving focus
- Student choice in approach
- Documentation for assessment

#### Thursday: Assessment & Feedback
- Individual conferences (5 students)
- Peer sharing opportunities
- Game-based assessment
- Portfolio development

#### Friday: Celebration & Preview
- Week reflection and celebration
- Sharing mathematical discoveries
- Preview of next week
- Take-home family math activity

### Practical Implementation Details:

#### Material Organization:
- **Math bins**: One per table group with counting materials
- **Ten frame station**: Permanent setup with various manipulatives
- **Number line**: Floor number line 0-10 for kinesthetic learners
- **French word wall**: Mathematical vocabulary with visuals
- **Choice board**: Visual menu of available activities

#### Transition Strategies:
- **Number songs** for cleanup and movement
- **Counting steps** to next activity location
- **French number calls** for attention ("Si tu entends 'trois', lève ta main")
- **Visual timers** for activity length awareness

#### Technology Integration:
- **Interactive whiteboard**: For whole group number activities
- **Tablets**: Counting apps in French (supervised use)
- **Digital camera**: For documenting mathematical thinking
- **Voice recorder**: For math talk documentation
