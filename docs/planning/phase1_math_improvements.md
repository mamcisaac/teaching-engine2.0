## COMPREHENSIVE IMPROVEMENTS

### Unit 1: Numbers All Around Us (September) - ENHANCED
**Target Expectations:** 1.N1, 1.N2, 1.N3

#### Mathematical Progression Enhancement:
**Week 1-2: Concrete Stage**
- Physical counting with natural materials
- One-to-one correspondence with manipulatives
- Subitizing with real objects (dice, dominoes, fingers)

**Week 3: Pictorial Stage**  
- Number representations with drawings
- Dot patterns and ten frames
- Visual number lines and charts

**Week 4: Symbolic Stage**
- Numeral recognition and formation
- Number symbols in various contexts
- Beginning written number work

#### French Mathematical Vocabulary Development:
- **Core Numbers:** zéro, un, deux, trois, quatre, cinq, six, sept, huit, neuf, dix
- **Counting Terms:** compter, nombrer, dénombrer, énumérer
- **Quantity Language:** combien, plus, moins, autant, pareil
- **Subitizing Phrases:** "Je vois...", "Il y a...", "Ça fait..."

### Unit 2: Making Sense of Numbers (October) - ENHANCED  
**Target Expectations:** 1.N4, 1.N5, 1.N6

#### Mathematical Progression Enhancement:
**Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract for Number Representation:**
- Manipulatives (base-10 blocks, counting bears)
- Pictures and diagrams (ten frames, dot arrangements)
- Symbols and numerals with place value understanding

#### Specific French Mathematical Discourse:
- **Comparison Language:** "plus que", "moins que", "égal à", "pareil"
- **Grouping Terms:** "groupe", "ensemble", "collection", "série"
- **Representation Vocabulary:** "représenter", "montrer", "illustrer", "dessiner"

### Unit 3: Patterns and Shapes (November) - ENHANCED
**Target Expectations:** 1.RR1, 1.RR2, 1.FE2

#### Enhanced Pattern Development:
**Progressive Pattern Complexity:**
1. **AB patterns** with concrete materials
2. **ABC patterns** with pictorial representations  
3. **ABAB, ABBA patterns** with abstract symbols
4. **Growing patterns** introduction

#### Shape Learning Progression:
**3D to 2D Connection:**
- Concrete 3D objects → shadow/face exploration → 2D representations
- Sorting activities with real objects before pictures
- Property exploration through manipulation

#### French Mathematical Language:
- **Pattern Terms:** "régularité", "motif", "répéter", "continuer", "prédire"
- **Shape Vocabulary:** "forme", "côté", "sommet", "face", "arête"
- **Sorting Language:** "trier", "classer", "caractéristique", "propriété"

### Unit 4: Adding and Subtracting (December-January) - MAJOR ENHANCEMENT
**Target Expectations:** 1.N7, 1.N8

#### Critical Mathematical Progression:
**Stage 1: Concrete Understanding (3 weeks)**
- Physical joining and separating with manipulatives
- Story acting with real objects
- Counting on strategies with concrete materials

**Stage 2: Pictorial Representation (3 weeks)**
- Drawing pictures to solve problems
- Ten frames for addition/subtraction
- Number lines for strategies

**Stage 3: Abstract Thinking (2 weeks)**
- Mental math beginning
- Written number sentences
- Strategy explanations

#### Enhanced French Mathematical Discourse:
- **Operation Language:** "ajouter", "enlever", "combiner", "séparer"
- **Strategy Terms:** "compter à partir de", "doubles", "presque doubles"
- **Problem-Solving Phrases:** "Je pense que...", "Ma stratégie est...", "J'ai trouvé..."

### Unit 5: Mental Math Strategies (February) - COMPLETE RESTRUCTURE
**Target Expectations:** 1.N9, 1.RR3

#### Systematic Strategy Development:
**Week 1: Counting Strategies**
- Counting on from larger number
- Counting back for subtraction
- Skip counting by 2s, 5s, 10s

**Week 2: Pattern-Based Strategies**
- Doubles facts (2+2, 3+3, etc.)
- Near doubles (3+4 = 3+3+1)
- Making 10 strategies

**Week 3: Equality and Balance**
- Balance scales with manipulatives
- Equation balancing activities
- True/false number sentences

#### Advanced French Mathematical Communication:
- **Strategy Language:** "stratégie", "méthode", "façon de faire"
- **Thinking Words:** "penser", "réfléchir", "calculer mentalement"
- **Balance Terms:** "équilibre", "égal", "déséquilibre", "différent"

### Unit 6: Measurement Exploration (March) - ENHANCED DEPTH
**Target Expectations:** 1.FE1

#### Comprehensive Measurement Progression:
**Week 1: Direct Comparison**
- Comparing lengths, heights, weights directly
- Using comparison language accurately
- Ordering objects by measurement attributes

**Week 2: Indirect Comparison**
- Using intermediary objects for comparison
- Introduction to units of measurement
- Consistent unit importance

**Week 3: Non-Standard Units**
- Measuring with paper clips, cubes, hands
- Recording measurements and comparing
- Understanding the need for standard units

#### French Measurement Vocabulary:
- **Measurement Terms:** "mesurer", "longueur", "hauteur", "largeur", "poids"
- **Comparison Language:** "plus long", "plus court", "plus lourd", "plus léger"
- **Units Language:** "unité", "mesure", "environ", "exactement"
