# ❄️ Arts Unit 3: Textures and Winter Wonders
**Grade 1 French Immersion | November 4 - December 20, 2025 | 18 Hours Total**

## 📚 Unit Overview

### Grandes idées / Big Ideas
- **FR**: Les textures racontent des histoires tactiles et visuelles, particulièrement dans la magie de l'hiver
- **EN**: Textures tell tactile and visual stories, especially in the magic of winter

### Questions essentielles / Essential Questions
- Comment les textures nous font-elles ressentir différentes sensations?
- How do textures make us feel different sensations?
- Que pouvons-nous découvrir en touchant et en créant différentes textures?
- What can we discover by touching and creating different textures?
- Comment l'hiver transforme-t-il les textures de notre monde?
- How does winter transform the textures of our world?

### Expectations Covered
- **AV1**: Reconnaître la valeur de son environnement visuel (winter texture observation)
- **AV2**: Utiliser la création artistique afin de communiquer des idées ou des sentiments (expression through texture)
- **AV3**: Utiliser une variété d'outils, de matériaux, de styles et de techniques (texture creation methods, winter materials)
- **AV4**: Reconnaître la valeur de l'art en tant qu'élément de notre culture (winter cultural celebrations)

## 🎨 TEXTURE EXPLORATION ACTIVITIES

### 1. NATURAL TEXTURE COLLECTION AND RUBBING

#### **Chasse aux textures naturelles / Natural Texture Hunt**
**Duration**: 4 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Outdoor Winter Texture Exploration**
- **Minds-On**: Explore school grounds for winter textures
- **Action**: Collect safe natural materials (bark, pine cones, stones, ice formations)
- **Documentation**: Texture journal with French descriptive words
- **Materials**: Collection bags, magnifying glasses, clipboards, cameras

**Texture Vocabulary Development**:
- *Winter textures*: rugueux (rough), lisse (smooth), piquant (prickly), dur (hard), mou (soft)
- *Natural materials*: écorce (bark), pomme de pin (pine cone), glace (ice), neige (snow), branche (branch)

**Session 2: Indoor Texture Investigation**
- **Activity**: Create texture rubbings using collected materials
- **Technique**: Paper over texture, side-of-crayon rubbing method
- **Exploration**: Different pressure applications for varied effects
- **Materials**: Thin paper, unwrapped crayons, natural texture materials

**Session 3: Fabric and Material Texture Study**
- **Collection**: Gather various fabric textures (wool, silk, burlap, corduroy, velvet)
- **Investigation**: Compare natural vs. manufactured textures
- **Creation**: Fabric texture rubbing compositions
- **Materials**: Fabric samples, rubbing papers, colored pencils

**Session 4: Texture Pattern Creation**
- **Combination**: Layer multiple texture rubbings
- **Pattern**: Create repeating texture patterns
- **Composition**: Arrange textures to tell winter stories
- **Materials**: Previous rubbing work, arrangement tools, adhesive

### 2. FOOD TEXTURE ART PROJECTS

#### **Projets de texture alimentaire / Food Texture Art Projects**
**Duration**: 3 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Winter Food Texture Exploration**
- **Investigation**: Explore textures of winter foods (oatmeal, rice, beans, pasta)
- **Safety**: Food-safe art materials only, allergy considerations
- **Documentation**: Create texture vocabulary cards
- **Materials**: Various dried foods, safe adhesives, documentation sheets

**Session 2: Food Collage Creations**
- **Technique**: Glue different textured foods to create winter scenes
- **Focus**: Snowflake patterns with rice, tree textures with oatmeal
- **Safety**: Non-toxic, washable adhesives only
- **Materials**: Food materials, safe glue, heavy cardboard bases

**Session 3: Printing with Food Textures**
- **Method**: Dip textured foods in safe paint for printing
- **Creation**: Winter landscape backgrounds using food prints
- **Innovation**: Combine food printing with other techniques
- **Materials**: Washable paints, food items, printing paper

### 3. PAPER TEXTURE CREATION

#### **Création de textures de papier / Paper Texture Creation**
**Duration**: 4 sessions x 45 minutes

**Session 1: Paper Manipulation Techniques**
- **Techniques**: Crumpling, pleating, tearing, rolling
- **Exploration**: How manipulation creates different textures
- **Practice**: Master various paper manipulation methods
- **Materials**: Various paper types, manipulation tools

**Session 2: Layered Paper Landscapes**
- **Creation**: Build winter scenes using textured paper layers
- **Depth**: Use overlapping for three-dimensional effects
- **Texture Variety**: Combine different paper manipulation techniques
- **Materials**: Colored papers, layering adhesives, cutting tools

**Session 3: Paper Weaving Projects**
- **Technique**: Simple over-under weaving patterns
- **Materials**: Use textured and colored paper strips
- **Creation**: Winter-themed woven placemats or wall hangings
- **Materials**: Paper strips, weaving bases, cutting tools

**Session 4: Mixed Media Paper Art**
- **Combination**: Integrate all paper texture techniques
- **Theme**: Create comprehensive winter texture compositions
- **Enhancement**: Add color and drawing details
- **Materials**: All previous materials, drawing supplies

### 4. MULTI-SENSORY TEXTURE EXPERIENCES

#### **Expériences multi-sensorielles / Multi-Sensory Experiences**
**Duration**: 3 sessions x 30 minutes

**Session 1: Texture Mystery Boxes**
- **Activity**: Identify textures by touch only
- **Language**: Describe textures in French vocabulary
- **Discovery**: Connect touch sensations to visual appearance
- **Materials**: Mystery boxes, various textured objects, recording sheets

**Session 2: Texture Sound Exploration**
- **Investigation**: What sounds do different textures make?
