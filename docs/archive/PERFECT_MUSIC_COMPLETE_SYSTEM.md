### Unit 2: Rhythm and Movement (Oct 14 - Nov 21)
**Duration**: 6 weeks, 10 hours  
**Expectations**: ME 1, MA 1.1

### Unit 4: Creating Music Together (Jan 26 - Mar 13)

**Expectations**: CC 1.1, CC 1.2

**Focus**: Composition and notation
- Creating musical ideas

- Introduction to symbols

**Culminating Task**: Class original composition

**Duration**: 6 weeks, 10 hours
