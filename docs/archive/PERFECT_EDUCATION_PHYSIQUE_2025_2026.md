## 🏃‍♀️ THE 7 PERFECT UNIT PLANS

### Unit 1: Mon corps en mouvement (Sept 4 - Oct 17)
**Duration**: 6.5 weeks, 21 hours  
**Expectations**: 1.1, 1.2, 3.1

**Focus**: Body awareness, coordination, posture, understanding physical activity effects
- Developing coordination of different body parts
- Adjusting body and posture in movements
- Understanding what happens to body during activity
- Building foundation movement skills

**Culminating Task**: "My Amazing Body" movement demonstration showcasing different body parts and movements

### Unit 2: Bouger partout (Oct 20 - Nov 28)
**Duration**: 6 weeks, 18 hours  
**Expectations**: 1.3, 1.4, 1.5

**Focus**: Locomotor skills, balance, spatial awareness, obstacle navigation  
- Various modes of displacement with pace variation
- Moving on different surfaces while maintaining balance
- Chaining movements around obstacles
- Developing spatial relationships

**Culminating Task**: Design and navigate classroom obstacle course demonstrating various locomotor movements

### Unit 3: Jouer avec les objets (Dec 1 - Jan 31)
**Duration**: 9 weeks, 24 hours  
**Expectations**: 1.6, 1.7, 1.8, 1.9

**Focus**: Object manipulation, hand-eye coordination, throwing/catching
- Coordinating locomotor actions with transported objects
- Manipulating various objects with control
- Coordinating projection actions (throwing)
- Synchronizing with objects during reception (catching)

**Culminating Task**: Winter Olympics object skills demonstration showcasing manipulation, projection, and reception abilities

### Unit 4: Jouer ensemble (Feb 2 - Mar 20)
**Duration**: 7 weeks, 21 hours  
**Expectations**: 2.1, 2.2, 2.6

**Focus**: Cooperation, teamwork, creative movement expression
- Adjusting to partners for common tasks
- Adjusting movements to partners' actions  
- Creating movement ideas and sequences in groups
- Developing social skills through movement

**Culminating Task**: Partner and group movement showcase featuring cooperative activities and creative sequences

### Unit 5: Jeux et défis (Mar 23 - Apr 25)
**Duration**: 5 weeks, 18 hours  
**Expectations**: 2.3, 2.4

**Focus**: Game skills, reaction abilities, fair play
- Reacting appropriately to opponents' actions
- Reacting to teammates' and opponents' actions
- Developing quick thinking and decision-making
- Learning sportsmanship and fair play

**Culminating Task**: Spring games festival featuring student-led activities demonstrating reaction skills and fair play

### Unit 6: Santé et bien-être (Apr 28 - May 22)
**Duration**: 3.5 weeks, 15 hours  
**Expectation**: 3.2

**Focus**: Health awareness, fitness benefits, wellness choices
- Putting into practice effects of physical activity on body
- Understanding exercise benefits
- Making healthy lifestyle choices
- Developing lifelong wellness habits

**Culminating Task**: Create and present "Healthy Me" plan showcasing understanding of physical activity benefits

### Unit 7: Célébrons nos mouvements (May 25 - June 25)
**Duration**: 4.5 weeks, 18 hours  
**Expectations**: Integration of all 16

**Focus**: Skill integration, reflection, celebration, summer planning
- Demonstrating all movement skills learned
- Reflecting on physical growth and development
- Celebrating achievements and progress
- Planning continued activity

**Culminating Task**: Physical education celebration showcase featuring student choice activities and demonstrations
