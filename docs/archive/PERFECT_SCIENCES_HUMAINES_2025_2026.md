## 📝 THE 5 PERFECT UNIT PLANS

### Unit 1: Ma famille et notre classe (Sept 4 - Oct 10)
**Duration**: 6 weeks, 18 hours  
**Expectations**: 1ICC.1, 1ER.1

**Focus**: Family diversity, uniqueness of people, needs vs wants
- Celebrating cultural diversity in families
- Understanding what makes each person special
- Learning difference between needs and wants
- Building inclusive classroom community

**Culminating Task**: Class museum of family diversity with photos, artifacts, and stories

### Unit 2: Nos droits et responsabilités (Oct 14 - Nov 28)
**Duration**: 7 weeks, 20 hours  
**Expectations**: 1C.1, 1PA.1

**Focus**: Rights, responsibilities, decision-making, conflict resolution
- Learning classroom and family rights/responsibilities  
- Practicing decision-making processes
- Developing peaceful conflict resolution skills
- Creating class charter and rules

**Culminating Task**: Classroom charter of rights and responsibilities with problem-solving guidelines

### Unit 3: Mon histoire dans le temps (Dec 1 - Jan 31)
**Duration**: 8 weeks, 24 hours  
**Expectation**: 1LT.2

**Focus**: Personal timelines, organizing life events, understanding time
- Creating personal history timelines
- Organizing memories chronologically
- Understanding growth and change over time
- Connecting to family histories

**Culminating Task**: Personal history museum with timelines, artifacts, and recorded stories

### Unit 4: Explorer notre monde (Feb 2 - Apr 11)
**Duration**: 10 weeks, 28 hours  
**Expectation**: 1LT.1

**Focus**: Maps, directions, landmarks, geographic thinking
- Using maps, plans, and globes
- Finding important community places
- Learning directions and spatial relationships
- Developing geographic vocabulary

**Culminating Task**: Giant floor map of community with guided tours

### Unit 5: Citoyens numériques responsables (Apr 14 - June 25)
**Duration**: 11 weeks, 30 hours  
**Expectation**: 1C.2

**Focus**: Digital citizenship, online safety, responsible technology use
- Learning age-appropriate digital citizenship
- Understanding online safety rules
- Practicing respectful technology use
- Developing responsible digital habits

**Culminating Task**: Digital citizenship pledges and safety demonstrations for younger students
