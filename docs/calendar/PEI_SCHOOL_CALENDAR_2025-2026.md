## 📝 **IMPORTANT REMINDERS**

### **Never Schedule Lessons On:**
1. Any statutory holiday
2. Any PD day
3. Any weekend
4. During Christmas break (Dec 22 - Jan 4)
5. During March break (Mar 9-13)

### **Always Remember:**
- Check school-specific variations to PD days
- Verify exact dates with school administration
- Plan for potential snow days (typical in PEI)
- Keep some flexibility for special school events
- Halloween and Valentine's Day fall on regular teaching days (theme accordingly)

### **Special Considerations:**
- **Remembrance Day**: Can have ceremony/assembly but not regular lessons
- **Last Day of School**: Usually celebration/awards, not regular lessons
- **First Day After Break**: Students may need review/transition time
