# PE Unit 3: Gymnastics Basics and Winter Indoor Activities
**Grade 1 - Physical Education with Specialist Teacher**

## Unit Overview
- **Duration**: November 4 - December 20, 2025 (18 hours)
- **Format**: 30-minute blocks, 3 times per week
- **Focus**: Gymnastics fundamentals and engaging winter indoor movement activities
- **Building on**: Unit 2 locomotor skills and ball handling foundation
- **Holiday Integration**: Includes winter celebration movement themes

## Unit Goals
1. Introduce safe gymnastics basics (rolling, balance beam, jumping, tumbling)
2. Develop body awareness and spatial orientation through gymnastics
3. Create engaging winter indoor movement experiences
4. Build flexibility, strength, and core stability age-appropriately
5. Integrate holiday and winter themes into physical activities
6. Expand French movement vocabulary with gymnastics and winter terms
7. Emphasize equipment safety and gymnasium behavior

## Safety Protocols
- Mandatory mat placement and safety checks before each lesson
- Clear gymnastics equipment safety rules and procedures
- Proper warm-up focusing on flexibility and joint mobility
- Spotting techniques and peer safety awareness
- Emergency procedures for gymnastics-related incidents
- Equipment respect and care protocols
- Appropriate clothing checks for gymnastics activities

## Lesson Plans (30 minutes each)

### **Lesson 1: Introduction to Gymnastics Safety and Mat Work**
**Date**: November 4, 2025
**Location**: Gymnasium with gymnastics mats

**Warm-up (5 minutes)**
- Walking around mats with awareness of surroundings
- Arm circles and gentle joint mobility
- Practice safe mat entry and exit

**Main Activity (20 minutes)**
- **Gymnastics Safety Rules**: Mat placement, waiting turns, respectful behavior
- **Basic Mat Familiarization**: Sitting, lying, rolling on mats safely
- **Log Rolls**: Roll straight across mat like a log, keeping body straight
- **Pencil Rolls**: Arms overhead, roll maintaining straight line
- **Safety Games**: "Red Light, Green Light" on mats with controlled stops

**Cool Down (5 minutes)**
- Gentle stretching on mats
- Deep breathing exercises
- Review safety rules learned

**French Vocabulary**: gymnastique (gymnastics), tapis (mat), sécurité (safety), rouler (to roll)

## Assessment Strategies

### Formative Assessment
- Daily observation of gymnastics technique development
- Safety protocol adherence and equipment respect
- Body awareness and spatial orientation progress
- Cooperation and peer support behaviors
- French vocabulary usage in gymnastics context
- Individual effort and personal challenge acceptance

### Summative Assessment
- Demonstration of basic gymnastics skills (rolling, balancing, jumping)
- Safety knowledge and application in gymnastics setting
- Creative movement and winter theme integration
- Participation in cooperative activities and partner work
- Use of French gymnastics and winter vocabulary
- Overall physical confidence and body awareness improvement

## Differentiation Strategies

### For Advanced Learners
- Complex gymnastics sequences and combinations
- Leadership roles in partner and group activities
- Advanced balance challenges and creative elements
- Mentoring roles helping other students
- Extended French vocabulary and communication

### For Beginning Learners
- Simplified gymnastics movements with extra support
- Modified equipment and lower challenge levels
- Additional practice time and repetition
- Peer buddy system for support and encouragement
- Visual cues and step-by-step instruction

### For English Language Learners
- Visual gymnastics terminology cards with pictures
- Bilingual peer partners for translation support
- Demonstration-heavy instruction with minimal verbal explanation
- Gesture-based communication for gymnastics positions
- French-English cognate emphasis in vocabulary

### For Students with Physical Limitations
- Modified gymnastics activities within individual capabilities
- Adaptive equipment and alternative positioning
- Focus on personal improvement rather than comparison
- Alternative roles in group activities (coaching, counting, etc.)
- Emphasis on flexibility and strength within range
