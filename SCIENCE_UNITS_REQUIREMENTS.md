## PERFECT SCIENCE LESSON REQUIREMENTS

### Inquiry-Based Learning Framework
Every lesson must include:
1. **Question Formation** - Students generate investigable questions
2. **Hypothesis Development** - Age-appropriate predictions
3. **Investigation Design** - Simple, safe experimental procedures
4. **Data Collection** - Observations, measurements, recordings
5. **Analysis & Conclusion** - Pattern recognition, explanation attempts

### Hands-On Exploration Requirements
- **Minimum 70% hands-on activities** per lesson
- **Real materials and specimens** when possible
- **Multi-sensory investigations** (sight, sound, touch, smell - safe taste)
- **Outdoor learning opportunities** in every unit
- **Simple tools and equipment** appropriate for Grade 1

### Science Journals/Notebooks
- **Daily science journal entries** with drawings and words
- **Before/During/After** investigation recording
- **Vocabulary development** through word walls and definitions
- **Reflection prompts** age-appropriate for Grade 1
- **Bilingual documentation** (French/English as appropriate)

### Safety Documentation Requirements
- **Safety rules poster** in both languages
- **Risk assessment** for each hands-on activity  
- **Emergency procedures** clearly posted
- **Material safety considerations** (allergies, choking hazards)
- **Supervision protocols** for outdoor activities

### Scientific Vocabulary Development
- **Word walls** with visual representations
- **Bilingual terminology** (French primary, English support)
- **Action vocabulary** (observe, measure, compare, classify)
- **Concept vocabulary** (living/non-living, energy, change, adaptation)
- **Scientific process words** (hypothesis, investigation, conclusion)

### Observation Skills Development
- **Daily observation practice** with guided questions
- **Drawing and recording** scientific observations
- **Comparison activities** (same/different)
- **Pattern recognition** exercises
- **Measurement introduction** (length, mass, time)

### Assessment Excellence Standards

#### Formative Assessment (Ongoing)
- **Science journal reviews** weekly
- **Investigation observation checklists** daily
- **Verbal questioning** during explorations
- **Peer discussion facilitation**
- **Misconception identification and addressing**

#### Summative Assessment (Unit-based)
- **Investigation planning and execution**
- **Scientific explanation attempts** (oral and written)
- **Vocabulary application** in new contexts
- **Practical skill demonstrations**
- **Portfolio compilation** of best work

### Cross-Curricular Integration Requirements

#### Mathematics Connections
- **Measurement activities** (length, mass, time)
- **Data representation** (simple graphs, charts)
- **Pattern recognition** and extension
- **Counting and number sense** through investigations
- **Geometry** through shape identification in nature

#### Language Arts Integration
- **Science vocabulary development**
- **Reading scientific texts** (appropriate level)
- **Writing scientific explanations**
- **Oral communication** of findings
- **Listening skills** during investigations

#### French Immersion Considerations
- **Primary instruction in French**
- **Scientific terminology** in French first
- **Cultural contexts** relevant to French-speaking communities
- **Bilingual resources** when beneficial
- **Indigenous science perspectives** in both languages

### STEM Integration Opportunities
- **Technology tools** for data collection (simple apps, digital thermometers)
- **Engineering challenges** (building bird feeders, simple machines)
- **Mathematical measurement** in all investigations
- **Design thinking process** for solving problems

### Indigenous Science Perspectives
- **Traditional ecological knowledge** about local plants and animals
- **Seasonal indicators** used by Indigenous peoples
- **Sustainable practices** from Indigenous cultures
- **Land-based learning** approaches
- **Elder and knowledge keeper** guest speakers when possible
