# Grade 1 French Immersion Pedagogical Framework
## Emily McIsaac - West Kent Elementary, PEI

### Core Principles (ETFO-Aligned)

#### 1. Three-Part Lesson Structure with Depth
**Minds On (10-15 minutes)**
- Activate prior knowledge through meaningful connections
- Include Indigenous land acknowledgments where appropriate  
- Use provocations that spark curiosity
- Incorporate student voice and choice
- French vocabulary pre-teaching through games/songs

**Action (30-40 minutes)**
- Gradual Release of Responsibility (I do, We do, You do)
- Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract progression for math
- Inquiry-based exploration for science
- Authentic communication opportunities in French
- Play-based learning centers
- Collaborative learning structures

**Consolidation (10-15 minutes)**
- Student-led sharing and reflection
- Co-created success criteria checking
- Exit tickets in various modalities
- Connection to next learning
- Celebration of growth and effort

#### 2. Assessment Triangulation

**Observations (Daily)**
- Anecdotal notes on French language use
- Learning skills development tracking
- Social-emotional growth documentation
- Video/photo documentation with permissions

**Conversations (Ongoing)**
- Conferencing in French and English
- Peer assessment with sentence starters
- Self-assessment using success criteria
- Learning goals discussions

**Products (Varied)**
- Multi-modal demonstrations of learning
- Portfolios showing growth over time
- Performance tasks
- Digital creations

#### 3. Differentiation Through UDL

**Multiple Means of Representation**
- Visual supports (anchor charts in French/English)
- Auditory (songs, rhymes, oral instructions)
- Kinesthetic (movement, manipulatives)
- Digital tools when appropriate

**Multiple Means of Engagement**
- Choice in topics within curriculum
- Varied groupings (individual, partners, small groups)
- Interest-based learning centers
- Culturally responsive materials

**Multiple Means of Action/Expression**
- Oral, written, dramatic, artistic options
- Technology integration where meaningful
- Accommodations for all learners
- Extensions for advanced learners

#### 4. French Immersion Specifics

**Language Distribution**
- 100% French: Math, Science, Social Studies, Arts, PE
- 80% French: French Language Arts
- 100% English: English Language Arts
- Total: 85% French instruction

**Language Learning Strategies**
- Total Physical Response (TPR)
- Scaffolded language structures
- Cognate awareness
- Visual vocabulary walls
- Daily French songs and poems
- Code-switching only when necessary for safety/understanding

#### 5. Indigenous Perspectives Integration

**Authentic Integration (Not Tokenism)**
- Mi'kmaq territory acknowledgment daily
- Seasonal teachings and moon cycles
- Seven Sacred Teachings throughout curriculum
- Elder visits and knowledge keeper connections
- Indigenous authors and stories
- Land-based learning opportunities

#### 6. Play-Based Learning for Grade 1

**Purposeful Play**
- Learning centers with clear objectives
- Dramatic play for language development
- Math games and manipulatives
- Science exploration stations
- Outdoor learning and nature play
- Movement breaks every 20 minutes

### Assessment Distribution
- **Assessment FOR Learning**: 60% (formative, ongoing)
- **Assessment AS Learning**: 30% (self/peer assessment)
- **Assessment OF Learning**: 10% (summative, reporting)

### Cross-Curricular Integration Priorities
1. Literacy across all subjects
2. Numeracy in science and daily routines
3. Social-emotional learning embedded
4. Environmental education through all seasons
5. Technology as a tool, not a subject

### Family and Community Engagement
- Weekly French phrases sent home
- Monthly bilingual newsletters
- Student-led conferences (November, March, June)
- Community helper visits
- Field trips connected to learning
- Family culture sharing opportunities

### Quality Indicators for Every Lesson
✓ Clear curriculum expectations addressed
✓ Success criteria co-created or reviewed
✓ French language development embedded
✓ Differentiation evident for all learners
✓ Indigenous perspective authentically included
✓ Assessment opportunity identified
✓ Play-based or hands-on component
✓ Student voice and choice present
✓ Real-world connections made
✓ Social-emotional learning integrated
