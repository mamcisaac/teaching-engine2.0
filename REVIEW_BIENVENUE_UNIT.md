## CRITICAL FAILURES IDENTIFIED

### 1. DURATION CRISIS ⏰
**CURRENT**: 60 minutes per lesson  
**REQUIRED**: 45 minutes per lesson  
**IMPACT**: MAJOR - Violates ETFO Grade 1 standards

All 16 lessons incorrectly specify 60-minute duration. Grade 1 French Immersion lessons MUST be 45 minutes to align with:
- Developmental attention spans
- ETFO time allocation guidelines
- Daily schedule requirements

### 2. THREE-PART STRUCTURE MISSING ⚠️
**CURRENT**: Basic mindsOn/action/consolidation content exists  
**REQUIRED**: Explicit timing (8/27/10 minutes)  
**IMPACT**: CRITICAL - No pedagogical timing structure

Lessons contain three-part content but lack:
- Minds On: 8 minutes (currently no timing specified)
- Action: 27 minutes (currently no timing specified)  
- Consolidation: 10 minutes (currently no timing specified)

### 3. ASSESSMENT INADEQUACY 📊
**CURRENT**: Basic "formative" designation with generic notes  
**REQUIRED**: Observable checkboxes with specific criteria  
**IMPACT**: MAJOR - Cannot track student progress

Current assessment notes:
```
"Ongoing observation, documentation of French language use, portfolio collection"
```

**Required format**:
```json

  "type": "formative",
```
