# Phase 3: Français langue première - Cross-Curricular Integration Guide

## 🌟 MISSION STATEMENT

**Français as the Foundation Language**: Transform every learning moment into French language acquisition by making Français the natural vehicle for ALL Grade 1 subject learning.

**Date:** August 12, 2025  
**Focus:** Grade 1 French Immersion - Comprehensive Cross-Curricular Integration  
**Philosophy:** Français isn't a separate subject—it's the language of learning.

---

## 🎯 INTEGRATION FRAMEWORK

### Core Integration Principles
1. **Authentic Context**: Français emerges naturally from subject content
2. **Vocabulary First**: Build subject-specific French vocabulary systematically
3. **Oral Foundation**: All integration begins with listening and speaking
4. **Gradual Release**: From teacher modeling to student independence
5. **Cultural Connection**: Integrate francophone perspectives naturally

### Français Curriculum Alignment
**15 Expectations Across 3 Strands:**
- **Communication orale (7)**: Foundation for all integration
- **Écriture et représentation (3)**: Writing emerges from subject content
- **Lecture et visionnement (5)**: Reading supports subject learning

## 📚 SUBJECT-BY-SUBJECT INTEGRATION

### 1. FRANÇAIS ↔ MATHÉMATIQUES (Daily Integration Required)

#### Core Mathematical Vocabulary in French
**Numbers & Counting (September)**
- Les nombres: un, deux, trois... vingt
- Compter: Je compte jusqu'à dix
- Plus/moins: "Trois plus deux égale cinq"
- Combien: "Combien y a-t-il?"

**Patterns & Shapes (October)**
- Les formes: cercle, carré, triangle, rectangle
- Les motifs: "Quel est le motif?"
- Répéter: "Le motif se répète"
- Suivant: "Qu'est-ce qui vient suivant?"

**Measurement Language (November-January)**
- Mesurer: long, court, grand, petit
- Comparer: plus long que, plus court que
- Les outils: règle, balance, horloge
- Le temps: matin, midi, soir, nuit

**Data & Graphing (February-April)**
- Les données: information, collection
- Le graphique: diagramme, pictogramme
- Préféré: "Mon animal préféré est..."
- Résultats: "Les résultats montrent que..."

#### Daily Integration Activities
**Morning Math Circle** (10 minutes daily)
- Count attendance in French: "Nous sommes vingt élèves aujourd'hui"
- Weather graphing: "Il fait soleil. Ajoutons une étoile au graphique"
- Calendar patterns: "Aujourd'hui c'est lundi. Hier c'était..."

**Math Story Problems** (15 minutes, 3x/week)
- "Marie a trois pommes. Jean lui donne deux pommes. Combien Marie a-t-elle maintenant?"
- Use manipulatives with French narration
- Students explain thinking: "Je pense que... parce que..."

**Measurement Explorations** (20 minutes weekly)
- "Mesurons notre table avec des cubes"
- "Comparons la hauteur des plantes"
- Record findings: "La plante mesure cinq cubes"

#### Assessment Connections
- **1CO.5**: Express mathematical thinking orally in French
- **1É.1**: Write number stories and math explanations
- **1L.2**: Read simple math problems and instructions

### 2. FRANÇAIS ↔ SCIENCES DE LA NATURE

#### Science Vocabulary Development
**Living Things (September-October)**
- Les êtres vivants: plante, animal, personne
- Grandir: "Les plantes grandissent avec de l'eau"
- Besoins: eau, nourriture, air, soleil
- Observer: "J'observe que..."

**Seasons & Weather (November-December)**
- Les saisons: automne, hiver, printemps, été
- Le temps: il pleut, il neige, il fait froid
- S'habiller: manteau, tuque, mitaines
- Changer: "Les feuilles changent de couleur"

**Materials & Objects (January-February)**
- Les matériaux: bois, métal, plastique, tissu
- Propriétés: dur, mou, lisse, rugueux
- Utiliser: "On utilise le bois pour..."
- Trier: "Trions les objets par matériau"

**Forces & Movement (March-April)**
- Le mouvement: pousser, tirer, rouler
- La force: fort, faible, rapide, lent
- Prédire: "Je prédis que la balle va..."
- Expérimenter: "Expérimentons avec..."

#### Daily Integration Activities
**Science Observation Journal** (10 minutes daily)
- Morning weather report in French
- Plant growth observations: "Notre haricot a grandi de..."
- Animal behavior notes: "Le poisson nage..."

**Investigation Language** (20 minutes, 2x/week)
- Hypothesis formation: "Si..., alors..."
- Procedure explanation: "D'abord, ensuite, finalement"
- Results sharing: "Nous avons découvert que..."

**Nature Vocabulary Walks** (30 minutes weekly)
- Outdoor observations with French vocabulary
- "Je vois un arbre avec des feuilles vertes"
- Collect vocabulary in nature journal

#### Assessment Connections
- **1CO.2**: Follow simple science instructions in French
- **1É.2**: Write observations and predictions
- **1L.3**: Make connections between science texts and personal experience

### 3. FRANÇAIS ↔ SCIENCES HUMAINES

#### Social Studies Vocabulary
**Identity & Family (September-October)**
- La famille: maman, papa, grand-mère, frère, sœur
- Les rôles: fils, fille, enfant, parent
- Se présenter: "Je m'appelle... J'ai... ans"
- Appartenir: "J'appartiens à ma famille"

**Community & Helpers (November-December)**
- La communauté: voisin, ami, école, magasin
- Les métiers: enseignant, médecin, pompier, policier
- Aider: "Le médecin aide les malades"
- Vivre ensemble: "Nous vivons dans la même communauté"

**Places & Geography (January-February)**
- Les lieux: maison, école, parc, bibliothèque
- Directions: ici, là, près, loin
- L'Île-du-Prince-Édouard: notre province
- Décrire: "Ma maison est près de l'école"

**Past & Present (March-April)**
- Autrefois: "Autrefois, les gens..."
- Maintenant: "Maintenant, nous..."
- Changer: "Les choses ont changé"
- Comparer: "Avant et maintenant"

#### Daily Integration Activities
**Community Circle** (15 minutes daily)
- Share family news: "Ma grand-mère visite ce weekend"
- Discuss community helpers: "J'ai vu un pompier hier"
- Describe weekend places: "Je suis allé au parc"

**Cultural Story Time** (20 minutes, 2x/week)
- Stories about francophone communities
- PEI French heritage tales
- Family tradition sharing

**Mapping Our World** (25 minutes weekly)
- Create classroom maps with French labels
- Describe routes: "Pour aller à la bibliothèque..."
- Compare places: "L'école est plus grande que ma maison"

#### Assessment Connections
- **1CO.3**: Make connections to cultural identity
- **1É.2**: Write about family and community experiences
- **1L.3**: Connect social studies texts to personal life

### 4. FRANÇAIS ↔ ARTS VISUELS

#### Arts Vocabulary
**Colors & Shapes (September-October)**
- Les couleurs: rouge, bleu, jaune, vert, orange, violet
- Mélanger: "Rouge et jaune font orange"
- Les formes: dans l'art aussi!
- Créer: "Je crée une œuvre d'art"

**Materials & Techniques (November-December)**
- Les outils: pinceau, crayon, ciseaux, colle
- Les matériaux: papier, carton, peinture, tissu
- Techniques: dessiner, peindre, découper, coller
- Texture: lisse, rugueux, doux, dur

**Expression & Emotion (January-February)**
- Les émotions: content, triste, fâché, surpris
- Exprimer: "Mon dessin exprime la joie"
- Inspiration: "Je suis inspiré par..."
- Interpréter: "Je pense que cette œuvre..."

**Artists & Culture (March-April)**
- L'artiste: "L'artiste a créé..."
- Admirer: "J'admire cette peinture"
- Francophone artists and traditions
- Critiquer: "J'aime... parce que..."

#### Daily Integration Activities
**Art Vocabulary Warm-up** (5 minutes daily)
- Name colors and materials in French
- Describe yesterday's art: "Hier, j'ai peint..."
- Preview today's activity: "Aujourd'hui, nous allons..."

**Creative Expression** (30 minutes, 2x/week)
- Describe artistic process: "D'abord, je dessine..."
- Share artistic choices: "J'ai choisi le rouge parce que..."
- Gallery walks with French commentary

**Art Appreciation** (20 minutes weekly)
- View francophone artists' works
- Describe what we see: "Je vois..."
- Express opinions: "Cette œuvre me fait penser à..."

#### Assessment Connections
- **1CO.5**: Describe artistic processes and choices
- **1É.2**: Write about artistic experiences and preferences
- **1L.4**: Identify techniques in visual texts and artwork

- Les chansons: francophone folk songs
- La mélodie: "La mélodie est belle"

## 📋 ASSESSMENT CONNECTIONS

### Cross-Curricular Assessment Strategy

#### **Authentic Assessment Through Integration**
Rather than separate subject assessments, evaluate Français growth through authentic subject contexts:

**Communication Orale Assessment:**
- Math problem-solving explanations (1CO.5)
- Science investigation discussions (1CO.2)
- Art process descriptions (1CO.5)
- Physical activity instructions following (1CO.2)

**Écriture Assessment:**
- Math journal entries (1É.1, 1É.2)
- Science observation logs (1É.1)
- Art reflection writing (1É.2)
- Personal goal setting (1É.2)

**Lecture Assessment:**
- Math story problems (1L.2)
- Science information texts (1L.2)
- Social studies community texts (1L.3)
- Health and safety instructions (1L.2)

#### **Portfolio Evidence Collection**
Create integrated portfolio sections:
- **Math-French**: Problem-solving videos, number stories
- **Science-French**: Investigation journals, hypothesis writing
- **Arts-French**: Process descriptions, cultural reflections
- **Health-French**: Personal goal documentation, emotion journals

## 🌟 CONCLUSION

**Français as the Natural Language of Learning**: By making French the vehicle for all subject learning, students develop authentic, functional bilingualism. This approach honors the French Immersion philosophy while ensuring rich, meaningful language acquisition through genuine academic content.

**The Goal**: Students who think, learn, and express themselves naturally in French across all subject areas—not students who translate from English to French, but students for whom French IS the language of learning.

**Success Measure**: When students no longer see "French class" but simply see learning happening in French, we will have achieved true cross-curricular integration.
