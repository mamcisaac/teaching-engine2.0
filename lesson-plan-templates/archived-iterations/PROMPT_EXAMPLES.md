# AI Prompt Examples

## Perfect Prompts That Prevent Hallucination

### Example 1: French Language Arts (September)

```
Generate a Grade 1 French Immersion Français (Immersion) lesson.

CONSTRAINTS (MUST FOLLOW):
- Unit: "Les salutations"
- Lesson 1 of 8
- Month 9: Use 30% French, 70% English
- Maximum 3 new French vocabulary words
- Total duration: 45 minutes (Minds On: 10 min, Action: 30 min, Consolidation: 5 min)

REQUIRED STRUCTURE:
1. Title (French with English translation)
2. Learning Objective: One clear "Students will be able to..." statement with 2-3 observable success criteria
3. Vocabulary: 3 French words with English, gesture, and visual cue for each
4. Minds On (10 min): Engaging hook activity
5. Action (30 min): Main learning activity with hands-on work
6. Consolidation (5 min): Quick wrap-up activity
7. Materials: Use ONLY these supplies: crayons, markers, pencils, erasers, paper, construction paper, glue sticks, scissors, unifix cubes, counting bears
8. Assessment: Choose ONE method (observation, exit-ticket, thumbs-check, or partner-share)
9. Differentiation: 2-3 support strategies, 1-2 extension ideas

FRENCH FOCUS:
- Emphasize oral communication
- Use songs, rhymes, or chants
- Include repetition and echo speaking
- Focus on everyday vocabulary
- Use puppets or storytelling

GRADE 1 REQUIREMENTS:
- Include movement in at least one activity
- Use concrete materials and manipulatives
- Keep instructions to single steps
- Use visual supports for all vocabulary
- Include partner work (not whole class sharing)
- No silent work over 5 minutes
- No abstract concepts without concrete examples
- No reading/writing beyond Grade 1 level
```

### Expected AI Response:

```
Title: Bonjour les amis! - Hello Friends!

Objective: Students will be able to greet others in French

Success Criteria:
- Says "Bonjour" when greeting someone
- Waves while saying greeting
- Responds to French greeting

Vocabulary:
1. Bonjour - Hello - Gesture: Wave hand - Visual: Smiling sun picture
2. Au revoir - Goodbye - Gesture: Wave goodbye - Visual: Waving hand picture
3. Ami(e) - Friend - Gesture: Hug self - Visual: Two stick figures together

Minds On Activity: Puppet Pierre arrives in mystery box. Only speaks French! Pierre says "Bonjour!" Students echo and wave. Practice with different voices (loud, whisper, happy).

Action Activity: Greeting train. Students form line, first student turns and says "Bonjour" with wave to next student. That student responds and turns to next. Movement break: Dance to "Bonjour" song. Partner practice with greeting cards.

Consolidation Activity: Exit circle. Each student says "Au revoir" to puppet Pierre and gets hand stamp.

Materials: puppet, mystery box, greeting cards, stamps, paper, crayons

Assessment: Observation - Look for: correct pronunciation attempt, using gesture with greeting, responding appropriately

Support Strategies: Allow wave without words initially, pair with confident speaker, use picture cards as prompts

Extension Ideas: Add "Comment ça va?", create greeting book with drawings
```

### Why This Prompt Works

✅ **What AI MUST provide:**
- Exactly 3 vocabulary words (month 9 limit)
- 30% French / 70% English balance
- Activities totaling 45 minutes
- Movement in greeting train
- Standard materials only
- Observable assessment criteria

❌ **What AI CANNOT add:**
- "After recess, students..." (no time context given)
- "The 22 students..." (no class size given)  
- "Building on yesterday..." (no previous lesson context)
- "Since it's Monday..." (no day given)
- "Students will be tired..." (no energy level assumption)

### Example 2: Mathematics (January)

```
Generate a Grade 1 French Immersion Mathématiques lesson.

CONSTRAINTS (MUST FOLLOW):
- Unit: "Les formes géométriques"
- Lesson 5 of 10
- Month 1: Use 65% French, 35% English
- Maximum 4 new French vocabulary words
- Total duration: 45 minutes (Minds On: 10 min, Action: 30 min, Consolidation: 5 min)

[Rest of structure same as above]

MATH FOCUS:
- Use counting manipulatives (bears, cubes, blocks)
- Focus on numbers 1-20 for lessons 1-10, expand after
- Include patterns and sorting
- Use math songs in French
- Connect to real classroom objects
```

### Key Features Preventing Hallucination

1. **No Temporal Context** - AI cannot guess about time of day, day of week, or seasonal events
2. **No Class Context** - AI cannot assume class size, student names, or classroom layout
3. **No Previous Lesson Dependency** - Each lesson stands alone
4. **Fixed Material List** - AI must choose from provided standard supplies
5. **Strict Structure** - AI must fill specific sections, no room for tangential content
6. **Observable Criteria Only** - Assessment must be concrete and visible

### Result

The AI generates clean, practical lessons without any contextual hallucinations. Emily adds the real classroom context when she implements the lesson.
