## Unit Overview

### Big Ideas / Grandes idées
- **English:** Patterns help us predict and organize our world. Shapes have unique properties that help us describe and sort them. Mathematical patterns exist everywhere in nature and human-made objects.
- **Français:** Les régularités nous aident à prédire et organiser notre monde. Les formes ont des propriétés uniques qui nous aident à les décrire et trier. Les motifs mathématiques existent partout dans la nature et objets créés par l'humain.

### Essential Questions / Questions essentielles
1. Quelle est la règle de cette régularité et comment le sais-tu?
2. Qu'est-ce qui vient ensuite dans ce motif et pourquoi?
3. Comment peux-tu trier ces formes et expliquer ta règle?
4. Où vois-tu des régularités dans notre environnement?
5. Comment peux-tu créer ton propre motif?

### Curriculum Expectations
- **1.RR1:** Identify and extend patterns
- **1.RR2:** Create patterns using various materials
- **1.FE2:** Sort and classify 3D and 2D shapes

## Week 1: Pattern Recognition (November 4-8, 2025)

### Learning Goals
Students will identify and describe patterns using AB and ABC sequences.

### Daily Lessons

#### Day 1: Introduction aux motifs (AB Patterns)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Coloured blocks, pattern cards, autumn leaves
**Vocabulary Focus:** motif, répéter, règle

**Activities:**
1. **Circle Time Pattern Discovery (15 min)**
   - Show AB pattern with body movements: debout-assis-debout-assis
   - Students identify the pattern rule: "Qu'est-ce qui se répète?"
   - Practice AB patterns with clapping: fort-doux-fort-doux

2. **Concrete AB Pattern Exploration (25 min)**
   - Use red and yellow blocks to create AB patterns
   - Students work in pairs to continue given patterns
   - Teacher circulates asking: "Quelle est la règle de ton motif?"

3. **Autumn Pattern Hunt (15 min)**
   - Create AB patterns using real autumn leaves
   - Document patterns with photos for classroom display
   - Discuss seasonal patterns: "Où voyez-vous des motifs en automne?"

4. **Reflection Circle (5 min)**
   - Students share one pattern they discovered
   - Introduce tomorrow's preview: "Demain, nous explorerons des motifs ABC!"

**Assessment:** Observation checklist for pattern identification and French vocabulary use

#### Day 2: Motifs ABC (ABC Patterns)
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Three colours of manipulatives, rhythm instruments, pattern boards
**Vocabulary Focus:** séquence, continuer, prédire

**Activities:**
1. **Warm-up with AB Review (10 min)**
   - Quick AB pattern review with movement
   - Transition: "Aujourd'hui, nous ajoutons un troisième élément!"

2. **ABC Pattern Introduction (20 min)**
   - Demonstrate with rouge-bleu-jaune blocks
   - Students echo the pattern verbally: "rouge-bleu-jaune-rouge-bleu-jaune"
   - Build understanding: "Il y a trois éléments qui se répètent"

3. **Musical ABC Patterns (20 min)**
   - Create sound patterns: tambourin-maracas-bâton
   - Students take turns being pattern leaders
   - Record patterns on chart paper with symbols

4. **Independent Pattern Work (10 min)**
   - Students create their own ABC patterns at stations
   - Partners describe each other's patterns in French
   - Challenge: "Peux-tu faire un motif avec quatre éléments?"

**Assessment:** Pattern creation rubric focusing on accuracy and French explanation

#### Day 3: Pattern Translation
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Pattern blocks, crayons, movement cards, recording sheets
**Vocabulary Focus:** traduire, même motif, différent matériel

**Activities:**
1. **Pattern Translation Introduction (15 min)**
   - Show AB pattern with blocks, then translate to colours on paper
   - Discuss: "C'est le même motif mais avec des matériaux différents"

2. **Multi-Modal Pattern Stations (30 min)**
   **Station 1:** Colour to Movement (rouge-bleu = saut-frappe)
   **Station 2:** Sound to Shape (tam-tam-clap = cercle-carré-triangle)
   **Station 3:** Size to Action (grand-petit = bras en haut-bras en bas)

3. **Pattern Gallery Walk (10 min)**
   - Students post their translated patterns
   - Gallery walk with French descriptions
   - Voting for "motif le plus créatif"

4. **Exit Ticket (5 min)**
   - Draw one AB pattern and translate it to a different material
   - Write: "Mon motif dit..."

**Assessment:** Portfolio evidence of pattern translation abilities

#### Day 4: Thanksgiving Pattern Connections
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Turkey feather cutouts, harvest vegetables (real or plastic), Thanksgiving-themed pattern cards
**Vocabulary Focus:** Action de grâces, plumes, récolte

**Activities:**
1. **Thanksgiving Pattern Discussion (15 min)**
   - Explore turkey feather patterns in nature
   - "Les dindes ont des motifs naturels dans leurs plumes"
   - Show images of actual turkey feather patterns

2. **Turkey Feather Pattern Creation (25 min)**
   - Students create AB and ABC patterns using feather cutouts
   - Colours: brown-orange-red (automne colours)
   - Mount on turkey body templates

3. **Harvest Arrangement Patterns (15 min)**
   - Use mini pumpkins, corn, apples to create patterns
   - "Comment pouvons-nous arranger nos légumes de récolte?"
   - Practice vocabulary: citrouille-maïs-pomme

4. **Cultural Pattern Sharing (5 min)**
   - Discuss: "Quels motifs voyez-vous dans vos traditions familiales?"
   - Preview: "Demain, nous créerons nos propres motifs!"

**Assessment:** Thanksgiving pattern portfolios with French explanations

#### Day 5: Pattern Prediction and Extension
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Various manipulatives, pattern strips, prediction cards
**Vocabulary Focus:** prédire, qu'est-ce qui vient ensuite, je pense que

**Activities:**
1. **Prediction Challenge Introduction (10 min)**
   - Show incomplete patterns, cover the next element
   - "Qu'est-ce qui vient ensuite dans ce motif?"
   - Model thinking aloud: "Je pense que... parce que..."

2. **Pattern Prediction Stations (35 min)**
   **Station 1:** Visual patterns with shapes
   **Station 2:** Movement patterns to complete
   **Station 3:** Sound patterns for extension
   **Station 4:** Growing patterns (advanced challenge)

3. **Peer Teaching Circle (15 min)**
   - Students teach their pattern to a partner
   - Partner must predict the next three elements
   - Rotate roles: teacher becomes student

**Weekly Assessment:** Pattern portfolio review and French oral assessment

## Week 2: Advanced Patterns and Shape Introduction (November 11-15, 2025)

### Learning Goals
Students will create original patterns and begin exploring 2D shapes.

### Daily Lessons

#### Day 6: Creating Original Patterns
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Choice of manipulatives, pattern recording sheets, creativity materials
**Vocabulary Focus:** créer, original, mon motif, j'ai choisi

**Activities:**
1. **Pattern Designer Introduction (10 min)**
   - "Aujourd'hui, vous êtes des designers de motifs!"
   - Show famous patterns in art and architecture
   - Establish success criteria for original patterns

2. **Free Choice Pattern Creation (30 min)**
   - Students choose materials and create unique patterns
   - Must explain their pattern rule to teacher
   - Document with drawings and photos

3. **Pattern Museum Setup (15 min)**
   - Create classroom "Musée des motifs"
   - Students write pattern descriptions in French
   - Prepare for Monday's gallery walk

4. **Reflection and Goal Setting (5 min)**
   - "Quel type de motif veux-tu essayer demain?"
   - Preview shape exploration

**Assessment:** Original pattern creation with French documentation

#### Day 7: Introduction to 2D Shapes
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** 2D shape sets, shape sorting mats, magnifying glasses
**Vocabulary Focus:** forme, côté, sommet, angle

**Activities:**
1. **Shape Hunt Warm-up (15 min)**
   - "Cherchons des formes dans notre classe!"
   - Students find rectangles, circles, triangles, squares
   - Create list on anchor chart with French names

2. **Shape Attribute Exploration (25 min)**
   - Use magnifying glasses to examine shapes closely
   - Count sides (côtés) and corners (sommets)
   - Sort shapes by number of sides
   - "Le triangle a trois côtés et trois sommets"

3. **Shape Sorting Challenge (15 min)**
   - Multiple sorting criteria: size, colour, number of sides
   - Students explain their sorting rule: "J'ai trié par..."
   - Challenge: Resort using different criteria

4. **Shape Journal Setup (5 min)**
   - Begin individual shape journals
   - Record discoveries about each shape

**Assessment:** Shape attribute identification and sorting explanation

#### Day 8: Shape Pattern Combinations
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** 2D shapes, pattern mats, shape stamps
**Vocabulary Focus:** combiner, formes et motifs, répéter des formes

**Activities:**
1. **Shape Pattern Introduction (15 min)**
   - Demonstrate AB pattern using circle-square-circle-square
   - "Nous pouvons faire des motifs avec des formes!"
   - Show ABC pattern: triangle-rectangle-circle

2. **Shape Pattern Stations (30 min)**
   **Station 1:** Physical shape patterns on floor mats
   **Station 2:** Shape stamp patterns on paper
   **Station 3:** Shape cutout patterns for bulletin board
   **Station 4:** Digital shape patterns (if technology available)

3. **Pattern and Shape Integration (10 min)**
   - Students create patterns that include both shapes AND colours
   - "Peux-tu faire un motif avec des formes bleues et rouges?"
   - Advanced: size variations within shape patterns

4. **Exit Ticket Creation (5 min)**
   - Quick sketch of favourite shape pattern
   - Circle the "motif le plus difficile" completed today

**Assessment:** Combination pattern portfolio documentation

#### Day 9: 3D Shape Introduction
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** 3D shape collection, real-world objects, shape sorting bins
**Vocabulary Focus:** solide, cube, sphère, cylindre, face, arête

**Activities:**
1. **3D vs 2D Discovery (20 min)**
   - Compare flat shapes to solid shapes
   - "Une forme plate vs une forme solide"
   - Hands-on exploration: can you pick it up? Does it roll?

2. **Real World 3D Shape Hunt (25 min)**
   - Find classroom objects that match 3D shapes
   - Boîte = cube, balle = sphère, crayon = cylindre
   - Create "Shape Museum" with real objects and labels

3. **3D Shape Patterns (10 min)**
   - Create patterns using 3D shapes
   - Challenge: "Les formes solides peuvent-elles faire des motifs?"
   - Document successful 3D patterns

4. **Shape Journal Update (5 min)**
   - Add 3D discoveries to shape journals
   - Compare 2D and 3D versions of same shape family

**Assessment:** 3D shape identification and real-world connections

#### Day 10: Environmental Pattern Investigation
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Cameras/tablets, outdoor exploration sheets, clipboards
**Vocabulary Focus:** environnement, nature, partout, découvrir

**Activities:**
1. **Outdoor Pattern Preparation (10 min)**
   - Discuss outdoor safety and expectations
   - "Nous allons découvrir des motifs dans la nature"
   - Review pattern vocabulary for outdoor use

2. **Nature Pattern Expedition (35 min)**
   - Outdoor exploration in school yard/nearby area
   - Document patterns in: leaves, bark, shadows, buildings
   - Work in partners with recording sheets
   - Take photos of discovered patterns

3. **Pattern Documentation (10 min)**
   - Return to classroom and sort discoveries
   - Create categories: "motifs naturels" vs "motifs créés par l'humain"
   - Begin planning presentation

4. **Weekly Reflection (5 min)**
   - "Quel a été votre découverte la plus surprenante?"
   - Preview next week's shape focus

**Weekly Assessment:** Environmental pattern documentation and vocabulary use

## Week 3: Shape Properties and Spatial Reasoning (November 18-22, 2025)

### Learning Goals
Students will identify shape properties and develop spatial vocabulary.

### Daily Lessons

#### Day 11: Shape Properties Deep Dive
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Shape attribute blocks, property sorting mats, Venn diagrams
**Vocabulary Focus:** propriétés, caractéristiques, même, différent

**Activities:**
1. **Shape Detective Introduction (15 min)**
   - "Nous sommes des détectives de formes!"
   - Introduce property investigation tools
   - Model how to describe shape properties systematically

2. **Property Investigation Stations (30 min)**
   **Station 1:** Side counting and recording
   **Station 2:** Corner identification and marking
   **Station 3:** Size comparison and ordering
   **Station 4:** Colour and pattern attributes

3. **Venn Diagram Sorting (10 min)**
   - Sort shapes using two criteria
   - "Formes with 3 sides AND red colour"
   - Students explain overlapping categories

4. **Property Journal Recording (5 min)**
   - Document property discoveries
   - Set goals for tomorrow's sorting challenges

**Assessment:** Property identification accuracy and French explanations

#### Day 12: Advanced Shape Sorting
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Large variety of 2D and 3D shapes, sorting hoops, labels
**Vocabulary Focus:** trier, classer, critère, règle de tri

**Activities:**
1. **Sorting Challenge Setup (10 min)**
   - Review multiple sorting criteria
   - "Aujourd'hui, nous trions avec des règles complexes"
   - Demonstrate multi-level sorting

2. **Complex Sorting Stations (35 min)**
   **Station 1:** Sort by two properties (size AND colour)
   **Station 2:** Create sorting challenges for others
   **Station 3:** Sort 3D shapes by function (rolls, stacks, slides)
   **Station 4:** Shape pattern sorting (which shapes work in patterns?)

3. **Sorting Challenge Exchange (10 min)**
   - Teams attempt other teams' sorting challenges
   - Guess the sorting rule: "Je pense que tu as trié par..."
   - Celebrate successful rule identification

4. **Sorting Strategy Reflection (5 min)**
   - "Quelle stratégie de tri était la plus difficile?"
   - Preview: spatial positioning activities

**Assessment:** Complex sorting accuracy and strategy explanation

#### Day 13: Spatial Relationship Vocabulary
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Position cards, classroom objects, spatial relation mats
**Vocabulary Focus:** à côté de, derrière, devant, au-dessus, en dessous

**Activities:**
1. **Spatial Simon Says (15 min)**
   - "Simon dit: mets ton crayon à côté de ton livre"
   - Practice spatial vocabulary with concrete actions
   - Students take turns being "Simon"

2. **Classroom Mapping Activity (25 min)**
   - Create simple classroom map showing spatial relationships
   - "La bibliothèque est à côté de la zone de lecture"
   - Students practice describing locations using spatial terms

3. **Position Challenge Games (15 min)**
   - Partner games: describe hidden object locations
   - "Mon objet est derrière quelque chose de bleu"
   - Rotate partners for practice variety

4. **Exit Positioning (5 min)**
   - Students line up using spatial directions
   - "Marie, mets-toi derrière Jean et à côté de Sophie"

**Assessment:** Spatial vocabulary use in context and accuracy

#### Day 14: Construction Challenges
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Building blocks, construction cards, geometric building pieces
**Vocabulary Focus:** construire, bâtir, structure, solide

**Activities:**
1. **Engineering Introduction (10 min)**
   - "Nous sommes des ingénieurs mathématiques!"
   - Show examples of geometric structures in architecture
   - Introduce construction challenges

2. **Geometric Construction Stations (35 min)**
   **Station 1:** Build specific 2D shape outlines with blocks
   **Station 2:** Create 3D structures using geometric shapes
   **Station 3:** Pattern construction (build repeating structures)
   **Station 4:** Free construction with shape documentation

3. **Construction Gallery Walk (10 min)**
   - Display and describe constructions
   - "J'ai utilisé des triangles et des carrés pour construire..."
   - Peer appreciation and suggestions

4. **Construction Journal (5 min)**
   - Sketch and describe favourite construction
   - Plan tomorrow's construction improvements

**Assessment:** Construction documentation and French mathematical vocabulary

#### Day 15: Direction Following and Creating
**Duration:** 60 minutes

**Materials:** Direction cards, classroom map, treasure hunt materials
**Vocabulary Focus:** directions, suivre, créer des instructions, étapes

**Activities:**
1. **Direction Following Practice (20 min)**
   - Follow simple classroom directions using spatial vocabulary
   - "Commence au bureau, va derrière la bibliothèque"
   - Practice as whole class, then individual attempts

2. **Mathematical Treasure Hunt (25 min)**
   - Follow geometric clues around classroom
   - "Trouve trois triangles, puis va à côté du centre de maths"
   - Incorporate pattern and shape recognition

3. **Direction Creation (10 min)**
   - Students create direction sequences for classmates
   - Focus on clear, sequential instructions
   - Test directions with partners

4. **Weekly Celebration (5 min)**
   - Celebrate spatial vocabulary growth
   - Preview final week's culminating activities

**Weekly Assessment:** Direction following accuracy and creation clarity

## Assessment and Evaluation

### Success Criteria / Critères de réussite
- ✅ Je peux identifier la règle d'une régularité et la continuer
- ✅ Je peux créer mes propres motifs avec des règles claires
- ✅ Je peux traduire des régularités entre différents modes (son, mouvement, couleur)
- ✅ Je peux trier des formes et expliquer mes critères en français
- ✅ Je peux trouver des régularités dans notre environnement
- ✅ Je peux enseigner une régularité à un ami

### Assessment Strategies
**Week 1:** Pattern recognition and creation portfolios
**Week 2:** Advanced pattern documentation and shape identification
**Week 3:** Shape property investigations and spatial vocabulary use
**Week 4:** Teaching demonstrations and community fair participation

### Differentiation Strategies

#### Emerging Learners
- **Concrete Stage Extended:** AB patterns with large manipulatives, basic 2D shapes, guided sorting with clear criteria, visual pattern cards, partner support for French vocabulary

#### Developing Learners
- **Balanced Multimodal:** ABC and ABAB patterns, 3D and 2D shape properties, independent sorting with explanation, pattern translation between modes, developing French pattern discourse

#### Extending Learners
- **Complex Pattern Creation:** Growing patterns, ABCD+ patterns, advanced shape properties, creating sorting challenges, sophisticated French mathematical explanations, teaching younger students
